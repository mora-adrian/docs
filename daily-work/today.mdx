## Today

**Today** is the screen to open first and keep open. It pulls together everything that needs a human: repair orders you're responsible for, tasks due today or overdue, items blocked on parts or approvals, and upcoming appointments. If you only check one screen, check this one — it's organized so the next useful action is obvious.

Use it to:

- See your assigned repair orders and their current status.
- Spot past-due targets before they become late deliveries.
- Jump straight into a repair order, customer, or task.

## Tasks

Tasks are lightweight to-dos that can stand alone or attach to a repair order, project, or record.

- **Create** a task from the Tasks screen, from inside a repair order's **Tasks** tab, or from the command palette.
- **Assign** it to yourself or a teammate and give it a due date.
- **Complete** it from the task list or the repair order — completion is recorded in activity history.

<Tip>
Tasks are for commitments, not reminders-in-your-head. "Call customer when fender arrives" belongs on the repair order so anyone covering for you sees it.
</Tip>

## Notifications

The **Notifications** screen collects what the system thinks you should know: assignments, mentions, inbound customer replies, status changes you asked to follow, and integration alerts.

- Configure what you receive in **Settings → Preferences**.
- Clicking a notification opens the exact record it refers to.
- Notifications respect your shop and role — you'll never see one for a shop you don't belong to.

## A good daily rhythm

1. **Morning:** Today → clear overdue tasks → scan Repair board for blockers.
2. **Midday:** Messages inbox → triage new inbound items.
3. **Afternoon:** Check target dates on active repairs; update statuses before you leave so tomorrow's board is accurate.
