## Today

**Today** is the screen to open first and keep open. It gathers everything that needs a person: the repair orders you are responsible for, tasks due today or overdue, items blocked on parts or approvals, and upcoming appointments. If you check only one screen, check this one — it is organized so the next useful action is obvious.

Use Today to:

- See your assigned repair orders and their current status.
- Spot past-due targets before they become late deliveries.
- Open a repair order, customer, or task directly.

## Tasks

A task is a lightweight to-do. It can stand alone or attach to a repair order, a project, or a record.

To work with tasks:

1. **Create** a task from the Tasks screen, from a repair order's **Tasks** tab, or from the command palette.
2. **Assign** it to yourself or a teammate and set a due date.
3. **Complete** it from the task list or the repair order. Completion is recorded in the activity history.

<Tip>
Use tasks for commitments, not memory. "Call customer when the fender arrives" belongs on the repair order so a teammate covering for you can see it.
</Tip>

## Notifications

The **Notifications** screen collects what you should know: assignments, mentions, inbound customer replies, status changes you asked to follow, and integration alerts.

- Choose what you receive in **Settings → Preferences**.
- Select a notification to open the exact record it refers to.
- Notifications respect your shop and your role. You never see one for a shop you do not belong to.

## A daily rhythm that works

1. **Morning:** Open Today. Clear overdue tasks. Scan the Repair board for blockers.
2. **Midday:** Work the Messages inbox. Triage new inbound items.
3. **End of day:** Check target dates on active repairs and update statuses, so tomorrow's board starts accurate.
