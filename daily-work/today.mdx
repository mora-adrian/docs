## Today

**Today** is the screen to open first and keep open. It answers one question: *what needs a person right now?* Items are grouped into four sections, and each row links straight to the repair order.

| Section | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Close to delivery** | Jobs approaching their target or delivery date — the ones to push over the line. |
| **Past the money gate** | Jobs that progressed past a financial checkpoint (authorization, approval) and need the next step taken. |
| **Supplements waiting** | Jobs with a supplement submitted or pending — waiting on insurer action or internal follow-up. |
| **Missing documents** | Jobs lacking required paperwork — signatures, authorizations, or claim documents. |

Each row shows why the item surfaced and your role on it — **Advocate**, **Planner**, or **Planner & advocate** — so you know why it is on your list.

## Tasks

A task is a lightweight to-do. It can stand alone or attach to a repair order, a project, or a record.

### The Tasks screen

- **Scope tabs** — **Mine** (assigned to you), **Created by you**, and **All** (visible to workspace managers).
- **Status filter** — open, completed, or overdue.
- **Due filter** — Overdue, Due today, Due this week, No due date, or Any.
- **Link filter** — General tasks or Repair-order tasks.

### Work a task

1. **Create** one from the Tasks screen, a repair order's Tasks tab, or the command palette. Set an assignee and a due date.
2. **Update** status from the task sheet, which also keeps history and lets you reassign or re-link the task.
3. **Complete** it in place — completion is recorded in the activity history.

<Tip>
Use tasks for commitments, not memory. "Call customer when the fender arrives" belongs on the repair order so a teammate covering for you can see it.
</Tip>

## Notifications

The **Notifications** screen lists what Kinetic thinks you should know: assignments, mentions, inbound customer replies, status changes, and integration alerts. Each notification names its channel and opens the exact record it refers to when selected.

- Choose what you receive in **Settings → Preferences**.
- Unread notifications drive the badge in the sidebar.
- Notifications respect your shop and role — you never see another shop's activity.

## A daily rhythm that works

1. **Morning:** Today → clear overdue tasks → scan the Repairs board for blockers.
2. **Midday:** Messages inbox → triage new inbound items.
3. **End of day:** check target dates on active repairs and update statuses, so tomorrow's board starts accurate.
