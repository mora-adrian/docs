title: "Email review"
description: "The review queue for inbound claim email — who sees it, and what to do with each item."
---

**Email review** is the triage queue for mail that lands in the shop's monitored mailbox. Kinetic watches the connected mailbox for claim-related email and queues items that need a human decision. The nav item appears only for members with a connected mailbox, and its badge shows the pending count.

## What lands here

- Inbound email that could not be matched confidently to a customer or repair order.
- Messages that matched with low confidence and need confirmation.
- Claim-related mail your mailbox monitor flagged for review.

## Work the queue

For each item:

1. **Read the excerpt** — sender, subject, and a preview of the body.
2. **Match it** — assign the message to the right person, company, or repair order. Once matched, the message and its history file onto that record.
3. **Dismiss what is not claim work** so the queue stays clean.

<Tip>
Keep the queue near zero. Unreviewed mail means unanswered adjusters and unfilled records — the monitor only helps if someone works the queue daily.
</Tip>

## Connect your mailbox

Mailbox connections are managed from **Settings → Preferences**. Connect once; Kinetic reads the mailbox continuously from then on. If the connection breaks, the badge and Settings show it — reconnect there.
