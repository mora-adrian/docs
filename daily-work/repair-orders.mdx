A repair order is the full job — customer, vehicle, claim, estimate, parts, communications, schedule, and money — under one stable record. It keeps the same link for its whole life, no matter how its status changes.

## Opening a repair order

- Click any board card or table row to open the **drawer** — a read-only preview panel.
- Click **Open full screen** for the complete record at `/repair-orders/<id>`.

## The drawer (peek)

The drawer is for a quick look and a quick note:

- **Identity** — customer name and nickname, phone, email, vehicle, VIN, and a pinned preview photo.
- **Status** — current status and days in it, on a rail showing progress through your phases.
- **Facts** — **All details** opens a sheet with sections for the repair order, assignments, estimate, insurance, and customer: phase, outcome (Won/Lost), outcome likelihood, repairability, target date, repair order and claim numbers, loss type, the four assignees (repair planner, client advocate, estimator, technician), totals, customer pay, deductible, appointment, in/out dates, insurer, adjuster, company, referral, and contact fields.
- **Parties** — Referrer, Driver, Attorney, Insurer, Adjuster, Appraiser, Other. **Change {role}** lets you pick a person or company, or clear it.
- **Editable fields** — fields CCC ONE owns show a CCC tag and can't be edited. Kinetic-owned fields show a pencil: **Target**, **Outcome likelihood** (with a **Calculated** suggestion you can apply), **Repairability**, and **Total loss**.
- **Recent activity** preview with **See all activity**, and a note composer.

## The full record

The full record's tabs:

### Activity

The order's history: notes, calls, emails, texts, and events. **Filter chips** narrow to one kind (Calls, Emails, Events, Notes, Texts) or **All activity**. A **Repair progress** card summarizes stage, created date, and target. The note composer at the top supports dictation and autosaves drafts; **Load older activity** pages back in time.

### Emails

Persisted email history. Each card shows an **Inbound**/**Outbound**/**Draft** badge, subject, parties, attachments with **Download**, and quoted history. **Reply** appears on the newest inbound email. **Compose** starts a new email with To, Cc, Subject, attachments from files, dictation, and ⌘+Enter send. Supplement emails carry the **Auto follow-up** switch — see [Messages](/daily-work/messages).

### Texts

The SMS/iMessage thread with the full composer — templates (`@`), Improve with AI, talk-to-text, voice memos, attachments, reactions, message info — plus the sentiment and line-health strip. All documented in [Messages](/daily-work/messages).

### Calls

Call history: each entry shows incoming/outgoing, duration, and recording state; **Play recording** where a recording exists; **Details** expands disposition, summary, and transcript. Incoming calls also trigger a screen pop (see below).

### Tasks

Repair-order tasks with the shared task sheet — title, due date, assignee, links, notes. See [Tasks & notifications](/daily-work/tasks).

### Estimate

The estimate line table with:

- **Filter chips** — Labor (Mechanical, Body, Structural, Paint), Parts, and Operations (R&I, Repair, Replace), multi-select. **Simple view** / **Complete view** toggle — Simple hides manual-entry lines. "Showing X of Y lines" with **Clear filters**.
- **Totals** — Labor and "Parts + materials + other" roll up to Total, with tooltips explaining how CCC ONE's subtotal splits.
- **Explain estimate** — generates a plain-language, customer-facing walkthrough: overview, per-section totals, the bigger items, repair-or-replace notes, and paint blending. From the explanation sheet you can **Regenerate** or **Email to customer** (confirms recipient and subject, then sends it as an email on the record). If the estimate changed since the explanation was written, Kinetic warns before you send.
- **Audit with Rivet** — queues an estimate review; see [Rivet](/ai-review/rivet).

### Parts

Every part line with filter and sort controls:

- **Columns** — Line, Part number, Part, Quantity, Status, Paint, Unit price, Total.
- **Filter parts** — search the list.
- **Sorting** — sort columns ascending, descending, or off.
- **Rollups** — **Parts received**, numbered parts total, and **Outstanding** totals.

### ARW — Accounts Receivable Worksheet

The ARW tracks who owes what and where each payment stands. Each row has a **Payer** (Customer or Insurer), a **Payment for** purpose, and three amount fields:

- **Prepaid** — money already received.
- **In transit** — a payment sent or promised but not yet landed.
- **Due** — what's still owed. For CCC-controlled purposes, Due is calculated from the CCC ONE amount less prepaid and in transit, and the field locks.

Purposes: Deductible, Appearance allowance, Betterment, Bottom line discount, Unrelated prior damage, Related prior damage, Estimate, Supplement 1–9, Other. CCC-controlled purposes may appear on one row only.

**Payment proof** — each In-transit cell can carry an attachment: **Upload/Replace from device** or **Choose from Files**, then **View** or **Remove proof**. There is no separate "confirm payment" button — when money arrives, move the amount from **In transit** to **Prepaid** and save.

The footer reconciles: **Totals**, **CCC ONE**, **Due at pickup**, and a **Difference** showing **Balanced**, **Over by**, or **Short by**. **Add row**, **Discard**, and **Save worksheet** manage edits; if someone else saved meanwhile, Kinetic asks you to reload rather than overwrite.

### Files

Every document and photo on the order:

- **Upload** — **Add files** or drag and drop; up to 75 per batch. Each file shows Queued, Uploading, Waiting to retry, Uploaded, or Failed.
- **Folders** — Pinned, Documents, phase-grouped folders with a **Current** badge, and Unsorted. Tags include Initial Estimate, Pre-repair Scan, Supplement, Work Authorization, Total Loss Release, Appraisal Clause, Facts of Loss.
- **Toolbar** — **Export PDF**, **Send to adjuster** (packages selections into a supplement email), **Send to CCC ONE**, **Delete**, grid/list toggle, collapse-all (**⇧O**).
- **Row actions** — rename, pin, move to a phase, tag, delete, set as the drawer preview image (or restore the automatic one).
- **Preview** — click a file for the viewer with previous/next, **Download**, description editor, and phase select.

### Signatures

Send contract templates for e-signature:

1. Choose a **Template** — the built-in **Facts of Loss** questionnaire is here too.
2. **Preview** opens the document.
3. **Send via text** sends the signature request; the customer signs on a hosted page.

Each request lists recipient, template, and status — sent, viewed, signed, rejected, or expired — with "Viewed {date}" / "Signed {date}" / "Awaiting signature" lines. Re-clicking send re-delivers the same request rather than making a duplicate.

**Facts of Loss** — instead of a signature, it generates a questionnaire link: **Copy link** or **Send link via text**. The customer answers "About you," "The accident," "Who was in the vehicle," and "Since the accident," and the answers file onto the repair order.

### Calendar

The order's planning view — Plan and Repairs bars, draggable **Target / out** date, Agenda, and Milestones. See [Calendar](/daily-work/calendar#the-repair-order-calendar-tab).

## Screen pops

When the shop phone rings, a screen pop appears top-right: **Incoming call** with the caller's name (or number if unknown), matched contacts, and buttons for their active repair orders — click one to jump straight to the record. For unknown callers, **Create contact** opens a prefilled new-contact form. Pops dismiss on their own shortly after the call is answered.

## Creating a repair order

- **+ New** / **+ Add** on a board column creates a card in that status; fill in customer and vehicle to establish identity.
- CCC ONE events can also create and update orders — CCC-controlled fields and statuses overwrite what a person entered, because CCC ONE is authoritative for what it supplies.
