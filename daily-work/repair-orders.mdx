A **repair order** is one repair job on one vehicle. Kinetic assigns it a permanent ID at creation and keeps it forever — the same record follows the vehicle from first contact through delivery, payment, and follow-up. Its address, `/repair-orders/<id>`, is stable and shareable inside your shop.

## The header and sidebar

The header always shows the repair order number, the status pill, and a *past target* indicator when the job is behind. The sidebar holds the record's facts:

- Customer and vehicle card (name, phone, email; year/make/model/trim, VIN, color, odometer)
- Insurer and claim number
- Status, days in status, and the pipeline rail
- Target and out dates with a confidence badge
- Expected insurance payout (from the vehicle's market value — **Refresh value** pulls a current figure)
- Outcome likelihood with repairability — editable, with **Use calculated** to release a manual value back to the computed one
- Assigned seats: advocate, planner, technician
- Parties on the job

Fields supplied by **CCC ONE** carry a CCC mark and cannot be edited — change them in CCC and the update flows back automatically. Fields Kinetic owns show a pencil that opens a single-field edit dialog.

## The tabs

Eleven tabs organize everything about the job. Empty tabs show honest empty states.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Activity** | The complete audit trail — every status change, edit, message, and event, with who did it and when. Staff notes live here too. |
| **Emails** | The email thread for this job, with a full composer (To/CC, subject, body, attachments, drafts). |
| **Texts** | The text-message thread, iMessage-style: sent bubbles on the right, received on the left. The composer supports attachments, emoji tapbacks, AI improve, and dictation (talk to text). |
| **Calls** | Call history for this job — direction, duration, status, recordings, and transcriptions where enabled. |
| **Tasks** | Work items attached to this repair order, with assignees, due dates, and status. |
| **Estimate** | The estimate lines for the job. Filter by category — Body, Structural, Mechanical, Paint, Labor, Parts, R&I (remove and install), Repair, Replace, and "Parts + materials + other" — or switch to Simple view. **Explain this estimate** summarizes it in plain language. |
| **Parts** | Every part line: Line, Part, Part #, Qty, Status, Unit price, Total. Filter parts by text and status; Outstanding and Paint views isolate what is open. A receipt percentage tracks parts received. |
| **ARW** | The accounts receivable worksheet — one line per receivable: deductible, estimate, supplements 1–9, appearance allowance, betterment, bottom-line discount, prior damage (related and unrelated), and other. CCC-controlled totals (like estimate and supplement amounts) lock to the CCC value; you fill in due and paid amounts for the rest. Received totals roll up to the boards' **Received** column. |
| **Files** | Photos, documents, and uploads for the job — organized, previewable, and downloadable. Drop files to upload from desk or floor. |
| **Signatures** | Signature requests sent for this job: document, recipient, and signing status. |
| **Calendar** | The job's appointments and a planner view of milestones and events on a timeline. |

## Create a repair order

1. Select **New repair order**, or use the command palette.
2. Link or create the **customer** and the **vehicle** — always search first to avoid duplicates.
3. Add **claim and insurer** details if the job is a claim, plus target date, starting status, communication preference, and lead source.
4. Save. Kinetic assigns the repair order number from your shop's sequence immediately.

Kinetic flags likely **duplicates** during creation so you can confirm or continue — check them; merging later is harder.

## Status changes and CCC ONE

Change status by dragging on the board or editing the record. If your shop connects to **CCC ONE**, CCC events update the record automatically and CCC statuses map to your shop's configured statuses. The CCC label shows as the provenance of the value.

<Warning>
Do not park a repair order in the wrong status to hide it. Status is workflow truth — reports, targets, and follow-ups all depend on it.
</Warning>
