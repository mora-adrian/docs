The calendar (`/calendar`) runs the shop's logistics: customer pickups and drop-offs, rental and courtesy moves, vendor runs, and the truck routes that tie them together.

## Views and layout

- **Week / Month tabs** — the toolbar switches between a 7-day week grid and a month grid. The month view additionally shows repair-order milestone chips (arrival and delivery dates).
- **Today button** — jumps back to the current day.
- **Day panel** — selecting a day opens a panel listing that day's appointments, the **Arrivals** and **Deliveries** groups (one deduplicated milestone chip per repair order per direction), and the **Tow plan**.
- **Map** — a map of the day's routes and appointments sits beside the calendar on wide screens and stacks below it on smaller ones.

Each day cell shows appointment chips. When more appointments exist than fit, a "+N more" chip appears; clicking the day shows the full list.

## Appointment types

**New appointment** opens a dialog with the type picker grouped as:

| Group | Types | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Concierge | **Concierge in**, **Concierge out**, **Rental in**, **Rental out** | Your truck moves the vehicle to or from the customer. |
| Sublets | **Sublet out**, **Sublet return** | Vendor legs — the truck moves the vehicle to or from a sublet. |
| In-Person | **Experience in**, **Experience out** | The customer drives in or picks up. |

Other types exist on existing appointments and appear under **Current type** when editing: **Suggested** (estimate visit), **Mechanical out/return**, **Coatings out/return**.

## Scheduling an appointment

1. Click **New appointment**.
2. **Repair order** — search by repair order number or customer. Selecting one pre-fills the address from the customer's address.
3. **Type** — pick from the grouped picker.
4. **When** — date and a half-hour time slot.
5. **Transport** — **In person** or **Tow truck**. Towed types lock this to **Tow truck** automatically.
6. **Place** — one field whose label changes by type: **Pickup address**, **Drop-off address**, **Address**, or **Vendor or address**. It autocompletes addresses and shows a drive-time hint ("≈ 1:20 · 12 mi from the depot").
7. **Gate code** / **Garage code** — only for truck moves.
8. **Notes** — free text.
9. Click **Schedule**.

Scheduling rules: weekdays only, between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM. Kinetic refuses weekend bookings, out-of-window times, and times that don't exist due to daylight saving.

## Editing, canceling, and moving

- Click an appointment row, or use its **Edit appointment** button, to change details.
- **Mark complete** and **Cancel appointment** sit on scheduled rows; completed and cancelled rows show a badge instead.
- **Drag and drop** — drag a chip to another day to move the date (time of day is kept), or drag one row onto another in the day panel to swap their times. Drag is desktop/mouse-oriented; on touch, use the edit dialog instead.
- **Remove duplicates** — the day panel offers **Remove N duplicate(s)** when it detects the same repair order, type, transport, and address booked more than once.

## The tow plan

Kinetic builds the day's truck routes automatically. The first time you view a day that has towed appointments, it plans after a moment; after that, click **Optimize** or **Reoptimize** to rebuild.

- Each route card is a **Truck** (Truck 1, Truck 2). The shop plans for two trucks.
- A truck's day is a chain of **legs** anchored at the shop: the truck leaves the depot, optionally makes a delivery then a pickup, and returns — including a 60-minute lunch return around midday. Legs show stop-by-stop times and per-leg drive times.
- Reorder stops by dragging a leg earlier or later with its grip handle. A truck can only chain a delivery into a pickup; two pickups or a second delivery require returning to base first. Only a draft route can be reordered.

**Route badges and what they mean:**

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Stale** | Appointments changed since the plan was built — reoptimize. |
| **Outside window** | The route can't fit the scheduling window — re-plan or move appointments. |
| **Estimated drive times** | Map routing was unavailable for some legs; those times are straight-line estimates. Reoptimize to recompute. |
| **Past 6:00 PM** | The route finishes after the end of the tow day. |

Stop rows show **Pick up** or **Deliver** badges, the appointment label with the repair order number, the address, and drive time ("+0:45", or "≈+0:45" for a straight-line estimate). Depot rows show "Leave {shop}", "Return to {shop}", and the lunch return.

If the tow plan can't build, Kinetic shows **Could not build the tow plan** — the most common cause is the shop having no address on file, because the shop address anchors every route.

<Note>
There is no driver-assignment field — routes are identified only as Truck 1 and Truck 2. Coordinate which person drives which truck outside the app.
</Note>

## The map

The map draws each truck's route as a colored line with numbered stop pins, a depot pin, and small pins for un-routed appointments. Controls: **Zoom in**, **Zoom out**, **Fit all stops**. If mapping isn't configured for the shop, the map shows "Mapping isn't configured for this shop" — everything else still works.

## The driver feed ("Copy calendar link")

Drivers don't need the app. Click **Copy calendar link** in the day panel to mint a shared calendar feed ("Tow calendar") with a `webcal:` URL. iPhone instructions are provided in place: Settings → Apps → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Add Other Account → Subscribed Calendar → paste the link.

Subscribed drivers see each tow as a calendar event — "P/U in {City, ST ZIP} | {vehicle}" — with the address, gate and garage codes, customer phone, and the planned arrival time when routed.

## The repair-order Calendar tab

A repair order's own **Calendar** tab is different from the global calendar — it's a read-mostly planner for that job:

- **Month grid** with two range bars: **Plan** (start → target) and **Repairs** (actual, in progress until done). A dashed edge means still running.
- **The only editable thing** is the **Target / out** date — drag the bar's end edge, or focus it and use arrow keys (±1 day; Up/Down ±7), Enter to apply, Escape to cancel. Other milestone fields owned by CCC ONE are read-only with a CCC mark.
- **Agenda** — a chronological list of the order's appointments and milestones, grouped by day with relative labels (Today, in 3 days).
- **Milestones** — Appointment, Arrival / in-date, Repairs complete, Repair start, Target / out, Delivered, Archived.
- **Rental expires** — appears automatically 30 days after arrival.

On phones, the month grid hides and only Agenda and Milestones show.
