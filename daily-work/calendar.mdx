title: "Calendar & scheduling"
description: "Appointments, drop-offs, pickups, and route planning tied to repair orders."
---

The **Calendar** screen schedules the shop's real-world commitments: drop-offs, pickups, estimate appointments, deliveries, and errands. Appointments link to the customer and the repair order, so the schedule always carries context.

## What you can do

- **Create appointments** with a type (drop-off, pickup, delivery, estimate), a time, an assignee, and a location.
- **Autofill addresses** from the customer record with address suggestions.
- **Estimate drive time** to plan pickups and deliveries realistically.
- **Plan routes** for a day's stops, and reorder stops when plans change.
- **Subscribe to calendar feeds** so technicians see their day in the calendar app they already use.
- **Catch double-bookings** — deduplication flags appointments that conflict.

<Tip>
Schedule against the repair order, not from memory. An appointment created on a repair order inherits the customer, vehicle, and address, and it appears on the job's timeline.
</Tip>

## Production planning

The **Production** view shows capacity, technician assignments, parts readiness, holds, and aging per status — the inputs a production meeting needs. Your admin configures capacities and constraints in **Settings → Production**.

Scheduling and load-leveling recommendations explain their constraints, so you can see *why* Kinetic suggests a plan before you accept it. Safety- and quality-critical steps are never auto-completed: QC sign-off stays bound to human evidence and checklist state.
