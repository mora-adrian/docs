title: "CSI surveys"
description: "Customer satisfaction surveys — sending, tracking, and closing the loop on responses."
---

**CSI** (Customer Satisfaction Index) surveys measure how the repair went from the customer's side. They're a core quality signal — and often tied to insurer/DRP scorecards.

## How it works

1. After delivery, the customer receives a survey link by text or email.
2. They answer on their phone — no account or app needed; the secure link identifies their repair order.
3. Responses appear on the **CSI** screen and on the repair order's survey stepper.

## The CSI screen

- Track which surveys are sent, opened, and completed.
- Read responses and scores as they come in.
- See survey stage at a glance from the repair order drawer and record.

## Closing the loop

A low score is a task, not a statistic:

1. Read the response and the repair order history.
2. Contact the customer from Messages so the follow-up is on the record.
3. Create a task or note documenting the resolution.

<Tip>
Respond to unhappy surveys the same day. The customer just told you something went wrong while they still care — that window closes fast.
</Tip>

If a customer says their survey link expired or won't open, send a fresh link from the repair order — links are single-purpose and time-limited by design.
