**CSI** (Customer Satisfaction Index) surveys measure the repair from the customer's side. Scores are a core quality signal and often feed insurer and DRP (direct repair program) scorecards.

## How surveys work

1. After delivery, Kinetic schedules and sends a survey link by text or email.
2. The customer answers on their phone — no account or app required; the secure link identifies their repair order.
3. Responses appear on the **CSI** screen and on the repair order's survey stepper.

## The four sections

The screen groups surveys by lifecycle:

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Surveys queued to send. |
| **Sent & awaiting reply** | Delivered but not yet answered — your follow-up list. |
| **Responded** | Completed surveys with scores and comments. |
| **Skipped & cancelled** | Surveys that will not complete — skipped by rule or cancelled. |

## Survey states

Each survey carries a status badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Survey scheduled | Queued to send. |
| Survey sending | Sending right now. |
| Survey sent | Delivered, waiting on the customer. |
| Survey completed | The customer responded — score and comments are in. |
| Survey skipped | Intentionally not sent (for example, an opted-out customer). |
| Survey not sent | A send failure — check the record for the reason. |

## Close the loop on a low score

1. Read the response and the repair order's history first.
2. Contact the customer from Messages so the follow-up stays on the record.
3. Create a task or note documenting the resolution.

<Tip>
Respond to an unhappy survey the same day — the customer told you something went wrong while they still care. That window closes fast.
</Tip>

Survey links are single-purpose and expire. If a customer says a link will not open, send a fresh one from the repair order.
