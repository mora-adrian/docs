---
title: "CSI surveys"
description: "Customer satisfaction surveys — sending, tracking, and closing the loop on responses."
---

**CSI** (Customer Satisfaction Index) surveys measure how the repair went from the customer's side. Scores are a core quality signal, and they often feed insurer and DRP (direct repair program) scorecards.

## How surveys work

1. After delivery, the customer receives a survey link by text or email.
2. The customer answers on their phone. No account or app is required — the secure link identifies their repair order.
3. Responses appear on the **CSI** screen and on the repair order's survey stepper.

## The CSI screen

Use it to:

- Track which surveys are sent, opened, and completed.
- Read responses and scores as they arrive.
- See survey stage at a glance on the repair order drawer and record.

## Close the loop on a low score

A low score is a task, not a statistic.

1. Read the response and the repair order history.
2. Contact the customer from Messages so the follow-up stays on the record.
3. Create a task or note that documents the resolution.

<Tip>
Respond to an unhappy survey the same day. The customer just told you something went wrong while they still care — that window closes fast.
</Tip>

If a customer says their survey link expired or will not open, send a fresh link from the repair order. Survey links are single-purpose and time-limited by design.
