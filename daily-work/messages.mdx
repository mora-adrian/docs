The **Messages** screen is a shared, shop-wide inbox. Texts and emails with customers, adjusters, and vendors live here — not on anyone's personal phone — so coverage continues when someone is out.

## The inbox

- Inbound messages arrive on the known customer/repair order automatically.
- Messages the system can't confidently match land in the inbox for **triage**: assign them to a person or repair order and future messages will route correctly.
- Each thread shows channel (text or email), direction, participants, delivery status, and provider identity.

## Sending

- Reply from the thread, or start a new conversation from a person, repair order, or the Messages screen.
- Outbound sends go through the shop's configured number/address — the customer sees one consistent identity.
- Delivery status is tracked. If a provider reports a failure or an ambiguous result, it shows up for **reconciliation** rather than silently retrying — you'll see the state, never a mystery resend.

<Warning>
Every outbound message is final and auditable — the full thread is kept with the repair order. Write like it's on the record, because it is.
</Warning>

## Calls

Call history appears on repair orders and in the Messages/Calls tabs, including recordings and transcriptions where your phone integration is enabled. Incoming calls can trigger a **screen pop** — when a known customer calls, the matching record opens automatically so you answer with context.

## AI drafts

Where AI drafting is enabled, suggested replies come with their factual grounding and an escalation reason when the system isn't confident. AI can draft — a human sends. It will never invent a promise, coverage claim, price, or delivery date.

## Customer-facing links

Kinetic can send customers secure links for:

- **Facts of loss** — a guided form where the customer describes what happened; responses file onto the repair order.
- **CSI surveys** — satisfaction surveys after delivery (see [CSI](/daily-work/csi)).
- **Email review** — reviewing an outbound communication before it goes.
- **Signatures** — documents sent for electronic signature and tracked back on the repair order.

These links are tokenized and expire — if a customer says a link doesn't work, send a fresh one rather than forwarding your copy.
