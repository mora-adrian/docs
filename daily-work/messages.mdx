The **Messages** screen is a shared, shop-wide inbox: two panes with a conversation list on the left and the open thread on the right. Texts and emails with customers, adjusters, and vendors live here — not on anyone's personal phone — so coverage continues when someone is out.

## The conversation list

- Search conversations by customer, vehicle, claim, or content.
- Conversations attach to the customer and repair order automatically when Kinetic can match them.
- Items it cannot match stay in the inbox for **triage**: assign them to a person or repair order, and future messages from that sender route correctly.
- Each row shows channel (text or email), direction, delivery status, and a preview.

## The thread

Threads read like a phone: your messages on one side, theirs on the other, with delivery status and provider identity. The full history is kept with the repair order, so the thread you see here is the same one on the record's Texts tab.

From a conversation you can:

- **Assign** it to a teammate so ownership is clear.
- **Block or restrict** a sender when a customer opts out — restrictions live on the person's record and apply everywhere.
- Open the linked repair order or person record directly.

## Send a message

The composer is one card: text on top, a toolbar underneath with attach, **Improve with AI**, and **talk to text** dictation, plus the send button.

1. Reply inside a thread, or start a conversation from a person, a repair order, or the Messages screen.
2. The customer sees the shop's configured number or address — one consistent identity.
3. Watch delivery status. A failed or ambiguous provider result is flagged for **reconciliation** rather than silently retried — you always see the real state.

<Warning>
Every outbound message is final and auditable. Write every message as if it were on the record, because it is.
</Warning>

## Calls

Call history appears in the thread and on the repair order's Calls tab, including recordings and transcriptions where your phone integration is enabled. When a known customer calls, a **screen pop** opens the matching record so you answer with context.

## AI drafts

Where AI drafting is enabled, suggested replies show the facts they are grounded in, plus an escalation reason when the system is not confident. AI drafts; a person sends. It never invents a promise, coverage claim, price, or delivery date.

## Customer-facing links

Kinetic sends customers secure links for:

- **Facts of loss** — a guided form where the customer describes what happened; responses file onto the repair order.
- **CSI surveys** — satisfaction surveys after delivery (see [CSI surveys](/daily-work/csi)).
- **Signatures** — documents sent for electronic signature and tracked on the repair order.

Links are tokenized and expire. If a customer says a link does not work, send a fresh one from the repair order instead of forwarding your copy.
