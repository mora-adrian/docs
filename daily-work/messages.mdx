Messages (`/messages`) is the shop's shared inbox for customer texts and email, plus everything that doesn't yet belong to a repair order.

## The inbox

- **Search conversations** — filter the list by name or content.
- Each row shows the sender, channel (SMS, iMessage, or Email), the linked repair order number or an **Unassigned** badge, and the last-activity time.
- **Load more** pages older conversations in.
- **Hidden** — a collapsed section holds conversations you've hidden; nothing is deleted.

Open a conversation to read the thread. On phones, **Back to conversations** returns to the list.

### Assigning an unassigned thread

An unassigned thread is read-only until it belongs to a repair order:

1. Open the conversation.
2. Under **Assign to a repair order**, search repair orders.
3. Pick the order and click **Assign**.

### Conversation actions

The ⋮ menu on a thread:

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Hide for me** / **Restore for me** | Removes the thread from your list only. |
| **Hide for shop** / **Restore for shop** | Hides it from everyone in the shop. Does not block the number or delete messages. |
| **Block on all lines** | Stops inbound messages and direct sends on every text line in the account. Group messages may still pass; history is kept. Requires typing `BLOCK ALL LINES` to confirm. |

Use **Check block status** if the menu reports the block state is unknown.

## The thread

A thread shows bubbles with date markers (Today, Yesterday, weekday names, then ages). Controls:

- **Load older** — pulls earlier history.
- **Message info** — right-click a message → **Info** for Sent/Delivered/Error times, sender, service, and delivery status (Sent, Delivered, Read, Received, Failed, Bounced, Sending).
- **Copy** — right-click a message to copy its text.
- **Tapback reactions** — hover a message to react with Love, Like, Dislike, Laugh, Emphasize, or Question (iMessage service).
- **Save to repair order files** — right-click inbound media, or use the link under an attachment, to file it on the repair order. Click an attachment to preview it; audio plays inline.
- A **typing…** bubble appears when the other party is typing.

Watch the thread header for the **Do not text** alert — outbound texts are blocked for flagged people, and blocked messages can't be sent.

## Sentiment and line health

The thread header carries three live readings — the only place sentiment appears in Kinetic:

- **Conversation sentiment** — an AI reading of the customer: Concerned, Negative, Neutral, Positive, or Delighted, with a 0–100 score, a trend (improving, steady, declining), a summary, and the signals driving it — like "Customer waiting 3 h for a reply" or "4 texts in a row without a reply." If no reading exists yet, you'll see "Signals" — it appears after the next analysis run.
- **Chat health** — the texting network's read on this conversation: Healthy, At risk, Critical, Opted out, or Unknown. At risk means the customer may be cooling off — send messages that invite a reply. Critical means pause routine texts and have a person reach out directly. Opted out means the customer texted STOP — sending is blocked until they text back.
- **Line reputation** — the shop's texting line standing with carriers: Healthy, At risk, Critical, or Unknown. At risk or critical means carriers may filter the line — cut volume and wait for replies.

When these readings deteriorate, the composer itself warns you — for example "You've sent 4 texts without a reply" or "This line's reputation is critical." An opted-out customer is a hard block, not a warning.

## The text composer

The composer has a toolbar of actions, in this order:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Add attachment** | Attach images, video, audio, PDFs, or contact cards — up to 10 per message. A notice warns that carrier delivery of attachments isn't guaranteed. |
| **Attach from repair order files** | Opens a picker of files already on the repair order; attach up to the same limit. |
| **Emoji keyboard** | Searchable emoji picker. |
| **Improve with AI** | Rewrites your draft for clarity, grammar, and professionalism — same meaning, same length class, no new facts or emojis. Template variables are preserved. Text messages only. |
| **Talk to text** | Dictate instead of typing — becomes **Stop and insert text** while recording, then **Transcribing**. Dictated words insert at the cursor. Two-minute cap per take. |
| **Record voice memo** | Swap the composer for a live waveform recorder; **Stop voice memo** sends it as an audio attachment. |
| **Send** | Sends the message — or press ⌘+Enter (Ctrl+Enter on Windows). |

### Message templates

Type `@` in the text composer to open the template menu — each choice shows the name and a two-line preview. Arrow keys move, Enter or Tab inserts, Escape dismisses. Variables like `$CUSTOMER_NAME`, `$VEHICLE_DESCRIPTION`, `$APPOINTMENT_DATE`, `$RO_NUMBER`, `$TARGET_DATE`, `$ADVOCATE_NAME`, `$BRAND_NAME`, and `$TIME_OF_DAY_GREETING` fill in from the repair order when you insert. Templates are managed in **Settings → Messaging → Message templates**.

### Draft autosave

Drafts save automatically — look for "Draft saved" under the field. If the same draft changed on another device or tab, autosave pauses and Kinetic shows the other version with **Keep my version** / **Use the other version** choices.

## Email

Email lives on the repair order's **Emails** tab and in shared conversations. The composer has **To**, **Cc**, and **Subject** fields, attachments from repair order files (up to 10), dictation, and ⌘+Enter send.

**Auto follow-up** — on supplement emails to an adjuster, a switch enables "Auto follow-up every 2 business days until the adjuster replies." The card shows a follow-up log, and follow-ups stop automatically when the adjuster replies, the document arrives, the email bounces, the repair order closes, or a newer supplement goes out. You can also turn it off by hand.

**Send to adjuster** — in the **Files** tab, select documents and click **Send to adjuster** to package them into a supplement email with a prefilled subject and cover note.

## AI drafts

AI-generated replies appear as draft cards in conversations and in the Agent — each shows the recipient, channel, linked repair order, and content, with **Discard** and **Send**. A card reports its state: Queued to send, Sent, Discarded, Couldn't send, or Superseded by a newer draft. A draft is never sent without a person pressing **Send**.
