The **Messages** screen is a shared, shop-wide inbox. Texts and emails with customers, adjusters, and vendors live here — not on anyone's personal phone — so conversations continue when someone is out.

## Work the inbox

- Inbound messages that match a known customer or repair order attach to that record automatically.
- Messages Kinetic cannot match land in the inbox for **triage**. Assign them to a person or repair order; future messages then route correctly.
- Each thread shows the channel (text or email), the direction, the participants, the delivery status, and the provider.

## Send a message

1. Reply inside a thread, or start a conversation from a person, a repair order, or the Messages screen.
2. Write and send. The customer sees the shop's configured number or address — one consistent identity.
3. Watch the delivery status. If the provider reports a failure or an unclear result, the message is flagged for **reconciliation** instead of silently retrying. You always see the real state.

<Warning>
Every outbound message is final and auditable — the full thread stays on the repair order. Write every message as if it were on the record, because it is.
</Warning>

## Calls

Call history appears on repair orders and in the Calls tab, including recordings and transcriptions where your phone integration is enabled. When a known customer calls, a **screen pop** can open the matching record automatically, so you answer with the job in front of you.

## AI drafts

Where AI drafting is enabled, suggested replies show the facts they are based on, plus an escalation reason when the system is not confident. AI can draft; a person sends. It never invents a promise, a coverage claim, a price, or a delivery date.

## Customer-facing links

Kinetic sends customers secure links for:

- **Facts of loss** — a guided form where the customer describes what happened. Responses file onto the repair order.
- **CSI surveys** — satisfaction surveys sent after delivery. See [CSI surveys](/daily-work/csi).
- **Email review** — a review page for an outbound communication before it sends.
- **Signatures** — documents sent for electronic signature and tracked back to the repair order.

Links are tokenized and expire. If a customer says a link does not work, send a fresh one from the repair order instead of forwarding your copy.
