Kinetic keeps three core record types under **Records** in the sidebar: **People**, **Companies**, and **Vehicles**. Each is a searchable list with server-derived relationship counts, and each opens into a full detail screen.

## The three record types

| Record | What it holds | Its detail screen shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **People** | Customers, claimants, and contacts at other businesses: name, phone, email, preferred contact method, restrictions, and enrichment fields where enabled. | Contact details, communication preferences, linked companies, every repair order and message they appear on, and activity history. |
| **Companies** | Insurers, tow companies, dealers, vendors, fleets, and referral sources. | Company details, linked contacts (the people who work there), related repair orders, and activity. |
| **Vehicles** | Year/make/model/trim, VIN (vehicle identification number), color, plate, and market value when available. | Vehicle identity, valuation, the full repair history for that car, and linked owners. |

<Info>
"Customer" is not a separate entity — a customer is a person with a repair order. The same person record can be a vendor contact or a claimant on another job. One person, one record.
</Info>

## Why records matter

Everything links back to records. Texting a customer files the conversation on their person record and their repair order. When a vehicle returns a year later, its record shows the earlier repair. Clean records are what make the Agent's answers, the reports, and the automations accurate.

## Working with records

1. **Search before you create.** Look up a person by name or phone; look up a vehicle by VIN. A duplicate splits message history and repair history.
2. **Check duplicate suggestions.** Kinetic surfaces likely duplicates with evidence. Review before confirming a merge — merges are deliberate and never automatic, and never cross shops.
3. **Read relationship counts.** List rows show how many repair orders, vehicles, or messages each record has — a quick tell between a real record and a stub.
4. **Refresh vehicle value when it matters.** Where configured, a vehicle's record carries a market-value range that feeds expected insurance payout. Refresh it when the situation changes — for example, when a job might total.

## Contact details, preferences, and consent

A person's record is where preferred channel (text or email) and communication restrictions live. Honor what is recorded — if a customer asked not to be texted, that restriction applies everywhere they appear. Where contact enrichment is enabled, it fills only *empty* fields and never overwrites something a person entered.
