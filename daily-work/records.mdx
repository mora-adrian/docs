Records are the shop's shared address book and garage: people, companies, and vehicles that link to repair orders, communications, and each other.

## Lists

- **People** — Name, Phone, Email, Address, Referral source, Vehicles, Open repair orders.
- **Companies** — Company, Type, Address, Contacts, Linked repair orders.
- **Vehicles** — Vehicle, VIN, Owner, Odometer, Condition, Repair orders.

Search and open a record to see its detail panel with **Edit** and **Archive** (with a confirm step).

## Person record

**Fields:** Name (first/middle/last/suffix/nickname), up to three phones, email, address, company, referral source, default category, social usernames, and **Do not text** — a switch plus a reason that blocks outgoing texts while leaving email and calls working.

**Detail view:** contact info, company, referral, linked **Vehicles** and **Repair orders**.

**Contact enrichment** — when enabled in Settings, an **Enrich** button looks up public profile data (address, age, birth year, company, job title, socials, additional phone/email). Matches are labeled **High** or **Uncertain match**; enrichment only fills empty fields — it never overwrites what a person entered. If it isn't configured or there isn't enough detail to look up, Kinetic says so on the button.

## Company record

**Fields:** Legal name, DBA (doing business as), category, phone, fax, email, website, address and mailing address, social usernames.

**Detail view:** linked **Contacts** and **Repair orders**. **Enrich** pulls public company data — description, industry, employee count, estimated revenue, socials.

## Vehicle record

**Fields:** VIN, year, make, model, trim, exterior and interior color, odometer and odometer-out (which fills automatically at delivery), condition, license plate and state.

**Detail view:** owner and full **Repair history** across repair orders.

## How records relate

- A person owns vehicles and links to repair orders as customer, driver, or referrer.
- A company links contacts (its people) and repair orders.
- A vehicle carries its repair history no matter which order it appeared on.
- Repair orders reference all three — changing a person or vehicle updates every order that uses it.

<Note>
There is no merge/duplicate-resolution screen. To fix a duplicate, edit the record you want to keep and archive the other.
</Note>
