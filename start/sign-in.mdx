Kinetic protects client and insurance information, so every account uses a password **and** a six-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone. This page walks through every step you will meet.

## Before You Begin

You need:

1. **An invitation.** A shop admin or owner invites you from **Settings → Members**. You get an email with a link to join your shop. Accounts are never self-service.
2. **A phone with an authenticator app.** Any standard one works: 1Password, Authy, Google Authenticator, or Microsoft Authenticator. The app is free and takes a minute to install.
3. **Kinetic's web address: `collisionpartners.app`.** Bookmark it. Every shop uses the same address; Kinetic opens the shop your account belongs to. Old links to `kandmcollision.collisionpartners.app` still work and forward to it.

## Your First Sign-in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept your invitation">
    Open the invitation email and choose its link. The page says **Join** and your shop's name. Enter your **Full name** and a **Password** (8 or more characters), then choose **Create account**. If you already have a Kinetic account, the page says **You already have an account** instead: sign in with your existing email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Choose **Continue to sign in**. The page is titled **Sign in** and says **Use your work email and password.** Enter your email address and password, then choose **Sign in with password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up two-factor authentication">
    The first time, Kinetic sends you to **Set up two-factor authentication**. Enter your password again and choose **Set up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Open your authenticator app, add a new account, and scan the QR code on the screen. If you cannot scan it, copy the **pairing key** shown beside it and paste it into the app instead. Then type the six-digit code the app shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish setup">
    Kinetic shows a list of one-time **backup codes**. You can copy or download them if you like; they are optional. Tick **I have saved these backup codes** and choose **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You are in">
    If you belong to one shop you land in it. If you have a linked employee profile you land on **Today**; if not, you land on the **Repairs** board.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you ever lose your phone, your Kinetic administrator can reset your two-factor sign-in, so you are never locked out for good. See [Lost or replaced phone](#lost-or-replaced-phone).

## Every Day After That

1. Go to `collisionpartners.app`.
2. Enter your email and password and choose **Sign in with password**.
3. On the **Authenticate** screen, enter the six-digit code from your authenticator app and choose **Authenticate**.

The code changes every 30 seconds. If it fails, wait for the next one, and check that your phone's clock is set to automatic.

## Signing In with a Passkey

If your browser supports passkeys, the sign-in page also shows **Sign in with a passkey** below an **or** divider. Choose it and approve the prompt on your device with your fingerprint, face, or device PIN.

A passkey replaces **both** your password and the six-digit code, so you skip the **Authenticate** screen. If you have saved a passkey on the device, typing your email may also offer it inline.

You add passkeys yourself under **Settings → Security → Passkeys**. See [Your security settings](/admin/personal-security#passkeys). Your password and authenticator app keep working, so you can always fall back to them.

## How Long You Stay Signed In

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Idle limit** | 4 hours with no activity. |
| **Hard limit** | 12 hours after you signed in, no matter how active you are. |

In practice, expect to sign in **once per workday**. If your session ends while you are in the middle of something, Kinetic opens a **Sign in to continue** window that says **Your session ended. Sign in to pick up where you left off.** After you sign in again, you continue where you were.

<Note>
Closing the browser tab does not sign you out. On a shared or shop-floor computer, always choose **Sign out** from the shop menu at the top of the left sidebar when you finish.
</Note>

## Extra Checks for Sensitive Actions

Some actions are protected with a **fresh** code, even though you are already signed in. When you start one, a **Confirm it's you** window says **This action needs a fresh verification before it can continue.** Enter the current code from your authenticator app, or choose **Use a backup code instead**.

You will see this when you:

- add members, change roles, or suspend or restore someone,
- change shop settings, production capacity, or reports,
- connect or disconnect a mailbox or the phone system,
- archive a person, company, vehicle, or repair order,
- block a phone number,
- delete files or remove a party from a repair order.

After you verify once, you are trusted for **240 minutes** (four hours), so batch your admin work into one sitting.

Sending a signature request, sending files to CCC ONE, and switching shops need a verified sign-in but not a fresh code. If you signed in with your authenticator app code, they go straight through. You are asked for a code only if you signed in with a passkey that did not check your fingerprint, face, or PIN.

## More Than One Shop

If your account has access to more than one shop, you can switch between them from the shop menu at the top of the left sidebar. The menu lists your shops under **Shop**; choose another and the app reloads in that shop. The web address stays the same.

If you belong to several shops and none is chosen yet, Kinetic shows the same list right after you sign in, on a page titled **Select a workspace** (the app's own wording for your list of shops), with your name and email at the top. Choose a shop to enter it.

## "Access Pending"

If you see **Access pending** after you sign in, your account is real but is not attached to a shop yet. If there is an invitation waiting, it appears here with an **Accept** button. Otherwise, contact your administrator and then choose to retry.

An invitation can expire. If **Accept** fails, ask your admin to send a new one.

## Forgot Your Password

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your email and choose **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password.

Kinetic always says **If an account exists for that email, a reset link is on its way.** It says this whether or not the account exists, so nobody can use the form to find out who works at your shop. A reset link expires; if it does, request a new one.

## Lost or Replaced Phone

| Your situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You can still sign in on another device, or you are already signed in | Go to **Settings → Security** and choose **Reconfigure** to link your new phone. |
| You are locked out at the **Authenticate** screen | Ask your Kinetic administrator to reset your two-factor sign-in. The next time you sign in, Kinetic walks you through setting up your authenticator app again. The **Authenticate** screen does not accept backup codes. |

<Tip>
When you scan the QR code during setup, you can also copy the **pairing key** into your password manager. With it you can add Kinetic to a new phone yourself, without waiting for a reset.
</Tip>

## If Sign-in Says "Too Many Attempts"

Kinetic limits sign-in attempts to protect accounts. Wait a minute and try again. The limit applies to the email address and to the network you are on.

## Acceptable Use

Signing in means you agree to the **Acceptable Use Policy**, linked at the bottom of the sign-in page. In short: use Kinetic for shop work, never share your credentials, and never forward a client link (survey, signature, facts-of-loss) to anyone but that client.
