Your access in Lever comes from one thing: your **role** in your shop. There are three roles. The role is set by an admin in **Settings → Members**, and you can see yours next to your name in the member list.

## The Three Roles

| Role | Who it is for | In one sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Member** | Nearly everyone: client advocates, estimators, technicians, parts staff, front office. | Do the day-to-day work on repair orders, records, messages, and tasks. |
| **Admin** | Shop managers and office managers. | Everything a Member can do, plus manage people, settings, and archive records. |
| **Owner** | The shop owner and general manager. | Everything an Admin can do, plus manage other Owners. |

<Info>
**Admins and Owners have the same day-to-day powers.** The only difference is that an Owner can create, promote, suspend, or remove another Owner, and an Admin cannot. Lever will not let a shop end up with no Owner at all.
</Info>

## Job Title Is Not Access

Your **job title** (technician, estimator, advocate) appears on your employee profile and decides things like who shows up in a technician pick list. It does **not** grant or limit permissions. Two people with the same title can have different roles, and a Member who is a manager still cannot change settings.

## What Each Role Can Do

Read the table left to right: a check means "yes".

| Area | Member | Admin / Owner |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| **See** repair orders, boards, records, files, the calendar, messages, and reports on screen | Yes | Yes |
| **Work** repair orders: move status, set target date, edit facts, add notes, upload and tag files, manage parties | Yes | Yes |
| **Edit** people, companies, and vehicles | Yes | Yes |
| **Send** texts and emails, record calls, save files from messages | Yes | Yes |
| **Schedule** appointments and plan tow routes | Yes | Yes |
| **Send** signature requests and facts-of-loss links | Yes | Yes |
| **Review** estimates with Rivet and record verdicts | Yes | Yes |
| **Ask** the Agent questions and approve its drafts | Yes | Yes |
| **Create and work** tasks assigned to you or sent by you | Yes | Yes |
| **See every** task on the shop (the **All** view on the Tasks screen) | No | Yes |
| **Archive** a person, company, or vehicle | No | Yes |
| **Add members**, change roles, suspend or restore people, set days off | No | Yes |
| **Change shop settings**: hours, targets, messaging, automation switches, reports | No | Yes |
| **Production capacity** and overload alerts | View only | Edit |
| **AI usage** | No | Yes |
| **Connect** the phone system or reference documents | No | Yes |
| **Manage Owners** | No | Owner only |

<Note>
Your **personal** settings are always yours, whatever your role: your password, authenticator app, notification choices, email signature, mailbox, days off, and appearance.
</Note>

## What You Cannot See

Whatever your role, you can never see another shop's data. A record that belongs to a different shop is indistinguishable from one that does not exist. There are no share links and no temporary access.

## Protected Actions Ask for a Fresh Code

Some actions are protected even for admins and owners. Lever asks for a new authenticator app code the first time you do one, then trusts you for 240 minutes. The list is on the [Signing in](/start/sign-in#extra-checks-for-sensitive-actions) page.

## Choosing the Right Role for Someone

| Person | Recommended role |
| --- | --- |
| Client advocates, estimators, technicians, parts staff, front office | **Member** |
| Shop manager, office manager | **Admin** |
| Shop owner, general manager | **Owner**. Keep this to one or two people. |

Give someone the lowest role that lets them do their job. It is easy to promote later, and hard to undo an archived record.

## Changing or Removing Access

Admins manage this in **Settings → Members**. See [Members](/admin/members) for the exact steps, including what happens to a person's work when they leave.
