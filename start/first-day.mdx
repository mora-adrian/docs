Plan on about **20 minutes** for the first-day checklist. The first-week plan spreads the rest across a few short sessions so you never feel buried.

## First-day Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and secure your account (5 minutes)">
    Follow [Signing in](/start/sign-in). By the end you should have: a working password, your authenticator app linked, and your backup codes saved somewhere private.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up how Kinetic reaches you (3 minutes)">
    Open **Settings → Preferences**.

    - Choose your **Theme** (System, Light, or Dark) and **Notification sound**.
    - Under **Push notifications**, choose **Enable notifications** on the device you carry. Choose **Test** to prove it works. On an iPhone, add Kinetic to your Home Screen first. See [Alerts on your phone](/work/notifications#alerts-on-your-phone).
    - Write your **Email signature** if you will send email from Kinetic.
    - If you handle claim email, connect your **Mailbox**. See [Email review](/communicate/email-review).
    - If you take days off, set your **Schedule** so customers get the right message. See [Your preferences](/admin/personal-preferences).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Today (1 minute)">
    If you have a linked employee profile you land here automatically. **Today** lists the jobs where you are the repair planner or client advocate and something needs a push: close to delivery, past the money gate, supplements waiting, or missing documents. See [Today](/work/today).

    <Note>
    If **Today** says you have no work, it may only mean your user has not been linked to an employee profile yet. Ask your admin.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk the Repairs board (5 minutes)">
    Choose **Repairs** in the left sidebar and switch to **Board**. Scroll through every lane. For each card, find the repair order number, the customer, the vehicle, and the days in shop. Click a card once to open the drawer. Click **Open full screen** on one and explore its tabs. See [Pipeline overview](/pipeline/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find something with search (1 minute)">
    Press <Key>⌘</Key> <Key>K</Key> (<Key>Ctrl</Key> <Key>K</Key> on Windows) and type a customer's last name or a repair order number. Open the result. See [A tour of the screen](/start/tour#the-command-palette).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Messages (2 minutes)">
    Choose **Messages**. Open a conversation. Notice the repair order number or an **Unassigned** badge beside each customer. See [Messages](/communicate/messages).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do three small things on a real job (3 minutes)">
    On any active repair order that is yours:

    1. Post a **note** on the **Activity** tab. Try the microphone to dictate it.
    2. Create a **task** on the **Tasks** tab with a due date.
    3. Upload a **photo** on the **Files** tab.

    These three actions cover a large share of daily work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
That is the first day. You do not need to learn anything else yet.
</Check>

## First-week Plan

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Day 2: statuses">
    Read [All the statuses](/pipeline/statuses). Pick three vehicles on the Repairs board and, for each, explain out loud why it is in its current status and what has to happen next.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Day 3: customers and texting">
    Read [Texting customers](/communicate/texting) and [Records](/records/overview). Send one real text with the template menu (<Key>@</Key>). Look up a customer and open their vehicle's repair history.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Day 4: money and paperwork">
    Read [ARW](/repair-orders/arw), [Files](/repair-orders/files), and [Signatures](/repair-orders/signatures). Open a job near delivery and check that its documents and payments are in order.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Day 5: the end of the job">
    Read [Calendar](/schedule/calendar) and [CSI](/csi/overview). Look at the customer survey step on a delivered job. Then skim [Dos and don'ts](/help/dos-and-donts).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Week 2: AI tools">
    Read [AI overview](/ai/overview), [Rivet](/ai/rivet), and [Agent](/ai/agent). Ask the Agent a question you would normally dig for. Review one estimate with Rivet.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## A Daily Rhythm That Works

| When | Do this | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Start of day** | Clear what is overdue and see what needs you. | **Today**, **Tasks** |
| **Start of day** | Scan the board for jobs that are late or blocked. | **Repairs** |
| **Through the day** | Answer customers and adjusters from inside Kinetic. | **Messages**, repair order tabs |
| **Through the day** | Update a status the moment the real world changes. | The board |
| **End of day** | Sweep the board so tomorrow starts accurate. | **Repairs** |
| **Weekly** | Review aging reports and the CSI results. | Email, **CSI** |

More routines are in [Playbooks](/playbooks/overview).

## Common First-day Problems

| What you see | What it usually means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Access pending** | Your account is not attached to a shop yet. | Ask your admin. See [Signing in](/start/sign-in#access-pending). |
| **Today** is empty | No linked employee profile, or nothing needs attention. | Ask your admin to confirm your employee profile. |
| A field will not let you edit | It is marked **CCC ONE**. | Change it in CCC ONE. See [How Kinetic thinks](/start/how-kinetic-thinks#3-every-fact-has-an-owner). |
| You are asked for a code again | A protected action. | Enter the code from your authenticator app. This is normal. |
| No **Email review** in the menu | You have not connected a mailbox. | Connect one in **Settings → Preferences**. |
