Lever has the same screens for everyone, but each job uses a different handful of them. Find your job below and read those pages first. Your **access role** (Member, Admin, Owner) is separate; see [Roles and access](/start/roles).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Client advocate / service advisor">
    You are the customer's main contact from first call to pickup.

    **Your day:** [Today](/work/today), [Messages](/communicate/messages), [Tasks](/work/tasks), [CSI](/csi/overview).

    **Read first:**
    1. [Daily sweep](/playbooks/daily-sweep)
    2. [Lead to scheduled](/playbooks/lead-to-scheduled)
    3. [Texting](/communicate/texting)
    4. [Activity and notes](/repair-orders/activity)
    5. [Delivery](/playbooks/delivery)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Estimator">
    You write and defend the estimate.

    **Your day:** [Estimate tab](/repair-orders/estimate), [Rivet](/ai/rivet), [Files](/repair-orders/files).

    **Read first:**
    1. [Estimate](/repair-orders/estimate)
    2. [Rivet](/ai/rivet)
    3. [Supplements](/playbooks/supplements)
    4. [Email](/communicate/email)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Parts manager">
    You get the right parts here on time.

    **Your day:** [Parts](/repair-orders/parts), the **Parts Hold** lane on [Repairs](/pipeline/overview).

    **Read first:**
    1. [Parts](/repair-orders/parts)
    2. [Parts delays](/playbooks/parts-delays)
    3. [Statuses](/pipeline/statuses)
    4. [Tasks](/work/tasks)

    **Your day:** the [Repairs board](/pipeline/views), the repair order [drawer](/repair-orders/drawer), [Files](/repair-orders/files) for photos.

    **Read first:**
    1. [How Lever thinks](/start/how-lever-thinks)
    2. [Statuses](/pipeline/statuses)
    3. [Facts: first scan](/repair-orders/facts#first-scan)
    4. [Files](/repair-orders/files)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Office / accounts">
    You make sure the money matches.

    **Your day:** [ARW](/repair-orders/arw), the **Past the money gate** section of [Today](/work/today).

    **Read first:**
    1. [ARW](/repair-orders/arw)

    3. [Signatures](/repair-orders/signatures)
