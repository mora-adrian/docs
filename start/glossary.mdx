## Kinetic terms

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Shop">
    Your collision center. Everything in Kinetic belongs to exactly one shop, and you cannot see another shop's data.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Repair order">
    One job on one vehicle, from first contact until it is archived. It keeps the same internal link for its whole life. It also has a human **repair order number**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Status">
    The step a repair order is in, such as Parts Hold or Paint. Every repair order has exactly one. See [All the statuses](/pipeline/statuses).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Board">
    A screen that groups repair orders by status. There are four: **Opportunities**, **Repairs**, **Close & Deliver**, and **Enrichment**. Each can be shown as a **Table**, a **Board** of columns, or a **Timeline**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Lane (or column)">
    One status on a board, drawn as a vertical column of cards.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Card">
    One repair order on a board, showing its number, customer, vehicle, and key facts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Drawer (or peek)">
    The read-only panel that slides in from the right when you click a card or row. Its one action is **Open full screen**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tone">
    One of four meanings carried by the colored dot beside a status: **Needs us** (blue), **Waiting** (amber), **On track** (green), **Late** (red).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CCC ONE (and the CCC ONE mark)">
    Your estimating system. When a value comes from CCC ONE, Kinetic shows a **CCC ONE** mark beside it and does not let you edit it here. See [How Kinetic thinks](/start/how-kinetic-thinks#3-every-fact-has-an-owner).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Member">
    A person with an account in your shop. Members have a **role**: **Member**, **Admin**, or **Owner**. See [Roles](/start/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Command palette">
    The search box that opens with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows). It finds repair orders, people, companies, vehicles, screens, and settings pages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Today">
    Your personal screen: the repair orders you are responsible for that need attention. See [Today](/work/today).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rivet">
    The estimate-review tool. It suggests billable operations that might be missing from an estimate. See [Rivet](/ai/rivet).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Agent">
    The chat screen where you ask questions about your shop's data in plain language. See [Agent](/ai/agent).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Draft">
    Text you have started but not sent. Kinetic saves drafts automatically. An AI-written message waiting for approval is also a draft.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do not text">
    A setting on a person that blocks outgoing texts to them. Email and calls still work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Chat health and line reputation">
    Two readings that tell you whether texting a customer is still a good idea. See [Texting](/communicate/texting#chat-health-and-line-reputation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Team chat">
    A group text on the shop's texting line for staff only, where you can ask Kinetic questions or give it instructions. See [Texting Kinetic](/ai/text-kinetic).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Screen pop">
    A small card that appears when the shop phone rings, showing who is calling and their open repair orders.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The job itself

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Estimate">
    An itemized list of repairs, parts, and labor with prices. Written in CCC ONE. Also called the "initial estimate" or "first estimate".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Supplement">
    An additional estimate for damage found after the car is taken apart. The insurer must approve it. Kinetic numbers them Supplement 1 through Supplement 9 on the payment worksheet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Teardown, or blueprinting">
    Taking the damaged area apart to find all the damage. Done before final repair planning.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Scan (first scan, pre-repair scan, post-repair scan)">
    A diagnostic check that reads a vehicle's computer for fault codes. The **first scan** happens before repairs. A **post-repair scan** happens afterward. Kinetic tracks the first one with an icon on the card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Calibration, ADAS">
    **ADAS** stands for advanced driver assistance systems: the cameras and radar behind features like lane-keeping and automatic braking. After certain repairs these must be **calibrated** to point exactly where they should.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="OEM, aftermarket, LKQ">
    **OEM** parts come from the vehicle's manufacturer. **Aftermarket** parts are made by another company. **LKQ** (like, kind, and quality) parts are used parts from another vehicle.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sublet">
    Work the shop sends to an outside vendor, such as glass, alignment, or certain mechanical work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Blend (paint)">
    Fading new paint into the adjacent panels so the color matches without a visible line.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="R&I">
    Remove and install: taking a part off to reach something else, then putting it back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Structural">
    Repairs to the frame or the parts that hold the car's shape and protect its occupants.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Total loss">
    A vehicle the insurer decides is not worth repairing. They pay the customer the vehicle's value instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Repairability">
    Kinetic's word for whether a vehicle is being repaired or has been declared a total loss.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Outcome likelihood">
    A 1 to 4 score for how close the cost of repair is to the vehicle's value. 1 means a safe repair; 4 means a likely total loss. See [Facts](/repair-orders/facts#outcome-likelihood).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Target date, or out date">
    The date the shop expects to return the vehicle.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cycle time, key-to-key">
    The number of days from the customer handing over the key to getting it back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Concierge">
    The shop's truck collects or delivers the customer's vehicle. Kinetic labels these **Concierge in** and **Concierge out**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Experience in, Experience out">
    The customer drives the vehicle in themselves, or collects it in person. These are the in-person counterparts of Concierge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Facts of loss">
    The customer's own description of the accident: what happened, who was in the car, and what they noticed afterward. Kinetic collects it through a short online form.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## People and insurance

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Insurer">
    The insurance company paying for the repair.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Claim, claim number">
    The insurer's file for the accident. The claim number identifies it. Kinetic shows it with the insurer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Insured, claimant">
    The **insured** holds the insurance policy. A **claimant** is making a claim against someone else's policy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Adjuster">
    The insurer's representative who reviews damage and approves payment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Appraiser">
    An independent expert who estimates damage. A customer may hire one to challenge an insurer's number.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Appraisal clause">
    A right in many insurance policies to have independent appraisers resolve a dispute over the amount. Kinetic has a ready document for invoking it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deductible">
    The part of a covered repair the customer pays themselves.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Betterment">
    A deduction an insurer takes when a new part leaves the car better than before the accident.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Appearance allowance">
    A payment made instead of repairing minor cosmetic damage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bottom-line discount">
    A flat reduction taken from the estimate total.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Prior damage (related, unrelated)">
    Damage that was already on the car before this accident. **Related** prior damage sits in the area being repaired. **Unrelated** prior damage is elsewhere. Kinetic lists both as purposes on the payment worksheet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="DRP (direct repair program)">
    An arrangement between a shop and an insurer that speeds up claims. Insurers often watch satisfaction scores for these shops.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Client advocate">
    The shop employee who owns the customer relationship. Called a service advisor or customer service representative in many shops.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Repair planner">
    The shop employee who plans the work and keeps it moving, often an estimator or production manager.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Referrer">
    The person or business who sent a customer to the shop.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Money

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="ARW (accounts receivable worksheet)">
    The tab where you record who owes what, what has been paid, and what is on its way. See [ARW](/repair-orders/arw).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Prepaid, in transit, due">
    **Prepaid** is money already received. **In transit** is money sent or promised but not yet landed. **Due** is what is still owed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Money gate">
    A point in the repair after which a job should have received some payment. Kinetic flags jobs that pass it with nothing paid. See [Today](/work/today).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Expected insurance payout">
    A market-value range from recent vehicle sales: what the insurer is likely to value the car at. Shown on the repair order.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Satisfaction and reputation

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="CSI">
    Customer satisfaction index: the surveys Kinetic sends after delivery and the score they produce.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pulse check">
    The one-question text survey (1 to 10) that goes out after delivery.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="KPI">
    Key performance indicator: a number you track against a target, such as cars delivered per day.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Technology

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Authenticator app">
    A free app on your phone that shows a six-digit code that changes every 30 seconds. Kinetic asks for it when you sign in. 1Password, Authy, Google Authenticator, and Microsoft Authenticator all work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Authenticator app code">
    The six-digit code shown by your authenticator app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Backup codes">
    Single-use codes for signing in if you lose your phone. Shown once, so save them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Two-factor authentication (2FA)">
    Signing in with something you know (a password) and something you have (your phone).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Session">
    The period you stay signed in. Kinetic ends it after 4 hours idle or 12 hours total.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Push notification">
    An alert that appears on your device even when Kinetic is not open.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Home Screen app (PWA)">
    Kinetic added to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app. On iPhone and iPad this is required to receive push notifications.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Dictation (talk to text)">
    Speaking instead of typing. A microphone button in the composer turns your voice into text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Voice memo">
    A recorded audio message sent as an attachment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Calendar feed (subscribed calendar)">
    A link you add to your phone's calendar app so events appear there and stay updated automatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Template">
    A saved message you can insert with <kbd>@</kbd> in the text composer, with the customer's details filled in for you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Enrichment (contact enrichment)">
    Looking up public profile details to fill in blank fields on a contact. It never overwrites what a person entered.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
