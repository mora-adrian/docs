Every screen in Lever shares the same frame. Learn it once and you can find your way anywhere.

## The Frame

On a computer the screen has three parts:

1. **The left sidebar**, which lists every screen.
2. **The top bar**, which shows where you are (a breadcrumb) and the controls for that screen.
3. **The main area**, which scrolls.

On a phone, the sidebar becomes a menu that slides in from the left when you tap the menu button in the app bar at the top.

## The Left Sidebar

### The Shop Menu (Top)

The first row shows your **shop's logo and name**. Click it to open the shop menu:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Shop** list | Appears only if you belong to more than one shop. Choose a different shop to switch. A check mark shows the current one. |
| **Support** | Opens an email to the Lever team, with your name and the time already in the subject. |
| **Report a problem** | Opens a form so you can describe something that looks broken. |
| **Suggest a feature** | Opens a form for ideas. |
| **Sign out** | Ends your session. |

Beside the shop name is a **Collapse sidebar** button. A collapsed sidebar shrinks to icons only; hover over an icon to see its name. <Key>Ctrl</Key> <Key>B</Key> (<Key>⌘</Key> <Key>B</Key> on a Mac) collapses and expands it from the keyboard.

### Search

Below the shop menu is the **Search** button, which opens the [command palette](#the-command-palette). The shortcut is <Key>⌘</Key> <Key>K</Key> on a Mac or <Key>Ctrl</Key> <Key>K</Key> on Windows.

### The Menu Groups

| Group | Items | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *(top)* | **Today**, **Notifications**, **Tasks** | **Notifications** shows a red number for unread items. **Tasks** shows a red number for tasks assigned to you that you have not seen yet. |
| **Communications** | **Messages**, **Calendar**, **CSI**, **Email review** | **Messages** shows a conversation count. **Email review** appears only when you have connected a work mailbox, and shows a red number for items waiting. |
| **Pipeline** | **Opportunities**, **Repairs**, **Close & Deliver**, **Enrichment** | Each shows how many repair orders it holds. |
| **Records** | **People**, **Companies**, **Vehicles** | Each shows how many records exist. |
| **Operations** | **Agent**, **Rivet** | The AI tools. |
| **Recent** | Up to five repair orders you opened lately | Shows the repair order number and customer name. Click one to go straight back. It reads **No recent orders** until you open some. |

**Settings** sits at the bottom of the sidebar.

<Note>
A red badge means "this wants your attention". A gray count just tells you how much is there.
</Note>

## The Top Bar

Every screen starts with a **breadcrumb**, a short trail that tells you where you are. On the four boards and the other list screens, a small gray number beside it shows how many items are in the view.

On the four boards the top bar also has:

- **Refresh data**, a circular-arrow button that reloads the list. It spins while it works.
- **Table | Board | Timeline**, a switch for how the list is drawn. See [Views](/pipeline/views).

## The Drawer

Click a card or a row once and a panel slides in from the right: the **drawer**. It shows the repair order's identity, status, next dates, and recent activity at a glance. It is read-only, so you cannot change anything by accident.

- The page behind stays usable. Click another row and the drawer swaps to it.
- Press <Key>Esc</Key> or click **Close drawer** to dismiss it.
- Click **Open full screen** to work on the repair order.

The drawer opens the same way wherever a repair order appears: boards, Messages, Calendar, Notifications, and the records screens. See [The drawer](/repair-orders/drawer).

## The Full Repair Order Screen

Clicking **Open full screen** takes you to a page just for that repair order. Its top bar has:

- **Back to list**, an arrow that returns you to where you came from.
- The repair order number, its status pill, and a scan icon for vehicles being repaired.
- **Tabs**: Activity, Emails, Texts, Calls, Tasks, Estimate, Parts, ARW, Files, Signatures, and Calendar. Some tabs show a count.
- **Previous repair order** and **Next repair order** arrows with "3 of 24", so you can step through the list you came from without going back.

The left side shows the customer and vehicle, the status rail, the facts, the parties, and the customer survey. See [Repair orders](/repair-orders/overview).

## The Command Palette

Press <Key>⌘</Key> <Key>K</Key> (or <Key>Ctrl</Key> <Key>K</Key>) from anywhere. A search window opens.

- **With nothing typed**, it lists every screen so you can jump with the keyboard.
- **With text typed**, it searches repair orders, people, companies, and vehicles. You can search by repair order number, customer name, or VIN (the vehicle's 17-character serial number).
- It also finds **screens and settings pages by keyword**. Typing `staff` finds **Members**; `texting` finds **Messaging**.
- If there are more related people, companies, or vehicles than fit, choose **Show related records** to see them.
- Results only ever include what your shop owns and what you are allowed to see.

Use <Key>↑</Key> and <Key>↓</Key> to move, <Key>Enter</Key> to open, and <Key>Esc</Key> to close.

## On a Phone

On a phone or tablet, Lever adapts without removing features:

- An **app bar** at the top opens the menu.
- Buttons and rows get larger touch targets.
- Windows open as **bottom sheets** that slide up from the screen's bottom edge.
- Tab bars, columns on the board, and the KPI strip scroll sideways.
- Messages shows either the conversation list or the open conversation, one at a time, with **Back to conversations** to return.
- You can take photos and attach files straight from the phone.

<Tip>
**Add Lever to your Home Screen.** On iPhone or iPad, open Lever in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. It opens like an app, and it is *required* if you want push alerts on Apple devices. See [Notifications](/work/notifications#alerts-on-your-phone).
</Tip>

## Colors and Light or Dark

Lever's **Theme** can be **System** (follow your device), **Light**, or **Dark**. Change it, along with the typeface (**Inter**, **Geist**, or **IBM Plex Sans**) and the notification sound, in **Settings → Preferences**. See [Your preferences](/admin/personal-preferences).

Status dots follow the same meanings everywhere: <Pill tone="needs">needs us</Pill>, <Pill tone="waiting">waiting</Pill>, <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill>, <Pill tone="late">late</Pill>.

## If a Screen Looks Wrong

- A screen that cannot load shows an **error with a retry button**. It never shows an empty list in place of a failure.
- An **offline** message appears when your connection drops. Reconnect and choose retry.
- If something still looks broken, use **Report a problem** in the shop menu. Add a sentence about what happened (optional); the technical details are attached for you, and you get a **Support id** to quote if you follow up.
