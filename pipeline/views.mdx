Each of the four boards can be shown three ways. Switch between them with the **Table | Board | Timeline** control at the top right. All three show the same repair orders; only the drawing changes. Click any card or row once to open its [drawer](/repair-orders/drawer).

| View | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Table** | Finding one job fast, comparing many jobs on the same facts, sorting, and filtering. |
| **Board** | Seeing how many jobs are in each status, and moving jobs along. |
| **Timeline** | Seeing how long each job is planned to take, and which ones are running late. |

## Board View

The Board shows one **lane** (a vertical column) for each status, from left to right, with a **card** for each repair order.

### Reading a Lane

- A **colored dot** and the **status name** at the top, then a gray count of cards.
- At the bottom, **how many repair orders** the lane holds and their **total estimate value**.
- Cards are sorted with the **oldest job first** (most days in the shop), so the car that has been waiting longest is on top.
- A lane with a dashed empty box simply has nothing in it.
- If a lane has more cards than were loaded, choose **Load more** at the bottom of the board.

Scroll sideways with your mouse or trackpad, or press <Key>←</Key> and <Key>→</Key> to step one lane at a time. <Key>↑</Key> and <Key>↓</Key> scroll the page.

### Reading a Card

<BoardCardExample />

*An example card from the Repairs board. All names and numbers are made up.*

1. **Repair order number**, in a monospaced font so numbers line up.
2. **Pre-repair scan icon** (Repairs board only). Filled green means the pre-repair scan is done. An empty gray icon means it is not. If a car has reached **Disassembly & Repair Planning** without a scan, the empty icon pulses gently as a reminder. Hover it to see how it was recorded (CCC ONE, a Pre-repair Scan file, or a text) and the date. See [Pre-repair scan](/repair-orders/facts#pre-repair-scan).
3. **Client name**, with their initials in a circle.
4. **Vehicle** (year, make, model), with the manufacturer's logo.
5. **Insurer** paying for the repair. A dash means none is recorded.
6. **Days in shop, with days in this status in brackets.** `12 (3)` means 12 days since arrival and 3 days in the current status.
7. **Estimate total, with payments applied in brackets.** `$4,200.00 (–$500.00)` means a $4,200 estimate with $500 received so far.
8. **Client advocate**: the person responsible for the client. **Unassigned** if none.
9. **Warning icon** (Repairs board only). A red octagon means the job is missing paperwork. Hover to see which: **Missing signed work authorization**, **Missing initial estimate**, or both. It disappears when both are in place.

On the other boards the bottom-right corner shows a date instead of the warning: the **appointment date** on Opportunities, the **repairs-complete date** on Close & Deliver, and the **target date** on Enrichment.

A **Won** or **Lost** tag appears at the top right of a card on Opportunities and Close & Deliver when the job's outcome is decided.

### Moving a Repair Order

You can move a card to another status **on the same board** in two ways:

- **Drag it.** Pick up the card, and drop it on a lane. The lane highlights as you hover. The card fades while you carry it.
- **Use the card's menu.** Hover over a card and a small **⋮** appears at the top right (it is always visible on a touch screen). Choose it, and under **Move to**, choose a status. This is the keyboard and touch way to do exactly what dragging does.

<Warning>
Moving a card is a **real change** to the repair order and it is recorded in the activity history with your name. It is not a draft. If you are not sure, open the repair order first.
</Warning>

What you cannot do:

- Drag a card to a lane on a **different board**. CCC ONE moves cars between boards.
- Drop a card on the lane it is already in. That does nothing.

If CCC ONE later reports a different production stage, it can move the card again. The latest word from CCC ONE wins.

### The Production Load Line

On the Repairs board, a small group of lanes (from the first production status onward) shows a warning at the top when the shop is overbooked:

- **A quiet board stays quiet.** While the shop is within its capacity, nothing extra shows.
- A **red ring** around the group and one or more red lines such as **Overbooked · Refinish: 41 of 40 daily hours** means the shop has more work booked than it can process. The period (daily, weekly, or monthly) is the one your admin chose. Hover the red text to see the **Total**, **Body + Glass**, **Structural**, and **Refinish** figures. The same warning appears at the top of [Today](/work/today).

These figures are *estimate hours the shop can process*, not hours anyone has worked. See [Production capacity](/admin/production).

### Loading and Errors

While the board loads it shows a pale outline of lanes and cards. If loading fails it shows an error with a retry button, and a message about your connection if you are offline. It never shows an empty board in place of an error.

## Table View

The Table is a dense grid with one row per repair order.

### On a Phone

On a phone the Table shows one tappable row per repair order: the number, client, vehicle and insurer, status, and target date, with the same pager underneath.

### Search

The box at the top left, **Search repair order, client, VIN, claim…**, matches the repair order number, the client's name, the VIN, and the claim number. Results update as you type.

### Filters

Choose **Filters** to open the filter panel. A number on the button shows how many filters are active, and **Clear filters** beside it removes them all. Every filter you set also appears as a removable chip under the button, so you can see and drop one at a time. Filters narrow what you see and combine (every filter you set must match).

On a phone, **Filters** opens a bottom sheet instead. Set what you need, then choose **Show results**. **Clear filters** in the sheet's footer resets everything.

| Filter | What you pick |
| --- | --- |
| **Client Advocate** | One teammate. |
| **Repair Planner** | One teammate. |
| **Technician** | One technician. |
| **Status** | One of this board's statuses. |
| **Severity** | One of the five levels. |
| **Total Loss** | **Any**, **Yes**, or **No**. |
| **Pre-repair Scan** | **Any**, **Yes**, or **No**. Repairs board only. |
| **Released** | **Any**, **Yes**, or **No**: whether the vehicle has been released to the client. |
| **Client Approval** | **Any**, **Yes**, or **No**. |
| **Insurance Approval** | **Any**, **Yes**, or **No**. |
| **Arrival** | A date range (from and to, inclusive). |
| **Target** | A date range. |
| **Days Here** | A two-handled slider from 0 to 120. The top end means "any" (shown as 120+). The number applies when you let go. |

<Note>
Filters apply to the **Table** and **Board** views. Search applies to the Table. The Timeline shows the whole board.
</Note>

### Columns

Choose **Columns** to show or hide columns. **RO** and **Client** always stay. The menu has a switch for each of:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **RO** | The repair order number (pinned on the left). |
| **Client** | Name, with an avatar (optional). |
| **Vehicle** | Year, make, and model, with the maker's logo (optional). |
| **VIN** | The vehicle identification number. |
| **Insurer** | The insurer paying. |
| **Claim number** | The claim number. |
| **Estimate** | The current estimate amount. |
| **Received** | Payments received so far, from the ARW. |
| **Status** | The status pill. |
| **Team** | Avatars for the whole team on the job. |
| **Client advocate**, **Repair planner**, **Technician** | Each person by name. Hidden until you turn them on. |
| **Days here** | Days since arrival. |
| **Arrival** | Arrival date. |
| **Target** | The promised date. |
| **Appointment** | The appointment date. |
| **Total loss** | Whether it is flagged a total loss. |
| **Released** | Whether the vehicle was released. |
| **Client approval**, **Insurance approval** | Whether each approval is recorded. |
| **Severity** | The 1 to 5 meter. |
| **Last communication** | When the client was last texted. |

Two more switches under **Avatars** hide or show the **Client avatar** and **Vehicle avatar**.

**Your column choices and widths are saved in this browser, on this device.** Drag a column edge to resize it. If you use a different computer, you will set them again.

### Sorting and Paging

Click a column heading to sort ascending, again for descending. Sorting happens across the whole board, not just the visible rows. Use **Previous** and **Next** at the bottom to move between pages. The table shows the total count, which comes from the server so it stays honest when you filter.

If nothing matches, the table says **No matching repair orders** and suggests **Try another search or clear the filters.** If the board is truly empty it says **No repair orders yet**, with the reminder that **Repair orders arrive from CCC ONE.**

## Timeline View

The Timeline is the production planning board: every open repair order laid out on a calendar, grouped into lanes.

- **Phases** or **Technicians**, at the top left, chooses the lanes: one per status, or one per technician (with **Unassigned** for jobs with nobody). Each lane keeps its own priority order. Click a lane header, or press <Key>Shift</Key> <Key>O</Key>, to fold or open lanes.
- Each row pins its **vehicle logo** and **repair order number**, its **indicators**, its age (for example `30 (5)`: days here, then days in this status), and its **target date**, beside one bar for the car's stay.
- The bar reads like a progress bar. The legend at the top names its three parts: **In shop** (solid, from arrival to today), **To target** (dashed, the days left until the target date), and **Late** (red, the days past it). The bar ends on the days left to the target, and the words after it say how late it is, or that it has no target date. A row with no arrival or start date says **No arrival or start date**.
- Week lines and a soft column for today run behind every row.

Controls at the top: **Week**, **Month**, or **Quarter** to set the scale; the **Earlier** and **Later** arrows; and **Today** to scroll back to the current date. Scroll sideways with a trackpad, <Key>Shift</Key> and the mouse wheel, or the <Key>←</Key> and <Key>→</Key> keys; the view settles on whole days (whole weeks on **Quarter**).

Click a row to open its drawer. Right-click a row for **New task**, **Add note**, **Send message**, and (for owners and admins) **Technician**, to dispatch it to someone. Owners and admins can also drag a row by its grip to reorder it within its lane or move it to another technician. Priorities are queue positions only; they never change the job's status or dates.

If nothing is in production, the Timeline says **No repair orders in production**: *Open repairs appear here as they move through the shop.*

## Refresh Data

The circular-arrow **Refresh data** button reloads everything on the screen. Use it if a teammate told you they changed something and you do not see it yet.
