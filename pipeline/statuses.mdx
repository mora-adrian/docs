A **status** is the one-line answer to "where is this job?" Every repair order has exactly one. This page lists them all, board by board, in the order they appear.

<Info>
**Your shop's labels may differ slightly.** The statuses below are the standard set. Their names can be adjusted for a shop, and your administrator cannot rename them from Settings. If a name on your screen reads a little differently, the meaning is the same. To change names or order, contact Kinetic support.
</Info>

## Reading a status

Each status shows as a pill with a colored dot. The dot has four meanings.

<ToneLegend />

On a card, a number in parentheses beside the status, such as **Paint (3)**, is the number of **days the job has been in that status**.

<Warning>
**A vehicle past its target date shows a red dot, whatever its status.** A late car looks late even if it is sitting in a green status.
</Warning>

## How statuses change

| Who or what | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| **CCC ONE** | Moves a repair order between boards and, on the Repairs board, usually sets the status from CCC ONE's own production stage. It is the authority for everything it sends. |
| **You** | Move a card to another status **on the same board** by dragging it, or with the card's **Move to** menu. |
| **Kinetic itself** | Cancels a lead whose appointment passed without the car arriving. |

If CCC ONE sends a status Kinetic has no rule for, Kinetic keeps the job where it was and records an event on the repair order's **Activity** tab, titled **CCC status unmapped** followed by the status. It does not guess.

A car can also move *backward*: if someone unchecks "vehicle arrived" in CCC ONE for a car that is in production, Kinetic moves the card back to **Scheduled (Won)** on Opportunities.

<Note>
Because CCC ONE is the authority, a status you set by hand on the Repairs board can be changed again the next time CCC ONE reports a different production stage. If you disagree with what CCC ONE says, fix it in CCC ONE.
</Note>

## Opportunities

These are jobs that have not arrived yet. Kinetic reads the information CCC ONE has, and puts the lead in the status that shows what is missing.

To count as **complete**, the record needs: customer name (or company name), email, phone, and address, **and** the vehicle's VIN, year, make, model, and exterior color.

| Status | Tone | Meaning | How it gets here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Incomplete Lead** | <Pill tone="needs">needs us</Pill> | Something is missing from the customer or vehicle details, and there is no appointment yet. | CCC ONE data is missing any of the items above and no appointment or future arrival date exists. |
| **Unscheduled Lead** | <Pill tone="needs">needs us</Pill> | The details are complete, but no appointment is booked. | Complete details, no appointment. |
| **Evaluation** | <Pill tone="needs">needs us</Pill> | The lead is being evaluated. | Moved here by your team. |
| **Incomplete Appointment** | <Pill tone="needs">needs us</Pill> | An appointment exists, but details are still missing. | Missing details **and** an appointment or future arrival date. |
| **Scheduled (Won)** | <Pill tone="needs">needs us</Pill> | The details are complete **and** the customer is booked. The job is marked **Won**. | Complete details and an appointment or future arrival date. |
| **Cancel (Lost)** | <Pill tone="needs">needs us</Pill> | The customer did not come. The job is marked **Lost**. | Every appointment or arrival date has passed and the car never arrived. Kinetic moves it automatically. |

Every new CCC ONE update re-checks the rules, so adding a missing email in CCC ONE moves a lead forward without you touching Kinetic.

<Tip>
**A lead stuck in Incomplete Lead or Incomplete Appointment?** Open its drawer, look at the customer and vehicle, and see what is blank. Fix it in CCC ONE and the status updates itself. See [Playbook: lead to scheduled](/playbooks/lead-to-scheduled).
</Tip>

## Repairs

The default status when a car arrives is **Arrival & Triage**. After that, the list below is the usual order. Vehicles can skip or revisit statuses.

| Status | Tone | Meaning | CCC ONE production stages that put a car here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Arrival & Triage** | <Pill tone="needs">needs us</Pill> | The car is here and is being checked in, scanned, and authorized. | Check-in, Customer Hold, Insurance Hold, Preorder, Preorder Parts, Prescan, Waiting Authorization |
| **Dispatch Queue** | <Pill tone="needs">needs us</Pill> | Ready to be assigned to a technician or department. | Ready for Dispatch |
| **Disassembly & Repair Planning** | <Pill tone="needs">needs us</Pill> | The car is being taken apart and the repair is being planned. The first scan is due here. | Teardown, Blueprint, Warranty Blueprint |
| **Supplement Hold** | <Pill tone="waiting">waiting</Pill> | Waiting for the insurer or customer to approve extra work, or a possible total loss is being decided. | Waiting Insurance Approval, Waiting Customer Approval, Possible Total Loss, Total Loss: Awaiting Release, Total Loss: Waiting Pickup |
| **Parts Hold** | <Pill tone="waiting">waiting</Pill> | Waiting for parts to arrive. | Waiting on Parts, Backorder Part Hold, Reassembly Parts Waiting, Backorder: Reassembly Parts, Warranty Waiting on Parts |
| **Body** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | Body repair work. | Body, BFM, Waiting Tech, Warranty Body |
| **Structural** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | Frame and structural work. | Structural, Frame |
| **Mechanical** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | Mechanical work. | Mechanical |
| **Paint** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | Painting. | Paint Prep, Paint, Warranty Paint |
| **Reassembly** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | Putting the car back together. | Reassembly, Warranty Reassembly |
| **Wheels, Tires & Alignment** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | Wheels, tires, and alignment. | *Moved by your team.* |
| **Calibrations & Diagnostics** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | Sensor calibration and post-repair scans. | Calibration, Post Scan, Warranty Calibration |
| **Denib, Polish & Coatings** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | Final paint finishing. | Polish, Denib-Polish-Coatings |
| **Sublets** | <Pill tone="waiting">waiting</Pill> | The car is with an outside vendor. | Sublet, Sublet Glass, Sublet Offsite, Sublet Onsite |
| **Detail** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | Final cleaning. | Detail, Warranty Detail |
| **Repairs Complete** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | Work is finished. The vehicle is flagged **ready for customer pickup**. | Repair Completed |

CCC ONE also has **Quality Control** stages (Quality Control, Waiting Final Insurance Review, Final PPW, Warranty QC). Kinetic has no status of its own for them. When one arrives and nothing else maps, the car **stays in the status it is in**.

### The Possible Total Loss lane

**Supplement Hold** is drawn as two lanes on the Repairs board:

- **Supplement Hold** holds vehicles that are expected to be repaired.
- **Possible Total Loss** sits right beside it and holds vehicles that are heading toward being declared a total loss.

The second lane is not a status you can drop a card into. A vehicle appears there automatically when any of these is true: you marked it **Total loss** under **Repairability**, CCC ONE classified it as a total loss, or its CCC ONE stage mentions a total loss. Hover over the lane title and it reminds you: **Possible total losses follow the record's Repairability and CCC ONE.** See [Total loss](/playbooks/total-loss).

### Why the order matters

Kinetic treats **Body and everything after it** as *production*. Statuses before that (arrival, planning, supplement, and parts holds) are the *pre-production* part of the repair. This split drives the **Active Repairs**, **Incoming Revenue**, and **In-Process Revenue** numbers, and the production capacity warnings. See [The four boards](/pipeline/overview#repairs).

## Close & Deliver

| Status | Tone | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Repairs Complete** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | The default. CCC ONE has recorded the repairs as complete. The car is flagged **ready for customer pickup**. |
| **Tow Scheduled** | <Pill tone="waiting">waiting</Pill> | A tow-truck delivery is planned. Use it with a **Concierge out** appointment. |
| **Experience Scheduled** | <Pill tone="waiting">waiting</Pill> | An in-person pickup is planned. Use it with an **Experience out** appointment. |
| **Interview** | <Pill tone="waiting">waiting</Pill> | The hand-over conversation or inspection with the customer. |
| **Corrections** | <Pill tone="late">late</Pill> | Something needs to be fixed before the customer takes the car. |
| **Closed Won** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | The job is closed successfully. |
| **Closed Lost** | <Pill tone="late">late</Pill> | The job is closed without a successful outcome. |

Your team moves cars among these statuses by hand. Your shop may use **Interview** and **Corrections** slightly differently, so confirm the convention with your manager.

## Enrichment

| Status | Tone | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Audit** | <Pill tone="needs">needs us</Pill> | The default. The car has been delivered and the job record is waiting for review. |
| **Enrich** | <Pill tone="needs">needs us</Pill> | The record is being completed with missing details. |
| **Archive** | <Pill tone="ontrack">on track</Pill> | The job is finished and filed away. |

CCC ONE sends the job here when it records the vehicle as **delivered**. When CCC ONE records the job as **closed**, it is filed as finished (Won). A job CCC ONE records as cancelled, void, or a total loss is filed as Lost.

## Won and Lost

Some cards carry a **Won** or **Lost** tag. It is a separate fact from the status:

- On **Opportunities**, **Scheduled (Won)** jobs show **Won**; **Cancel (Lost)** jobs show **Lost**.
- On **Close & Deliver** and **Enrichment**, the tag shows the final result.
- On **Repairs** there is no tag, because the outcome is not decided yet.

A **Lost** job's progress bar turns red entirely.

## When a job has no status

Occasionally a repair order arrives that Kinetic cannot place. It appears in a lane called **No status** at the end of the board. Open it, check what CCC ONE has sent, and ask your admin if it stays there.
