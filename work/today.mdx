**Today** answers one question: *which of my jobs need me right now?* It is the screen to open first each morning and to come back to whenever you have a free minute.

## Who Sees What

Today is personal. It lists only the repair orders where **you** are the **repair planner**, the **client advocate**, or both. A teammate's Today shows their jobs, not yours.

Technicians see a different Today: a **MET** technician gets **Your work order**, their own assigned jobs in priority order; a **Paint** or **Structural** technician gets their department's queue. The header counts what needs attention (past target, over budget, on supplement or parts hold). If your technician specialty isn't set, Today says **Your technician view needs setup**.

<Warning>
Today relies on your user being linked to an **employee profile** in your shop. Without the link, Today says **Today isn't set up for you yet** and asks you to contact your shop administrator. Kinetic cannot tell which jobs are yours until the link exists.
</Warning>

Today leaves out archived repair orders and any job whose outcome was marked **Lost**.

## What Is on the Screen

The header shows today's date and your name. When you have tasks due today, a meter beside it, **Your tasks due today**, shows how many you have finished. Below are five sections. Each has a gray number beside its title showing the **true total**, even though only the first rows are drawn. When none of them has anything, Today says **All caught up**: *Nothing on your repair orders needs you right now.*

If your shop has gone over its production capacity, a red banner appears above the sections. See [Production capacity](/admin/production).

### The Five Sections

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Complete but not delivered">
    **What lands here:** a vehicle whose repairs are complete (or that is in a pickup-ready status) and has not been delivered. Longest waiting first.

    **The row says:** `Complete · 4 days awaiting pickup`.

    **Why it matters:** the car is done and the client is waiting. Finish the paperwork and schedule the pickup.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Near estimated completion date">
    **What lands here:** a vehicle that has not been delivered and has a **target date** within the next few days, **or a target date that has already passed**. Soonest first.

    **The row says:** `Target Oct 23` or `Target passed Oct 21`.

    **Why it matters:** these are the jobs to push over the line before the promised date.

    **Default window:** 3 days. Admins can change it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Review amounts owed">
    **What lands here:** a vehicle that has reached the money gate status (or any status after it, or Close & Deliver without delivery) and **no payment has been recorded** on its [ARW](/repair-orders/arw).

    **The row says:** `No payment received · in Parts Hold`.

    **Why it matters:** a job that is far along with no money in is a risk to the shop. Check the deductible and the insurer's payment.

    **Default gate:** **Parts Hold**. Admins can pick a different status.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Follow up on stale supplements">
    **What lands here:** a vehicle that has been in **Supplement Hold** for at least a few days.

    **The row says:** `3 days in supplement hold`.

    **Why it matters:** while the shop waits on an insurer to approve extra work, the car sits and the client's wait grows. Chase the adjuster. See [Supplements](/playbooks/supplements).

    **Default wait:** 3 days. Admins can change it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Follow up on critical documents">
    **What lands here:** a vehicle being repaired that arrived at least a day ago and is still missing a **signed work authorization**, an **initial estimate** file, or both.

    **The row says:** `Missing signed work authorization and initial estimate`.

    **Why it matters:** repairs should not run without the client's written authorization, and the initial estimate backs up everything that follows.

    **Default wait:** 1 day after arrival. Admins can change it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Reading a Row

Each row is one repair order:

- The **repair order number** in an outlined badge, then the client's name and the vehicle.
- A second line: the current **status**, a dot, then the reason it surfaced.
- On the right, a red <Tag>Past target</Tag> badge if the target date has passed.

Click anywhere on a row to open the repair order's [drawer](/repair-orders/drawer). From there choose **Open full screen** to act.

A job can appear in more than one section. A car with no payment that is also near its target date is in both.

## What to Do with Each Section

| Section | A good first move |
| --- | --- |
| Complete but not delivered | Check the **ARW** for what is due at pickup, the **Files** tab for the signed paperwork, and **Calendar** for the pickup appointment. |
| Near estimated completion date | Open the repair order. Is the target date still believable? If not, tell the client now. |
| Review amounts owed | Check the **ARW** tab. Is the deductible collected? Is an insurer payment in transit? Add what is known. |
| Follow up on stale supplements | Open the **Emails** tab. Has the supplement gone to the adjuster? Is **Auto follow-up** on? Call if it has been silent. |
| Follow up on critical documents | Open the **Files** tab and the **Signatures** tab. Send a work authorization for signature, or upload the initial estimate and tag it **Initial Estimate**. |

## Make the Thresholds Fit Your Shop

The limits behind Today (how soon counts as "near" a target date, where the money gate sits, and how many days supplements or paperwork may wait) belong to the shop. An admin sets them in **Settings → Repair orders → Today page**. See [Repair order settings](/admin/repair-order-settings#today-page).

## Good to Know

- Today is read when you open it. If you think it is out of date, reload the page.
- Today is a list of *exceptions*. A job that is fine does not appear, so an empty Today is good news, not a bug (as long as your profile is linked).
- Today shows only jobs assigned to you. To see everything, use [the boards](/pipeline/overview).

## Do and Don't

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Open Today first thing and again after lunch. Clear a row by fixing the cause, not by hiding the job.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't move a job to a different status just to make it leave Today. The list reflects the facts; fix the facts.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## If Something Looks Wrong

| Problem | Cause and fix |
| --- | --- |
| Today is empty but you have jobs | Your user may not be linked to an employee profile. Ask an admin. |
| A job you own is missing | Make sure *you* are the planner or advocate on the repair order. Those seats come from CCC ONE and the shop's [advocate rules](/admin/repair-order-settings#client-advocate-rules). |
| **Your work list could not be loaded** | A temporary problem. Choose the retry button. If it keeps happening, use **Report a problem**. |
