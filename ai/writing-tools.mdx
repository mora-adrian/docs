Kinetic has small helpers built into the places you type. They all follow one rule: **they change the draft box, never the record.** Nothing is sent or saved until you do it yourself.

## Talk to text (dictation)

The **microphone** button turns your voice into typed text. It appears in the Activity note box, the Texts and Email composers, and the task **Details** field.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press the microphone">
    Your browser may ask permission to use the microphone the first time. Allow it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Speak">
    The button shows that it is listening.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press it again to stop">
    The button shows that it is working, then the text is **added to the end of your draft**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- A dictation can run up to **2 minutes**; Kinetic stops a forgotten recording.
- Kinetic cleans up the recording and the punctuation. Your browser also reduces background noise, which helps on a loud shop floor.
- If it fails you see **Dictation is not available right now**. Type instead and try again later.
- Speak numbers clearly and **check** claim numbers, amounts, and names in the result.

## Improve with AI

In the **Texts** composer, the magic-wand button rewrites your draft to be clearer and more professional. It keeps your meaning and facts. See [Texting](/communicate/texting#improve-with-ai) for what it will and will not touch.

## Explain estimate

On the **Estimate** tab, **Explain estimate** opens a sheet titled **Your repair estimate explained**. It writes a plain-language version of the estimate for the customer:

- the **Grand total**,
- **The bigger items** and what they are,
- **Repair or replace?**, and
- **A note on paint blending**.

You can **Regenerate** it, or **Email to customer** to send it to the primary customer (**Email this explanation to primary customer**). If the estimate has changed since the explanation was written, the sheet warns you; regenerate before sending.

<Warning>
The explanation is a **customer-friendly summary**, not a promise of coverage or price. Read it before you email it.
</Warning>
