The **Agent** is a chat assistant that knows your shop's repair orders. Open it from **Operations → Agent** in the sidebar. The page is titled **Ask about your data**.

Think of it as a colleague who can look anything up instantly. It **reads**; it does not change records. It cites the repair orders it used, so you can click through and confirm.

## Ask a Question

Type in the box (**What would you like to know about a repair?**) and press <Key>Enter</Key> or **Send question**. Messages can be up to **4,000 characters**.

Good questions are specific:

- *Which repair orders are currently in paint?*
- *What did the customer on repair order 4821 last text us about?*
- *Which files are attached to a repair order?*
- *Find a vehicle by its VIN.*

The first three are the suggestions Kinetic shows on an empty screen. Click one to try it.

### Reading Answers

Answers cite the repair orders they used as small badges. Click one to preview the repair order beside the chat without leaving it: **Loading repair order…**, then the claim, insurer, outcome likelihood, days on site, target, the customer and vehicle, and **Recent activity**. The preview is read-only. Choose **Open full screen** in it to go to the full repair order, and **Close preview** to hide it. If a record cannot be found you see **This repair order could not be found.**

While it works you see **Searching records…**.

<Tip>
**Name the repair order.** "What's the status of the Hendricks Camry?" works, but "repair order 4821" is faster and unambiguous. If more than one match exists, the Agent offers **chips** to pick the right repair order.
</Tip>

## Have It Draft a Text

Say what you want to send: *Text the customer on 4821 that the parts arrived and we'll start Monday.*

The Agent writes the message and shows it as a **draft card** with two buttons: **Send** and **Discard**. Nothing is sent until you choose **Send**. You can also reply in plain words: **send it** sends, **cancel** cancels.

<Warning>
The draft goes out from the shop's number to the real customer. Read the name, the date, and the promise before you choose **Send**.
</Warning>

## Conversations

- **New conversation** (the button at the top) starts fresh. Use it when you change topic; the Agent otherwise remembers what you said earlier in the conversation.
- Reopening the page restores your last conversation (**Restoring conversation…**).
- If an answer fails, a retry option appears so you do not have to retype the question.

## What It Can and Can't Do

| It can | It can't |
| --- | --- |
| Find repair orders, people, companies, and vehicles | Create or edit records |
| Summarize a repair order, its texts, notes, and files | Decide who is at fault or what insurance owes |
| Compare, count, and list across jobs | Promise a delivery date |
| Draft a customer text for your approval | Send anything without your **Send** |
| Answer insurance-rule questions using documents your admin uploaded | Replace reading the rule yourself |

It works with **your** permissions. It cannot show you a shop's data that you cannot already see.

## Do and Don't

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Ask follow-ups ("and which of those are waiting on the insurer?"). Click through to confirm anything you will act on. Start a **New conversation** for a new topic.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't treat an answer about money or dates as final without opening the repair order. 
  </Card>
</Columns>
