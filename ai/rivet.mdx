**Rivet** is Lever's estimate reviewer. An estimate is the itemized list of repairs, parts, and labor prepared for a damaged car. Estimates sometimes leave things out: a sensor that must be recalibrated after a bumper is replaced, a seam sealer, a scan. Left out, those become problems later, or unpaid work.

Rivet reads the estimate and the damage photos, compares them with repair knowledge, and lists **operations the estimate may be missing**. It suggests. A person decides.

<Note>
Rivet never edits the estimate and never talks to the insurer. It produces a list of things to **consider**, each with its reasoning.
</Note>

## Which Repair Orders It Can Review

All of these must be true:

- The repair order is **not an opportunity** (it has moved past the first status).
- It is **not archived**.
- It is **not a total loss**.
- The estimate total is **over $300**.

If a repair order does not qualify, the **Audit with Rivet** button is unavailable.

## Run a Review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Estimate tab">
    On the repair order, open **Estimate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Audit with Rivet">
    The review is queued, then runs. The status reads **Review queued**, then **Review running**. It takes **several minutes**. You can leave the page; the results wait for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the results">
    When it finishes the button changes to **See audit results**. Rivet analyzes up to **24 photos** from the repair order's files, so upload the damage photos first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An email goes to the repair planner, with the person who requested the review on copy.

<Tip>
Run Rivet **after the estimate and photos are final** and **again after a supplement**. If the estimate has a newer revision than the one reviewed, the results show **Needs re-review**.
</Tip>

## Reading the Results

Findings are grouped by **operation group** or **section** of the estimate, such as front bumper, glass, or electrical. Inside a group they are ordered by how likely they are to be needed:

| Tier | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Almost always** | Nearly every repair like this needs it. Check first. |
| **Usually** | Needed in most cases. |
| **Consider** | Worth a look; depends on the car or the damage. |

Each finding has a **title**, the **reasoning**, and a **confidence** tag showing how sure Rivet is. Click a title or the reasoning to **copy** it, for example into a supplement request.

### Move Around

A **Sections** rail lists the groups. Click one to jump. Press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd> to collapse or expand all sections at once.

### Record a Verdict

Every finding should get a verdict. That is how the shop and Rivet learn, and how the team knows what has been handled.

| Control | Verdict | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thumbs up | **Added** | You added the operation to the estimate (or will, in a supplement). |
| Thumbs down, then **Not applicable** | Not applicable | It does not apply to this car or damage. A **note** is required. |
| Thumbs down, then **Already on estimate** | Already on the estimate | It is there under another description. A **note** is required. |
| Thumbs down, then **Duplicate** | Duplicate | Rivet listed the same thing twice. |

Reviewed findings move into a **Reviewed** group so the open ones stay on top.

### Ask AI About a Finding

**Ask AI** opens the [Agent](/ai/agent) with a question about that finding already typed. Use it when you want more explanation, or want it checked against the repair order's notes.

## The Estimates List

Open **Rivet** from the sidebar (**Operations → Rivet**) to see every estimate and its review state. Filter by status:

| Filter | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Action needed** | Reviewed, with findings still waiting for a verdict. |
| **Not audited** | No review yet. |
| **Review queued** | Waiting to start. |
| **Review running** | In progress. |
| **Audited** | Reviewed and handled. |
| **Review failed** | The review could not finish. Run it again. |

## Do and Don't

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Upload clear damage photos first. Record a verdict on every finding. Run Rivet again after a supplement. Write a useful note when you reject a finding.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't add an operation just because Rivet listed it. Don't forward a finding to an insurer without checking it. Don't ignore **Needs re-review**.
  </Card>
</Columns>
