title: "Text Kinetic"
description: "Staff can text Kinetic in a team chat to answer questions, add notes, attach photos, notify a coworker, or mark a first scan done."
---

You do not have to open the app to get small jobs done. In a **team chat** (a group text on the shop's texting line that includes Kinetic), staff can **mention Kinetic by name** and ask for help from their phone.

Your admin sets team chats up. See [Messaging settings](/admin/messaging-settings).

## What You Can Ask

| You want to | Say something like |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask a question** | "Kinetic, what's the status of the Hendricks Camry?" |
| **Add a note to a vehicle's file** | "Kinetic, note on the Camry: bumper cover arrived, one clip broken." |
| **Attach photos or files** | Send the photos in the chat and say which vehicle they belong to. |
| **Notify a coworker** | "Kinetic, tell Dana the Camry is ready for paint." |
| **Mark the first scan done** | "Kinetic, first scan done on the Camry." |

## Rules It Follows

- **One instruction per message.** If you ask for several things, it does the first and tells you there was more.
- **Keep it short.** Messages can be up to **1,500 characters**.
- **It uses your permissions.** It does what you could do in the app, as you. It does not do anything you are not allowed to.
- **Staff only.** It acts on messages from employees whose phone number is linked to their account.
- **If it is not sure which vehicle you mean,** it asks you to confirm from a short list. Answer to continue.
- **It stays silent if a customer or other outsider is in the chat.** Notes and file changes only run from iMessage.

## The Two Assistant Modes

When an admin creates a team chat they choose how Kinetic behaves:

| Mode | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Listen only** | Kinetic stores the conversation but never replies. |
| **Replies when addressed** | Kinetic answers only messages that mention it by name. |

## Do and Don't

  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Name the vehicle or repair order every time. Send one request at a time. Check its reply to confirm it understood.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't send it passwords or card numbers. Don't ask it to text a customer; use the repair order's Texts tab or the [Agent](/ai/agent).
  </Card>
