Lever uses AI to **read, summarize, draft, and flag**. It does not make decisions for you. It never decides coverage, liability, or what to promise a customer about delivery. A person at the shop owns every judgment call.

<Warning>
**Read before you send.** AI can be wrong. Anything an AI drafts that will be seen by a customer, an adjuster, or the file (a text, an email, a note) is yours once you press send. Check names, dates, dollar amounts, and promises.
</Warning>

## Where AI Appears

| Feature | What it does | Does it act on its own? | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Rivet** | Reviews an estimate and its photos against the shop's repair knowledge and lists operations that may be missing. | No. You run it, you decide on every finding. | [Rivet](/ai/rivet) |
| **Agent** | Answers questions about your repair orders in plain language and drafts texts for you to approve. | No. It only reads, and sends nothing until you press **Send**. | [Agent](/ai/agent) |
| **Improve with AI** and **dictation** | Tidies a draft; turns your voice into text. | No. It changes the draft box only. | [Writing and dictation tools](/ai/writing-tools) |
| **Explain estimate** | Writes a plain-language explanation of an estimate for the customer. | No. You choose whether to email it. | [Estimate](/repair-orders/estimate) |
| **Texting Lever** | Lets staff text Lever in a team chat to ask or do small tasks. | Only what the sender asks for, with the sender's own permissions. | [Text Lever](/ai/text-lever) |
| **Auto-reply and receptionist** | Answers inbound texts and calls from unknown contacts using your shop's information. | Yes, when your admin turns it on. | [Calls](/communicate/calls) and [Messaging settings](/admin/messaging-settings) |
| **Adjuster follow-ups** | Writes and sends reminders to an adjuster while a supplement is waiting. | Yes, every 2 business days, up to 10 times. | [Messaging settings](/admin/messaging-settings#follow-up-activity) |

## What AI Never Does

- It **never decides coverage, liability, or fault**.
- It **never commits the shop to a delivery date**. Target dates in Lever are calculated from rules, not promised.
- It **never approves a supplement or a payment**.
- The Agent **never changes a record**. It reads.
- Rivet **never edits an estimate**. It only reports what to consider.

## Good Habits

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Treat AI output as a first draft from a capable assistant. Verify numbers against the estimate. Tell the AI exactly what you need. Give feedback on Rivet findings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't paste passwords or payment card numbers into any AI box. Don't send an AI draft without reading it. Don't use AI to avoid a hard phone call.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Cost and Limits

AI usage is metered. Admins see monthly spend by feature on the **AI usage** settings page. See [AI usage](/admin/ai-usage).
