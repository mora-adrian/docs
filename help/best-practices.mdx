## Daily rhythm

1. Start with the [daily sweep](/playbooks/daily-sweep).
2. Work Today from the top, then Tasks.
3. Answer every text before lunch and again before close.
4. End the day by clearing notifications and adding tomorrow's tasks.

## Weekly review (30 minutes, same time every week)

- Open the [weekly reports](/admin/reports). Add a task for each row.
- Review the Repairs board for any car in one status more than a week.
- Read the Responded section of [CSI](/csi/overview).
- Check [Rivet](/ai/rivet) for **Action needed** estimates.
- Look at the KPI strip against targets.

## Writing good notes

A good note answers *who, what, when, and next*.

> Called Ms. Rivera at 2:10. She approved supplement 2 (+$840, rear body panel). Parts ETA Thursday. Told her target is Monday. Next: confirm paint code with the paint department.

## Keep the data clean

- Correct VINs, emails, and mobile numbers the first time. Missing mobile numbers are the main reason surveys do not send.
- One record per person and per company.
- Tag files correctly so that documents are found, and Today's **Missing documents** is accurate.
- Archive, don't delete.

## Customer communication

- Update before they ask.
- Be specific: what happened, what happens next, and when.
- Keep texts short. Use templates for routine updates and edit the details.
- Move complex or emotional conversations to a phone call.

## Use the small features

People who use the small features get the most out of Kinetic:

- [Command palette](/start/tour#the-command-palette) for instant lookup
- Dictation for notes
- [Improve with AI](/ai/writing-tools) for tone
- [Send to adjuster](/repair-orders/files) for supplements
- The pinned photo on each repair order
- Task due dates and assignments
