<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    - Fix data in **CCC ONE**, not around it.
    - Record every call as a **note**.
    - Upload photos and scans the **same day**.
    - Reply to customers from the **Texts tab**.
    - Send documents with **Send via text**, not by email.
    - Create a **task** with an owner and a date for every promise.
    - Record payments when they **land**.
    - Check [target dates](/repair-orders/facts#target-date) after a supplement.
    - Read what the AI wrote **before** you send.
    - Close the loop on every negative survey **the same day**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    - Don't use placeholder VINs, emails, or phone numbers.
    - Don't tell a customer a date the system only estimates.
    - Don't share customer links or your login.
    - Don't type over a **CCC ONE**-owned field.
    - Don't leave a waiting job without a next step.
    - Don't enter an insurer payment as **Customer**.
    - Don't release a car with money **Due at pickup**.
    - Don't ignore a **Chat health** warning.
    - Don't text the customer after they said STOP.
    - Don't delete an old record: archive it.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Why these matter most

- **Fix data at its source.** Kinetic mirrors CCC ONE. A hand-fix in Kinetic is overwritten by the next update, and the confusion costs you more than the fix.
- **Write it down.** Teammates cannot see what is only in your head. Notes make the repair order a shared memory.
- **Never promise an estimate.** Kinetic calculates dates from rules. They are good guesses, not commitments.
- **Respect customer consent.** A customer who opts out must not get more texts. Kinetic blocks them for you; don't try to get around it.
