---
title: "Frequently Asked Questions"
description: "Quick answers to the questions new users ask most."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I create a repair order in Kinetic?">
    No. Repair orders come from CCC ONE. Kinetic reads them and adds communication, tasks, and analysis on top.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my edit change back?">
    The field is owned by CCC ONE and was re-sent. Fix it in CCC ONE. See [Facts](/repair-orders/facts).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I rename statuses or add new ones?">
    No. The status names and order are fixed. Contact support if your shop needs a different label.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the AI text my customers by itself?">
    Only where you have turned it on: AI auto-reply, the receptionist, and adjuster follow-ups. Everything else is drafted for you to approve. See [AI in Kinetic](/ai/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who sees my notes?">
    Everyone in the shop who can open the repair order. Customers never see them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I delete a person, company, or vehicle?">
    You can archive it (admins). Records are not deleted so that history stays intact.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I delete a member?">
    No. Suspend them. Their history stays.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I send the same signature request twice?">
    Kinetic recognizes it and delivers the original again. It does not create a second.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do I keep getting asked for a code?">
    Sensitive actions require a fresh authenticator app code. After you confirm, you are not asked again for 240 minutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Kinetic on my phone?">
    Yes. It works in a phone browser, and you can add it to your Home Screen to get push notifications.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do signed documents go?">
    Onto the repair order's **Files** tab, tagged by type.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was no survey sent?">
    The [CSI](/csi/overview#when-a-survey-is-skipped) screen says why. Most often the customer has no mobile number.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do the colored dots mean?">
    Blue needs us, orange waiting, green on track, red late. See [Statuses](/pipeline/statuses#reading-a-status).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm new to collision repair. Where do I start?">
    Read the [collision repair primer](/start/collision-primer) and the [glossary](/start/glossary).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
