---
title: "AI Usage"
description: "See what the shop's AI features cost this month, by day and by feature."
---

Open **Settings → AI usage**. Only admins can see it (otherwise: **Only admins can see AI usage.**).

## What You See

- **Month**, with **Previous month** and **Next month** arrows.
- **Month to date**: the estimated spend so far.
- **Daily AI spend**: a chart with one bar per day.
- **Breakdown**: spend split by provider, model, and feature.

If there was none: **No metered AI calls this month.**

<Info>
Amounts are **estimated from list prices**. Some pass-through costs and taxes are not included. Use the numbers to spot trends, not to reconcile an invoice.
</Info>

## How to Use It

- A sudden spike usually means a feature is being used more, such as many [Rivet](/ai/rivet) reviews on one day.
- Compare month to month after you change a setting, such as turning on [auto-reply](/admin/messaging-settings).
- Share the page with whoever approves software spending.
