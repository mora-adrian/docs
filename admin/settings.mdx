Settings are organized by what they affect. Everything shown is real and persisted — there's no "coming soon" toggle that silently does nothing.

| Section | Who | What it controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Everyone | Your name, photo, email signature, and personal details. |
| **Preferences** | Everyone | Notification choices, display and personal defaults. |
| **Security** | Everyone (own) / Admins (shop) | Your password and authenticator app enrollment, recovery codes; admins see shop security posture. |
| **Members** | Admin+ | Invite people, assign roles, deactivate access. See [Roles & permissions](/getting-started/roles). |
| **Shop** | Admin+ | Shop identity: name, address, contact details shown to customers. |
| **Repair orders** | Admin+ | Statuses, numbering, workflow configuration — the columns on your boards and how CCC statuses map to them. |
| **Production** | Admin+ | Capacities, technician skills, and scheduling constraints the Production view uses. |
| **Messaging** | Admin+ | Text/email channels, sender identity, templates, and communication defaults. |
| **Reports** | Admin+ | Reporting configuration and scheduled outputs. |
| **AI usage** | Admin+ | Visibility into AI feature usage (agent, Rivet reviews, drafts). |

<Warning>
Most admin settings require a fresh authenticator app code (sensitive verification). Have your phone nearby before an admin session.
</Warning>

## Changing a setting safely

1. Change one thing at a time and check the result — especially statuses and messaging.
2. Status/workflow changes affect boards, reports, and CCC mapping at once; coordinate with production before reordering.
3. Destructive actions name the shop and the consequence — read those dialogs; they mean what they say.
