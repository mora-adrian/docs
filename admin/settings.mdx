Settings are organized by what they affect. Every setting shown is real and persists — there are no placeholder toggles that do nothing.

## The sections

| Section | Who can change it | What it controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Profile** | You | Your name, photo, email signature, and personal details. |
| **Preferences** | You | Notification choices, display, and personal defaults. |
| **Security** | You (your account); Admins (shop) | Your password, authenticator app enrollment, and recovery codes; admins see the shop's security posture. |
| **Members** | Admins | Invitations, roles, and deactivated access. See [Roles & permissions](/getting-started/roles). |
| **Shop** | Admins | Shop identity: name, address, and the contact details customers see. |
| **Repair orders** | Admins | Statuses, numbering, and workflow configuration — the columns on your boards and how CCC statuses map to them. |
| **Production** | Admins | Capacities, technician skills, and the scheduling constraints the Production view uses. |
| **Messaging** | Admins | Text and email channels, sender identity, templates, and communication defaults. |
| **Reports** | Admins | Report configuration and scheduled outputs. |
| **AI usage** | Admins | Visibility into AI feature usage: the Agent, Rivet reviews, and drafts. |

<Warning>
Most admin settings require a fresh authenticator app code before you can change them. Keep your phone nearby during an admin session.
</Warning>

## Change settings safely

1. Change one thing at a time, and check the result — especially statuses and messaging.
2. Treat status and workflow changes as production changes: they affect boards, reports, and CCC status mapping at once. Coordinate with your production team before reordering.
3. Read destructive-action dialogs fully. They name the shop and the consequence, and they mean what they say.
