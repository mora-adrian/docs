Settings split into two groups: **personal** settings every member manages for themselves, and **shop** settings restricted to admins (with an authenticator check for sensitive changes). Every setting shown is real and persists.

## Personal

### Preferences

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | What Kinetic alerts you about, and how. |
| Display | Appearance choices, including the UI typeface (Inter by default; Geist and IBM Plex Sans are the alternatives). |
| Mailbox | Connect the email account Kinetic monitors for claim mail — required before the Email review nav item appears. |
| Email signature | The signature appended to email you send from Kinetic. |
| Today / schedule | Personal scheduling and Today-screen preferences. |

### Security

- **Password** — change your password.
- **Authenticator app** — enroll or re-enroll your authenticator app; required on every account.
- **Recovery codes** — generate and store backup codes for lost-device recovery.

### Profile

Your name, photo, and public account details.

## Shop (admin)

### Shop

The shop's identity — name, address, and contact details customers see — plus **business hours**, **reference documents** shared across the team, the optional **contact enrichment** toggle, and the **phone system** connection for call logging and screen pops.

### Members

Invite people, search the member list, assign roles, and deactivate access. See [Roles & permissions](/getting-started/roles) for what each role allows. The last owner cannot be removed or demoted.

### Repair orders

| Section | What it configures |
| --- | --- |
| Numbering | The repair order number sequence. |
| Workflow statuses | The status columns on your boards and their order. |
| CCC mappings | How each CCC ONE status maps to one of your statuses. |
| Estimating defaults | Estimate-related defaults for new work. |
| Advocate rules | Assignment rules for the client-advocate seat. |
| KPI targets | The targets that drive board metrics and reports. |

<Warning>
Status and CCC mapping changes alter boards, reports, and automation at once. Coordinate with production before changing them, and change one thing at a time.
</Warning>

### Production

The **Capacity** table: billable estimate hours each department can process per period (per day, week, or month) — capacity to *process* work, not hours worked. The production columns view and load-leveling recommendations read from these values. Only admins can edit; if another session changed capacities since you loaded, Kinetic asks you to reload before saving.

### Messaging

| Section | What it configures |
| --- | --- |
| Text messaging | The shop's SMS/iMessage line for customer conversations. |
| AI auto-reply | Whether and when the AI replies automatically. |
| Message templates | Reusable message bodies, created and edited per template. |
| Team chats | Staff group-chat channels used for internal coordination. |
| CSI | Survey send rules and scheduling. |
| Follow-up activity | Rules for automated customer follow-ups. |

### Reports

Report configuration and scheduled outputs.

### AI usage

Usage visibility for AI features — breakdowns by **provider**, **model**, and **feature**, month to date and per period. Use it to watch spend and adoption.

## Change settings safely

1. Change one thing at a time and check the result.
2. Read destructive-action dialogs fully — they name the shop and the consequence.
3. Keep your authenticator app handy; most admin saves ask for a fresh code.
