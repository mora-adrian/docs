Open **Settings → Repair orders**. These five sections shape what the team sees on Today, how new jobs are assigned, and how Kinetic estimates dates and revenue.

<Note>
Kinetic does not let you edit statuses or repair order numbers here. Repair orders and their numbers come from CCC ONE, and the status names are fixed. See [Statuses](/pipeline/statuses).
</Note>

## Today page

Controls when rows appear on [Today](/work/today).

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Close to delivery** | 3 days | A job appears when its target date is within this many days. |
| **Supplements waiting** | 3 days | A job in supplement hold appears after this many days. |
| **Missing documents** | 1 day | A job appears this many days after arrival if the signed authorization or initial estimate is missing. |
| **Money gate status** | Parts Hold | The status at or after which a job with no recorded payment appears under **Past the money gate**. |

The maximum for any threshold is 60 days.

## Client advocate rules

When a **new** repair order arrives, Kinetic picks a client advocate for it automatically using these rules.

- Rules are checked **in order**. The first match wins.
- A rule is either **Repair order last digit** (add the digits it covers) or **Vehicle make** (add the makes).
- Each rule names the **assigned employee**.
- Use the arrows to move a rule up or down, or remove it.

<Tip>
Use digits to spread work evenly (even digits to Dana, odd to Chris) and makes for specialists (all Subaru to Pat). Put the specific rules above the general ones.
</Tip>

## CCC ONE employee mapping

CCC ONE sends the names of the estimator and technician on each job. Matching them to your team lets Kinetic show the right person.

- **Unmapped provider names** are listed so you can claim them.
- For each, enter a **display name**, an optional **worker ID**, and choose the **employee** (**Technician** or **Estimator**).

## Estimating and cycle time

These numbers feed [target dates](/repair-orders/facts#target-date), [outcome likelihood](/repair-orders/facts#outcome-likelihood), and revenue figures.

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Average repairable order** | Your typical pre-tax repair total. Used for new leads before an estimate exists. |
| **Average total-loss order** | Your typical total-loss job. |
| **Repairable cycle days** | Days from key-to-key for a repair. Default 12. |
| **Total-loss cycle days** | Days for a total loss. |
| **Labor hours per calendar day** | After supplement hold, the target date becomes arrival plus total labor hours divided by this number. |
| **High confidence within** and **Medium confidence within** | How close the three date estimates (entered, labor-hours, average-cycle) must be for **High** or **Medium** confidence. All three within the High number means High; within the Medium number means Medium; wider means Low. Applies once the job is past Parts Hold. |
| **Outcome likelihood boundaries** | Three ascending percentages (1 to 99) of vehicle value. Default 25, 50, 75. Level 1 is under the first; level 4 is at or above the last. |

The footer shows the **default new-lead estimate** Kinetic will use.

## Repair KPI targets

These targets show as deltas on the KPI strip of the boards.

- **Cars onsite target**
- **Cars arrived per day**
- **Cars delivered per day**
- **In-process revenue target**
- **Incoming revenue target**

<Warning>
Set realistic targets. Targets far above what the shop can do make every number look red and train people to ignore the strip.
</Warning>
