Kinetic connects to the systems a collision center already uses. Admins manage connections in Settings. Every integration reports real connection health, so you see failures — not a fake "connected" badge.

## Estimating

- **CCC ONE** — the estimating system of record. CCC events update repair order fields automatically, because CCC is authoritative for every field it supplies. CCC statuses map to your shop's statuses through admin configuration. Estimates flow from CCC into Rivet for AI review.

## Communications

- **Text messaging** — the shop's shared texting line for customer conversations.
- **Email** — outbound email with delivery tracking, plus a mailbox monitor that watches inbound mail for claim-related messages.
- **Voice calls** — recorded calls with transcription.
- **Phone screen pops** — an incoming call on your phone system can open the matching customer record automatically.
- **Dictation** — speech-to-text for composing notes and messages hands-free from the floor.

## Documents and signatures

- **E-signatures** — send authorizations and documents for electronic signature. Signature status tracks back to the repair order.

## Data services

- **Vehicle market value** — market-value ranges that power the expected insurance payout shown on repair orders.
- **Contact enrichment** — optional and admin-gated. It fills only *empty* contact fields and never overwrites something a person entered.

## AI providers

Estimate review, photo analysis, and the Agent's drafting features run on configured AI providers. Track usage in **Settings → AI usage**.

## When an integration fails

Failures surface visibly. A broken connection appears in Settings, and the features that depend on it degrade honestly instead of pretending to work. Reconnect the provider in Settings; queued work and retries resume where they left off. If a failure persists, contact your admin or platform operator.
