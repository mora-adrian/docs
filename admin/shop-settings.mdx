Open **Settings** and choose your shop's name under **Shop**. Only admins can change these.

## Identity

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Logo** | Shown beside the shop's name in the app. |
| **Name** | The shop's name, used in customer messages such as the survey text. |
| **Slug** | A short, unique identifier for the shop. Don't change it unless support asks. |
| **Shop ID** | Kinetic's internal identifier for the shop. Quote it when you contact support. |

## Business Hours

**Business hours** matter because Kinetic uses them to decide **how to route calls** and **when surveys go out**.

| Control | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Open days** | The days the shop is open. |
| **Opens at** and **Closes at** | Daily opening and closing times. |
| **Time zone** | The shop's time zone. Choose carefully: every scheduled message uses it. |
| **Closed on major holidays** | When on, the shop is treated as closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Eve. |

<Warning>
Wrong hours cause the receptionist to tell callers you're closed when you're open, or the other way round. Update them before and after any holiday closure.
</Warning>

## Contact Enrichment

Enrichment looks up public details to fill blanks on new people and companies. See [People](/records/people) and [Companies](/records/companies).

| Control | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Enrich new contacts** | When on, Kinetic automatically tries to enrich each new contact. |
| **Minimum match confidence** | How sure Kinetic must be that the result is the right person or company. Higher means fewer but safer fills. |

It only fills **blank** fields and never overwrites what you typed.

## Phone System

Connect the shop's phone system so incoming calls appear on the right repair order and the AI receptionist can work.

| Control | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect** | Link the phone system. |
| **Extensions** | For each member, the phone extension to match calls to people. |
| **Disconnect** | Stops incoming-call screen pops. |
| **Needs reconnect** | Shown when the connection has lapsed. Incoming calls aren't popping until you reconnect. |

See [Calls](/communicate/calls) for what the team sees.

## Reference Documents

**Reference documents** are PDFs the AI cites when someone asks an insurance-rule question, such as a state's claims regulation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose to add a document">
    Upload the PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Fill in **Source name**, **Source** (for example the regulation's citation), **Title**, **Version**, and **Effective date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing">
    The status goes **Processing**, then **Uploaded**. If it shows **Ingestion failed**, upload it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Uploading a new version of the same document marks the old one **Replaced**.

<Tip>
Upload only documents you are comfortable with the AI quoting. Keep **Effective date** correct, since people will rely on it.
</Tip>
