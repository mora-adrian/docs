Open **Settings** from the bottom of the sidebar. A **Search settings…** box at the top finds any setting by name, and **Back to app** returns you to your work.

## The settings menu

| Group | Page | What it controls | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Personal** | **Preferences** | Your name, email signature, theme, font, notification sound, schedule, mailbox, and push notifications | You |
| | **Security** | Your password, two-factor sign-in, and backup codes | You |
| **Shop** | The shop's name (**Shop settings**) | Logo, name, business hours, contact enrichment, phone system, reference documents | Admins and Owners |
| | **Repair orders** | Today thresholds, client advocate rules, CCC ONE employee mapping, cycle-time estimates, KPI targets | Admins and Owners |
| | **Production** | Department capacity and the columns that count against it | Admins and Owners |
| | **Messaging** | Texting, AI auto-reply, templates, team chats, customer survey, follow-up activity | Admins and Owners |
| | **Members** | Who has access and with what role | Admins and Owners |
| | **Reports** | Weekly email reports | Admins and Owners |
| | **AI usage** | Monthly AI spend | Admins and Owners |

Members who are not admins can read many of these pages but cannot change them. A page they cannot change says so, for example **Only admins can change reports.**

## What to set up first

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the basics">
    In [Shop settings](/admin/shop-settings): the **Name**, **Logo**, and **Business hours**. Business hours drive call routing and survey timing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    In [Members](/admin/members): add everyone, with the right [role](/start/roles).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map CCC ONE names to people">
    In [Repair order settings](/admin/repair-order-settings#ccc-one-employee-mapping): match the technician and estimator names CCC ONE sends to real employees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your numbers">
    In [Repair order settings](/admin/repair-order-settings#estimating-and-cycle-time): your average order value, cycle days, and labor hours per day. These make target dates and outcome likelihood realistic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect texting and phones">
    In [Messaging settings](/admin/messaging-settings) and the [phone system](/admin/shop-settings#phone-system).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on customer surveys">
    In [Messaging settings](/admin/messaging-settings#customer-survey): the **Pulse check** switch and your review links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up reports and capacity">
    [Reports](/admin/reports) and [Production](/admin/production).
  </Step>
</Steps>
