Open **Settings → Production**. Only admins can change it; if a teammate saved at the same moment you did, Kinetic asks you to reload before saving again.

This page powers the capacity warnings on the [Repairs board](/pipeline/overview#repairs). It answers: *how much work can we take in, and when are we over?*

## Capacity

The capacity table says how many **billable estimate hours** each department can process in a period.

| Department | Daily | Weekly | Monthly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Body + Glass** | hours | hours | hours |
| **Mechanical** | hours | hours | hours |
| **Refinish** | hours | hours | hours |
| **Structural** | hours | hours | hours |

Each period has its own **Save daily**, **Save weekly**, and **Save monthly** button under its column, so you can change one period without touching the others. Hours can have up to two decimals.

<Warning>
This is **estimate hours the shop can process, not hours worked**. If your body team finishes 40 estimate hours of work a day, enter 40, even if the people clock more or fewer hours.
</Warning>

## Production Columns

**Production columns** decide which statuses use up capacity, how long a job may sit in each, and who hears about an overload. Save them with **Save production columns**.

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Production starts at** | The first column whose orders count against capacity. Everything before it is pre-production. New shops start at **Parts Hold**. The list shows your repair columns in board order. |
| **Business days per phase** | One box per repair column: how long a vehicle may sit there before it flags overdue, counted on your business calendar. Whole numbers or up to two decimals, 365 at most. |
| **Total shop budget** | The sum of the boxes above, shown as **business days**. It must be more than zero. It also sets how much capacity weight each column carries. |
| **Alert when over** | The period (**Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly** capacity) the overload alerts compare against. All three still show on the board. |
| **Email alerts to** | The members who are emailed when a production column goes over capacity. |

<Note>
If any day box holds something that isn't a number, **Save production columns** stays unavailable until you fix it.
</Note>

## Good Practice

- Measure real throughput for a few weeks before you set capacity.
- Revisit after a staffing change.
- Send alerts to a person who can act on them, not a shared inbox no one reads.
