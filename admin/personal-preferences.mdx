title: "Your Preferences"
description: "Your name, email signature, theme, font, notification sound, schedule, connected mailbox, and push notifications."
---

Open **Settings → Preferences**. Everything here affects only you.

## Profile

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | How you appear to teammates. |
| **Email** | Your sign-in email. |
| **User ID** | Your internal identifier. Quote it to support. |

## Email Signature

Appended to emails you send. Use the **Bold**, **Italic**, and **Underline** buttons, and **Reset to default** to start over.

## Appearance

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Theme** | **System**, **Light**, or **Dark** |
| **Font** | **Inter**, **Geist**, or **IBM Plex Sans** |
| **Notification sound** | **Chime**, **Ping**, **Pop**, or **Off** |

## Your Schedule

Set the days you're off and when you're back. Tick **AI texts the customer back once…** to let the AI send a one-time reply to your customers while you're away, so they aren't left waiting.

## Mailbox

**Connect Google** or **Connect Microsoft** to bring your email into Lever. Lever can only **read** it. This turns on [Email review](/communicate/email-review).

## Push Notifications

Five switches, one for each category of notification, send alerts to your phone or computer.

<Note>
On an iPhone you must first add Lever to your Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or later). Open the site in Safari, choose **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
</Note>
