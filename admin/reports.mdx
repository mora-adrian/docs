---

Open **Settings → Reports**. Only admins can change reports (everyone else sees **Only admins can change reports.**).

Reports are emails sent to members of the shop once a week. They exist so that problems come to you, instead of you having to hunt for them.

## The Seven Reports

| Report | Lists | Default threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Supplement hold** | Vehicles in Supplement Hold at least this long, excluding possible total losses | 7 days |
| **Parts hold** | Vehicles in Parts Hold at least this long | 7 days |
| **Intake aging** | Vehicles still in an intake status that have been at the shop at least this long | 14 days |
| **Completed or closed, not delivered** | Vehicles that are completed or closed but have not been delivered | none |
| **Negative Client Satisfaction responses** | Clients who answered the Client Satisfaction survey with a poor rating in the last 7 days | none |
| **Customers not texted** | Clients on open, arrived repair orders who have not been texted in at least this many business days, including clients never texted. Grouped by client advocate. | 2 business days |
| **Low client sentiment** | Open, arrived repair orders whose client-sentiment gauge reads 40 or below (out of 100). Unscored repair orders are not listed. | fixed at 40 |

The day thresholds (supplement hold, parts hold, intake aging, and customers not texted) can be set from 1 to 365.

## Set One Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add recipients">
    Choose members. A report with no recipients does not send; the page says **Add a recipient to turn it on.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the day">
    The **weekday** the email goes out. Monday is the default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the time and time zone">
    The default time is 8:00 AM. Choose a time before people start work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the threshold">
    Where the report has one, enter the minimum days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send One Now

Each report has a button to send it immediately: **Email now** (to its recipients) or, when nobody is listed, **Email me a copy**. The page confirms with **Report queued for you**. If your latest change was not saved, it says so and does not send: **Your latest change was not saved, so the report was not sent.**

When nothing matches, the email says so (**No repair orders match right now**). That is good news, not an error.

## Make Them Useful

- Send each report to the **person who can act**: parts to the parts manager, negative CSI to the client advocate and the manager.
- Treat the email as a to-do list. Add a [task](/work/tasks) for each row.
- Raise or lower the thresholds until the lists are short enough to read.
