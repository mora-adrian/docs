Open **Settings → Messaging**. Only admins can change it.

## Text Messaging

| Item | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | **Configured**, **Not configured**, or **Unable to check**. |
| **From number** | The number customers see when you text them. |

<Info>
The page reminds you: **A contact must text your shop number first before you can reply.**
</Info>

## AI Auto-reply

A switch that lets Kinetic automatically reply to **inbound texts and missed calls from unknown contacts**.

- The business name, address, phone, and hours come from [Shop settings](/admin/shop-settings).
- **Additional context** is a free-text box for temporary information the AI should know: closures, holiday hours, a special notice. Remove it when it stops being true.

The AI answers using the shop's information. Read its replies in [Messages](/communicate/messages) and step in whenever a customer needs a person.

## Message Templates

Saved messages for staff to use with `@` in the text composer.

- **Add template**: give it a **name** and the **message**. Tap a variable to insert it (the customer's name, the vehicle, and so on).
- **Save** or **Delete** a template.

## Team Chats

Group chats on the shop's texting line that include Kinetic. See [Text Kinetic](/ai/text-kinetic).

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | A label for the chat. |
| **Assistant mode** | **Listen only** (Kinetic stores the conversation, never replies) or **Replies when addressed** (answers only messages that mention it). |
| **Members** | Active employees with a phone number. Pick at least two. |

Kinetic stays quiet if anyone outside the team is in the chat.

## Customer Survey

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pulse check** | The switch for the automatic pulse check and survey. When off, the CSI screen shows **Paused** and nothing sends. Scheduled surveys stay queued. |
| **Google review link** | Where customers are sent to review you on Google. |
| **Yelp review link** | Optional. A note warns that Yelp's policy does not allow businesses to ask customers for reviews, so decide with care. |

See [CSI](/csi/overview) for the whole process.

## Follow-up Activity

A log of the automatic supplement follow-ups to adjusters.

- **Stalled sequences** lists sequences that are active but overdue by more than 2 hours and not progressing. Look here if an adjuster says they never got a reminder.
- **Recent events** shows the last 50 events.

Follow-ups go every 2 business days, up to 10 times, until the adjuster replies.
