---

Open **Settings → Security**.

## Password

Enter your **Current password**, then a **New password** twice. When it works: **Password updated. Other sessions were signed out.** If you've forgotten the current one, choose **Email me a reset link**.

If the two new passwords differ you see **New passwords don't match.**

## Two-factor Authentication

Two-factor sign-in asks for a six-digit **authenticator app code** after your password.

- **Set up**: scan the code with an authenticator app on your phone, then enter the six digits it shows.
- **Reconfigure**: use a new phone or app.

## Backup Codes

Backup codes are one-time codes for when you can't reach your authenticator app.

- **Regenerate** creates a new set. They are shown **once**: **Save them now. They will only be shown once.**
- Copy them to a password manager or print them and keep them somewhere safe.
- A backup code works only at the **Confirm it's you** step, not on the sign-in screen.

<Warning>
If you lose your phone and have no backup codes, an admin cannot reset your two-factor sign-in. You must contact Kinetic support. Regenerate your backup codes before you need them.
</Warning>
