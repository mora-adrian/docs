Open **Settings → Security**.

## Password

Enter your **Current password**, then a **New password** twice. When it works: **Password updated. Other sessions were signed out.** If you've forgotten the current one, choose **Email me a reset link**.

If the two new passwords differ you see **New passwords don't match.**

## Passkeys

**Passkeys** let you sign in with your fingerprint, face, or device PIN instead of a password and code.

- **Add passkey** opens a window that asks you to **Name** it. Name it after the device so you can tell them apart later. Then follow your device prompt to save it on this device or a paired phone.
- Add one on each device you sign in from. Until you do, the list says **No passkeys yet**.
- Remove a passkey from its row. That device will no longer sign you in with it.
- If your browser doesn't support passkeys, the section says so. Try Chrome, Safari, or Edge on a device with a lock screen.

<Note>
A passkey is a full sign-in on its own, so it doesn't ask for an authenticator app code afterward. Your password and authenticator app still work.
</Note>

## Two-factor Authentication

Two-factor sign-in asks for a six-digit **authenticator app code** after your password.

- **Set up**: scan the code with an authenticator app on your phone, then enter the six digits it shows.
- **Reconfigure**: use a new phone or app.

## Backup Codes

Backup codes are optional one-time codes. Each one can stand in for an authenticator app code in the **Confirm it's you** window; the sign-in screen does not accept them.

- **Regenerate** creates a new set and shows it once.
- You don't need them to stay safe: if you lose your phone, your Kinetic administrator can reset your two-factor sign-in, and you set it up again at your next sign-in.
