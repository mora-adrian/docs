## Your account

- One account per person, invited by your shop admin. Never share credentials — activity is audited per user.
- An authenticator app code is required at sign-in and again for sensitive actions.
- Keep your recovery codes offline and private. They are the fallback if you lose your authenticator device.

## Sessions

- A session stays alive up to **4 hours** while idle and always ends **12 hours** after sign-in. Expect to sign in about once per workday.
- On a shared or floor computer, sign out when you finish. Closing the tab does not end the session.

## Sensitive-action checks

Admin and destructive actions — members, integrations, credentials, deletes — ask for a fresh authenticator app code. After you verify once, Kinetic trusts you for a 240-minute window. If prompts interrupt you often, batch your admin work into one sitting.

## If you lose access

- **Lost phone or authenticator app:** sign in with a recovery code, then re-enroll your authenticator app in **Settings → Security**.
- **No recovery codes:** your shop admin can reset your access.
- **Fully locked out:** contact your admin. Member administration happens inside Kinetic — there is no self-service unlock email, by design.

## If something looks wrong

Treat these as urgent:

- A sign-in email or authenticator prompt you did not trigger.
- A record changed that nobody touched.

Tell an admin immediately and change your password.

And one rule that prevents most incidents: never forward a customer link — survey, signature, or facts-of-loss — to anyone other than the intended customer.
