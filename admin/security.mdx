## Your account

- One account per person, invited by your shop admin. Never share credentials — activity is audited per user.
- An authenticator app code is required at sign-in and again for sensitive actions.
- Keep recovery codes offline and private; they're the fallback if you lose your authenticator device.

## Sessions

- Sessions stay alive up to **4 hours idle** and end no later than **12 hours** after sign-in. You'll sign in roughly once per workday.
- Using a shared/floor computer? Sign out when you're done — closing the tab doesn't end the session.

## Sensitive-action checks

Admin and destructive actions (members, integrations, credentials, deletes) ask for a fresh authenticator code, then trust you for a 240-minute window. If you get asked often, do your admin work in one sitting.

## Lost access

- **Lost phone / authenticator:** use a recovery code to sign in, then re-enroll in Settings → Security. No recovery codes? Your shop admin can reset you.
- **Locked out entirely:** contact your admin — they manage members from inside Kinetic. There's no self-service "unlock" email for security reasons.

## If something looks wrong

- Unexpected sign-in emails, authenticator prompts you didn't trigger, or records changed that nobody touched: tell an admin **immediately** and change your password.
- Never forward customer link URLs (survey, signature, facts of loss) to anyone but the intended customer.
