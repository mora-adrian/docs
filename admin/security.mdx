## How Kinetic Protects Access

| Protection | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Password plus authenticator app code** | Everyone signs in with a password. Two-factor sign-in is set up under [Security](/admin/personal-security). |
| **Sessions end** | After **4 hours** without activity, or **12 hours** in total. |
| **Extra checks for sensitive actions** | Actions such as changing members, security, or shop settings ask for a fresh code. After you confirm, you aren't asked again for **240 minutes**. |
| **Roles** | **Member**, **Admin**, and **Owner** limit what each person can change. See [Roles and access](/start/roles). |
| **Per-shop separation** | People only see the shops they belong to. |

## When Someone Joins

1. [Invite them](/admin/members) with the lowest role that works.
2. They get an email, set their own password, and set up two-factor sign-in the first time they sign in. Nobody shares a password.

## When Someone Leaves

1. **Suspend** them the same day. Access ends immediately.
2. Reassign their open [tasks](/work/tasks) and any clients they own.
3. Remove their phone extension and review team chats.

## When Someone Loses Their Device or Phone

- If they can still sign in somewhere, they choose **Reconfigure** under **Settings → Security** to link a new phone.
- If they are locked out, ask your Kinetic administrator to reset their two-factor sign-in. They set it up again the next time they sign in. The reset also signs them out everywhere and removes their passkeys, so a lost device can't keep signing in.
- There is no reset button in **Settings → Members**.
