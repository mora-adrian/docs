## How Kinetic Protects Access

| Protection | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Password plus authenticator app code** | Everyone signs in with a password. Two-factor sign-in is set up under [Security](/admin/personal-security). |
| **Sessions end** | After **4 hours** without activity, or **12 hours** in total. |
| **Extra checks for sensitive actions** | Actions such as sending a signature request or changing security ask for a fresh code. After you confirm, you aren't asked again for **240 minutes**. |
| **Roles** | **Member**, **Admin**, and **Owner** limit what each person can change. See [Roles and access](/start/roles). |
| **Per-shop separation** | People only see the shops they belong to. |

## When Someone Joins

1. [Add them](/admin/members) with the lowest role that works.
2. Share the password securely, never in a text to a group.
3. Ask them to set up two-factor sign-in on day one.

## When Someone Leaves

1. **Suspend** them the same day. Access ends immediately.
2. Reassign their open [tasks](/work/tasks) and any customers they own.
3. Remove their phone extension and review team chats.

## When Someone Loses Their Device or Phone

- They can sign in with a **backup code** at the extra-check step.
- Backup codes work **only** in the **Confirm it's you** step, not at the normal sign-in screen.
- If they have no backup codes and no authenticator, contact Kinetic support. **No admin button resets two-factor sign-in.**
