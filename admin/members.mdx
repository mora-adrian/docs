Open **Settings → Members**. Only Admins and Owners can use it. Search the list with the box at the top.

## The member list

The table shows **Name**, **Email**, and **Role**. Change a role with the dropdown in the row. The three roles are explained in [Roles and access](/start/roles).

## Add a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Add member">
    A dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    **Email**, **Name**, **Password**, and **Role** (**Member**, **Admin**, or **Owner**). Choose a strong password and tell the person to change it on first sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on approval">
    The **Pending (needs approval)** switch creates the account without access until an admin approves it. See [Sign in](/start/sign-in#access-pending).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The person can now sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Kinetic does **not** email an invitation. Give the person their email and password yourself, securely, and ask them to set up two-factor sign-in.
</Warning>

## Suspend and restore

**Suspend** removes someone's access immediately: *They will immediately lose access to this shop until an admin restores them.* Use it the day someone leaves. **Restore** gives access back. There is no delete, so the person's history stays intact.

Only an Owner can create, promote, or suspend another Owner.

## Days off and the AI

Each member has a **Schedule** dialog:

- **Days off**,
- **Back at**, the time they return, and
- **Let the AI reply to their customers on their day off**.

Members can also set this themselves under [Preferences](/admin/personal-preferences#your-schedule).

## Good practice

- Give people the **lowest role** that lets them do their job.
- Keep at least two Admins or Owners.
- Review the list every quarter and suspend anyone who has left.
