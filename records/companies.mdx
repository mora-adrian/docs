A **company** record is any organization you deal with: an insurance company, a dealership, a fleet or rental customer, a parts vendor, a tow company. A company can be the **customer** on a repair order, and a company is always who the **Insurer** party points to.

Open **Companies** in the sidebar, or press <Key>⌘</Key> <Key>K</Key> and type the name. See the [Records overview](/records/overview) for finding, editing, and archiving.

## What the Sheet Shows

| Row | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| A **logo** | Shown if one was found. |
| **Address** | The street address. |
| **Type** | The company's category, such as Insurance or Dealer, from your shop's list. |
| **Website** | The company's site. |
| Social links | **LinkedIn**, **Facebook**, and **Instagram**, when known. |
| **Contacts · 4** | The people at this company (adjusters, agents, managers) who exist in Lever. Click one to open that person. |
| **Linked repair orders · 12** | Every repair order the company is on. Click one to open its drawer. |

## The Edit Form

Choose **Edit** to open **Edit company**.

| Section | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Company Information** | **Company name** (required, shown as **Legal name**), **Doing business as**, **Default category**. |
| **Contact Information** | **Work number**, **Fax number**, **Email address**. |
| **Street Address** | **Street address**, **Street address line 2**, **City**, **State**, **Zip/Postal code**. |
| **Mailing Address** | A separate mailing address, if it differs from the street address. Same five fields. |
| **Social Media** | **LinkedIn username**, **Facebook username**, **Instagram username**. |

Save with **Save** or <Key>⌘</Key>+<Key>Enter</Key>.

## Company Enrichment

On a company's sheet, **Fetch this company's public brand profile** pulls in public details: a description, industry, employee count, estimated revenue, and social links. Like person enrichment, it only fills blanks and never overwrites what you entered.

- It needs a **website** or a **work email domain** on the record. Without one the sheet says **Add a website or a work email domain to this company to enable enrichment.**
- It may say **Not configured** if your shop has not turned enrichment on. See [Shop settings](/admin/shop-settings#contact-enrichment).
- If it fails you see **Enrichment failed. Try again.**

## Companies and Insurers

The **Insurer** on a repair order is a company record. Keeping the insurer's company record tidy (one record per insurer, its adjusters listed under **Contacts**) makes the **Insurer** column on the boards, the **Send to adjuster** email, and the Agent's answers correct.

<Tip>
Add each adjuster you deal with as a **person** and link them to their insurer's company. The next time that adjuster appears on a repair order, Lever already knows who they are.
</Tip>

## Do and Don't

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Use one record per company, with one consistent spelling. Fill in **Type** so reports and filters are meaningful. Add a website to unlock enrichment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't make a new company for each branch office unless they genuinely behave as separate customers. Don't leave an insurer's contacts unlinked.
  </Card>
</Columns>
