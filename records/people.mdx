A **person** record is anyone you deal with: customers, drivers, adjusters, appraisers, referrers, attorneys. A person can play several roles over time. The same person can be a customer on one job and the referrer on another, and it stays one record.

Open **People** in the sidebar to browse, or press <Key>⌘</Key> <Key>K</Key> and type a name. See [Records overview](/records/overview) for how to find, edit, and archive.

## What the Sheet Shows

Click a person and a sheet opens with:

| Row | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Phone** | Their main phone number. |
| **Email** | Their email address. |
| **Address** | Their mailing address. |
| **Referred by** | Where they heard about the shop. |
| **Company** | The company they belong to, if any. |
| **Vehicles · 2** | Every vehicle they own, with its VIN. |
| **Repair orders · 3** | Every repair order they appear on. Click one to open its drawer. |
| **Public profile data** | Anything enrichment found. See below. |

## The Edit Form

Choose **Edit** to open **Edit person**. The form is split into sections.

### Preferences

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Do not text** | A switch. When on, **outgoing texts to this person are blocked.** Email and calls still work. See [below](#contact-preferences). |
| **Reason** | Appears when the switch is on. *e.g. Customer requested no outreach.* |

### Name

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Prefix/Salutation** | None, Mr., Ms., Dr., Prof., Rev., Sir, Dame, or Hon. |
| **First name** | Required. |
| **Last name** | Required. |
| **Preferred name** | A nickname. It appears in quotes next to their name, such as Jordan "JR" Rivera. |
| **Suffix** | None, Jr, Sr, II, III, IV, or V. |

### Contact Numbers and Email

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Mobile number** | The number Kinetic texts. At least one number is recommended. |
| **Work number** | |
| **Home number** | |
| **Email address** | Used for notifications and statements. |

Phone numbers are cleaned up and stored in one standard form, so `704-555-0100`, `(704) 555 0100`, and `7045550100` are all the same number.

### Mailing Address

**Mailing address**, **Mailing address line 2**, **City**, **State**, and **Zip/Postal code**. Used for statements and correspondence, and as the pickup address that [appointment scheduling](/schedule/calendar) offers by default.

### Social Media

**LinkedIn username**, **Facebook username**, and **Instagram username**. If enrichment filled one in, a gray line beneath reads **Added automatically · {date}**.

### Classification

**Referral source** and **Default category**. Both are picked from lists your shop maintains.

Save the form with **Save** or <Key>⌘</Key>+<Key>Enter</Key>.

## Contact Preferences

### Do Not Text

Turn this on when a customer asks you to stop texting them, or when your team agrees texting would not be appropriate.

- Outgoing texts to that person are **blocked**. The thread on their repair order shows **{Name} is flagged do not text**, with your reason if you wrote one, and the composer is disabled.
- **Email and calls are not affected.**
- It also stops **survey texts** from going to them. A survey scheduled for a Do-not-text customer is skipped, and the CSI screen shows **Recipient blocked**.
- It does **not** stop *them* texting you. For a number that should never reach you, use **Block on all lines** in [Messages](/communicate/messages#blocking-a-number).

<Tip>
Always write a **Reason**. A teammate who finds a disabled composer deserves to know why.
</Tip>

## Contact Enrichment

**Enrichment** looks up *public* profile details about a person and fills in what is missing: social profiles, job title, birth year or age, an additional phone or address.

- It is **optional**, and off unless your admin turns it on in [Shop settings](/admin/shop-settings#contact-enrichment).
- It only fills **empty** fields. It **never overwrites** something a person typed.
- Each detail is labeled **High** (a confident match) or **Uncertain match**. Treat an uncertain match as a hint, not a fact, and check before relying on it.
- A footer shows **Public profile data · {date}** so you know how fresh it is.

At the bottom of a person's sheet, the **Enriched** section has an **Enrich** button. If it is disabled, a line explains why:

| You see | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact enrichment is off in Settings** | Your shop has not turned it on. |
| **Contact enrichment isn't set up for this shop** | The lookup service is not connected. |
| **Not enough contact details to look up** | The record needs more: a name plus a phone, email, or address. |
| **Already looked up recently** | It was run recently. Try again later. |

If nothing is found, you see **No public profile data on file.**

## Do and Don't

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Capture a mobile number and an email. Use **Preferred name** for how the customer likes to be addressed. Write a reason whenever you switch on **Do not text**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't trust an **Uncertain match** without checking. Don't make a second record for a customer who got a new phone number; edit the first one.
  </Card>
</Columns>
