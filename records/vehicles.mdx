A **vehicle** record is a particular car, truck, or SUV, identified by its **VIN** (vehicle identification number: the 17-character serial number stamped on the car, found on the dashboard near the windshield or on the driver's door frame). Vehicles outlive repairs. The same car in the shop twice has one record with two repairs in its history.

Open **Vehicles** in the sidebar, or press <Key>⌘</Key> <Key>K</Key> and type a VIN, make, or model.

## What the Sheet Shows

| Row | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **VIN** | The vehicle identification number, in a monospaced font. |
| **Owner** | The person who owns it. Click to open them. |
| **Color** | The exterior color. |
| **Odometer** | The mileage on record. |
| **Condition** | The vehicle's overall condition, from your shop's list. |
| **Repair history · 3** | Every repair order for this vehicle, whichever customer paid for it. Click one to open its drawer. |

## The Edit Form

Choose **Edit** to open **Edit vehicle**.

| Section | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Vehicle Information** | **VIN** (letters are made upper-case for you), **Year**, **Make**, **Model**, **Trim**. |
| **Appearance** | **Exterior color** and **Interior color**, chosen from a standard list: Beige, Black, Blue, Brown, Gold, Gray, Green, Orange, Purple, Red, Silver, White, Yellow, or Other. |
| **Details** | **Odometer** (current mileage as shown on the odometer), **Odometer out**, **Condition**. |
| **License Plate** | **License plate** and **License plate state**. |

**Odometer out** is the final mileage at delivery. You do not need to type it. It fills in automatically when the repair order is delivered.

Save with **Save** or <Key>⌘</Key>+<Key>Enter</Key>.

<Note>
CCC ONE supplies the VIN, year, make, model, color, and odometer when it creates a repair order, and can update them later. If one of these keeps changing back, correct it in CCC ONE.
</Note>

## Manufacturer Logos

Vehicle records, board cards, and the drawer show the **manufacturer's logo** beside the make, so a customer's Subaru reads like a Subaru at a glance. It comes from the **Make** field, so spell the make correctly ("Chevrolet", not "Chevy").

## Market Value

On a repair order, the **Expected insurance payout** row uses the vehicle's market value. It is based on recent sales for the vehicle (by VIN and mileage) and used to work out [Outcome likelihood](/repair-orders/facts#outcome-likelihood). Getting the **VIN and odometer right** makes the value, and so the outcome likelihood, more accurate.

## Do and Don't

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Check a VIN character by character. Photograph the VIN plate at intake. Record the odometer on arrival.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't enter a placeholder VIN. A fake VIN makes duplicate records, breaks the value lookup, and keeps a lead stuck in **Incomplete Lead**.
  </Card>
</Columns>
