Behind every repair order are three kinds of **record**: the **people** involved, the **companies** they work for or belong to, and the **vehicles**. Kinetic keeps one record for each real person, company, and vehicle, so the same customer who returns two years later is the same record, with the same history.

You will find them under **Records** in the sidebar: **People**, **Companies**, and **Vehicles**, each with a count.

## How Records Connect

| A… | links to… |
| --- | --- |
| **Person** | Their vehicles, their company, and every repair order they appear on (as the customer, driver, referrer, adjuster, and so on). |
| **Company** | Its contacts (people) and every repair order it is on, such as an insurer, a dealer, or a fleet customer. |
| **Vehicle** | Its owner and its whole repair history, whichever repair order each repair appeared on. |
| **Repair order** | A primary customer (a person or a company), a vehicle, and any other parties. |

Change a person's phone number on their record and every repair order that uses that person shows the new number. You never fix the same detail in five places.

## Where Records Come From

You do not add records by hand on these screens. They arrive from **CCC ONE** with the repair order (customer, vehicle, insurer, adjuster) and Kinetic keeps them linked. There are two other ways a record appears:

- **From a phone call.** When a call comes in from an unknown number, the screen pop offers **Create contact** to start a person record with that number. See [Calls](/communicate/calls#screen-pops).
- **From enrichment.** Public profile details can fill blank fields on a contact. See [People](/records/people#contact-enrichment).

<Note>
**CCC ONE can update a record.** When CCC ONE sends new customer or vehicle details, they replace what Kinetic had for the same fields. If a detail keeps changing back after you fix it, fix it in CCC ONE as well.
</Note>

## Finding a Record

There is no search box on the record lists. Use the **command palette** instead, which searches everything at once:

1. Press <Key>⌘</Key> <Key>K</Key> (<Key>Ctrl</Key> <Key>K</Key> on Windows) from anywhere.
2. Type a name, a phone number, a VIN, or a repair order number.
3. Choose the person, company, or vehicle from the results. If there are more related records, choose **Show related records**.

The record opens in its side sheet on the matching Records screen.

You can also browse the lists. Click a column heading to sort, and use **Previous** and **Next** at the bottom to page. The sorting and paging are done for the whole shop, not just the page on screen.

## The List Screens

| Screen | Columns | Sortable by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **People** | Name, Phone, Email, Address, Referral source, Vehicles, Open repair orders | Last name, first name, email |
| **Companies** | Company, Type, Address, Contacts, Linked repair orders | Name, type |
| **Vehicles** | Vehicle, VIN, Owner, Odometer, Condition, Repair orders | Year, make, model, VIN |

An empty list says **No people found.**, **No companies found.**, or **No vehicles found.** On a phone, the rows turn into cards that show the same facts.

## The Record Sheet

Click a row to open the record's sheet on the right. At the top it shows the name and key facts. Two buttons sit under it:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Opens the edit form in a window. Save with **Save**, or press <Key>⌘</Key>+<Key>Enter</Key>. |
| **Archive** | Takes the record out of use. The first click turns the button into **Confirm archive**; click again to confirm. Archiving is a protected action that requires an admin or owner and a fresh authenticator code. |

<Warning>
**Archive is for records that should never have existed**, such as a duplicate or a test entry. An archived record disappears from lists and searches. Do not archive a customer because the job is finished.
</Warning>

Below the buttons, the sheet lists everything the record is linked to, each as a link: the **Vehicles**, **Repair orders**, **Contacts**, or **Repair history** that belong to it. Click a repair order and its [drawer](/repair-orders/drawer) opens.

## Duplicates

There is no "merge" screen. If you find two records for one real person:

1. Decide which record is the better one (more history, correct spelling).
2. Edit it so it is correct.
3. Tell your admin which duplicate to archive, or archive it yourself if you are an admin or owner.

The best cure is prevention. **Before you create or edit, search**: a ten-second ⌘K check beats a merge later. Duplicates split history, so two repair orders for the same customer look like two customers.

## Good Habits

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Keep a **mobile number** and an **email** on every customer; texting and surveys need them. Fix spelling mistakes the first time you see them. Record how the customer found you in **Referral source**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't make a second record to "fix" a first. Don't put notes about a repair in a customer's record; put them on the repair order. Don't archive records just to tidy up.
  </Card>
</Columns>
