---
title: "Daily Sweep"
description: "A 20-minute routine to start each day: notifications, Today, tasks, messages, the boards, and the calendar, in an order that catches problems early."
---

## When to Use It

Every morning, before the phones get busy. Also at midday if you are covering for someone.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Notifications (2 minutes)">
    Open [Notifications](/work/notifications). Clear what you have handled. Open what you have not.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Today (5 minutes)">
    Open [Today](/work/today). Work the four sections in this order: **Missing documents**, **Supplements waiting**, **Past the money gate**, **Close to delivery**. For each row, take one action or create a task.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tasks (3 minutes)">
    Open [Tasks](/work/tasks). Anything **overdue** gets done now, reassigned, or rescheduled with a note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Messages (4 minutes)">
    Open [Messages](/communicate/messages). Answer every conversation with an unread customer text. Check any **Chat health** warnings before you reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="CSI (2 minutes)">
    Open [CSI](/csi/overview) and look at **Responded** for **Negative** scores. Call anyone unhappy today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Calendar (2 minutes)">
    Open [Calendar](/schedule/calendar). Check today's arrivals and deliveries. If a tow changed, **Reoptimize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Boards (2 minutes)">
    Scan the **Repairs** board for cards with a red dot. Open each and record why it is late and what is being done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Done Looks Like

Every row you touched has either been resolved or has a task with an owner and a date.

## Common Mistakes

- Skipping the order and reading email first. Today tells you what is actually urgent.
- Reading without acting. If you looked, do something or schedule something.
- Saving texts for later. Same-day replies are what customers remember.
