A **playbook** is a routine. The rest of this manual explains what each screen does; the playbooks tell you **what to do, in order, when a real situation comes up**, and what "done" looks like.

Each playbook has the same layout:

- **When to use it**: the trigger.
- **Before you start**: what you need.
- **Steps**: in order.
- **Done looks like**: how you know you can stop.
- **Common mistakes**.

## Principles Behind Every Playbook

1. **If it is not in Kinetic, it did not happen.** Record calls as notes. Upload files to the repair order. A teammate cannot act on what only you know.
2. **Fix data at its source.** Repair orders come from CCC ONE. If a customer detail, VIN, or status is wrong, fix it in CCC ONE and Kinetic will follow.
3. **Tell the customer before they ask.** The best update is the one they did not have to chase.
4. **Own the next step.** Every waiting job should have a task with a name and a date on it.
5. **Never promise what the system only estimates.** Target dates and outcome likelihood are calculations, not commitments.
