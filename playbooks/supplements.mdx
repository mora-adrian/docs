A **supplement** is a request for more money from the insurer after the shop finds damage that was not on the first estimate. The car stays in <Pill tone="waiting">Supplement Hold</Pill> until the insurer replies.

## When to Use It

Teardown finds hidden damage, or a part turns out to be unavailable and an alternative costs more.

## Before You Start

- The estimate in CCC ONE has been updated with the extra operations. Run [Rivet](/ai/rivet) if you want a second pair of eyes.
- Photos of the new damage are uploaded to the repair order's [Files](/repair-orders/files).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather proof">
    Upload photos of the hidden damage. Tag the supplement estimate **Supplement**, and the first scan **Pre-repair Scan**. Insurers pay what they can see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the package">
    On the **Files** tab choose **Send to adjuster**. Lever turns photos into PDF packets under the insurer portal size limit and opens an email draft. Read it and send. Leave **Auto follow-up every 2 business days until the adjuster replies** switched on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell the customer">
    Text them in plain words: what was found, that the insurer must approve, and that the target date may move. Do not promise a new date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record it">
    Add a note: who you sent it to, when, and what was asked for. Add a row for it on the [ARW](/repair-orders/arw) (**Supplement 1**, **2**, and so on) with the amount **Due**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chase on a schedule">
    The row appears on **Today → Supplements waiting** after a few days. Call the adjuster. The automatic follow-up email goes every 2 business days, up to 10 times, but a call is faster than any email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="When it is approved">
    Move the amount into the **ARW** as it is promised or paid, update the customer, move the car on, and add a note. Start the next step quickly; every day in hold is a day on the clock.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Done Looks Like

The insurer has approved, the ARW has the supplement row, the car has left hold, and the customer knows the new situation.

## Common Mistakes

- Sending photos in the wrong format or too large. Use **Send to adjuster**.
- Not telling the customer until the insurer replies.
- Forgetting the ARW row and being surprised at pickup.
- Letting a follow-up run on after the adjuster has replied by phone. Switch it off on the draft or the thread.
