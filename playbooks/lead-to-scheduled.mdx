## When to use it

A new lead appears on the **Opportunities** board in **Incomplete Lead** or **Unscheduled Lead**, and the customer has not yet booked a drop-off.

## Before you start

- You are on the [Opportunities board](/pipeline/overview).
- You know what the five lead statuses mean: see [Statuses](/pipeline/statuses#opportunities).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the lead">
    Click the card. The [drawer](/repair-orders/drawer) opens. Read the vehicle, claim, and any notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find what is missing">
    A lead counts as complete only with the customer's **name, email, phone, and address**, and the vehicle's **VIN, year, make, model, and exterior color**. Anything blank is why it is stuck in **Incomplete Lead**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact the customer the same day">
    Call first; follow with a text from the **Texts** tab. Introduce yourself, say why you are calling, and ask for what is missing. Speed matters: the first shop to respond often wins the job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what you learn">
    Fix missing details **in CCC ONE**. Kinetic updates within moments, and the card moves to **Unscheduled Lead** when details are complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book the appointment">
    Open [Calendar](/schedule/calendar), choose **New appointment**, pick the repair order and a type (**Experience in** if they will drive in, **Concierge in** if you will tow). Book it in CCC ONE too so the arrival date is recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in writing">
    Text the customer the date, time, address, and what to bring (insurance card, keys, any spare keys, claim number).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave a note and a task">
    Add a note to **Activity** summarizing the conversation. Create a [task](/work/tasks) for the day before the appointment: *Confirm arrival with customer*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Done looks like

The card shows <Pill tone="needs">Scheduled (Won)</Pill>, the appointment is on the calendar, the customer has a written confirmation, and a reminder task exists.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting for the customer to call back. Call twice, text once, and keep notes of each attempt.
- Entering a placeholder VIN to "complete" the lead. This breaks the market-value lookup and hides the real gap.
- Booking an appointment without recording it in CCC ONE.
- Leaving a lead in **Evaluation** with no task attached.
