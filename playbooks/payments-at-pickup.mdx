---
title: "Payments at Pickup"
description: "Reconcile the accounts receivable worksheet before the client arrives, so pickup is a handshake and not a surprise."
---

## When to Use It

A car is in **Repairs Complete** or about to be, or appears on **Today → Review amounts owed**.

## Before You Start

You know who owes what: the client's **deductible**, the insurer's payment, any **betterment** or **appearance allowance**. See [ARW](/repair-orders/arw).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ARW">
    Open the repair order's **ARW** sheet. Look at the **reconciliation footer**: **Totals**, **CCC ONE**, **Due at pickup**, and **Difference**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Difference">
    A red **Difference** means something is missing: an unlogged supplement, a forgotten deductible, or a payment you did not record. Find it before the client arrives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record payments the moment they land">
    When money arrives, move it from **In transit** to **Prepaid** and choose **Save worksheet**. Attach proof of payment where you have it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell the client what they owe">
    Text or call a day ahead: the amount due at pickup, what it covers, and accepted payment methods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take payment at pickup">
    Record it right then. Do not release the car until the **Due at pickup** is zero, unless a manager approves in writing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Done Looks Like

**Due at pickup** and **Difference** both read zero, the client has a receipt, and the ARW is saved.

## Common Mistakes

- Entering an insurer payment as **Client**.
- Typing over a **CCC ONE** row's **Due**. Record the payment instead.
- Leaving payments to "be entered tomorrow".
