## When to use it

A car reaches <Pill tone="ontrack">Repairs Complete</Pill>, or appears on **Today → Close to delivery**.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the paperwork">
    Make sure the signed documents are on the **Files** tab and the [payments](/playbooks/payments-at-pickup) are reconciled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the pickup">
    Create an appointment: **Experience out** if the customer is collecting, **Concierge out** if you are delivering. Move the card to **Experience Scheduled** or **Tow Scheduled**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect together">
    Walk around the car with the customer. Use **Interview** for this conversation. If something needs fixing, move the card to **Corrections** and tell the customer plainly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explain the warranty and care">
    Tell the customer about the shop's workmanship warranty and any care instructions for fresh paint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over the keys">
    Record the odometer reading. Add a note: who collected, the time, any comments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close in CCC ONE">
    When CCC ONE records the vehicle as delivered, Kinetic moves the job to **Enrichment**. The customer's [CSI](/csi/overview) pulse check follows about 24 hours later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Done looks like

The car is delivered, the customer signed off, and a survey is scheduled.

## Common mistakes

- Handing over the car before reconciling money.
- Skipping the walk-around, then hearing about a scratch by text.
- Forgetting to update the mobile number: no number means no survey.
