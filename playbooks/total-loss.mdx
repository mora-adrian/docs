A **total loss** means the car would cost more to repair than it is worth, so the insurer pays the car's value instead of repairing it. It is the hardest conversation a client has with a shop, so handle it carefully.

## How Kinetic Flags One

CCC ONE moves the card:

- A total-loss stage in CCC ONE puts it in <Pill tone="waiting">Pending Total Loss</Pill> on the Repairs board.
- When CCC ONE marks the estimate a total loss, it moves to **Total Loss** on the Close & Deliver board.

Setting **Repairability** to **Total loss** in Kinetic records the decision on the repair order but does not move the card. See [Statuses](/pipeline/statuses#pending-total-loss-and-total-loss).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm with the insurer">
    Only the insurer decides. Do not tell the client it is a total loss until the adjuster confirms in writing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Repairability">
    Set **Repairability** to **Total loss** on the repair order so the record reflects it. It stays blank until someone rules on it, and Kinetic never assumes a car is repairable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell the client with care">
    Call personally. Explain what a total loss means, what happens to the car, and what they should expect from the insurer. Follow up in writing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the release">
    On the [Signatures](/repair-orders/signatures) tab send the **Vehicle Release, Waiver, & Hold Harmless**. It is used for total losses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect what is owed">
    Record storage, teardown, or other charges on the [ARW](/repair-orders/arw).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release and close">
    Arrange pickup by the insurer's salvage vendor or the client. Record who took the car, and when.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Changes in Kinetic

- Total-loss jobs are **not** reviewed by Rivet.
- They are **not** sent satisfaction surveys.
- Their target-date cycle is shorter (the shop's total-loss cycle days).

## Common Mistakes

- Telling the client before the insurer has decided.
- Skipping the release form.
- Forgetting to bill storage and teardown.
