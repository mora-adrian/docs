---
title: "Best practices"
description: "The habits that make Kinetic pay off — clean data, honest statuses, and fast follow-up."
---

Kinetic is only as good as the truth inside it. These practices separate shops that get value from shops that just have another login.

## Keep statuses honest

- Move a repair order the moment the real world changes. A car sitting in "In repair" that is actually waiting on parts corrupts every report and target date.
- Never park a job in the wrong status to hide it. A stuck job is a blocker to surface — put it on the record.
- Sweep your board before you leave, so tomorrow starts accurate.

## Keep one record per real thing

- Search before you create a person or a vehicle. Duplicates split history and confuse every downstream feature.
- Fill in phone, email, and preferred contact method. Messaging and the Agent depend on them.
- When Kinetic suggests a duplicate, take ten seconds to look. Checking now costs less than merging later.

## Communicate from inside the app

- Use Messages for every customer text and email, so history attaches to the repair order. Personal phones create invisible conversations.
- Record each customer's preferred channel, and honor restrictions.
- Work the shared inbox every day. Triage unmatched items instead of letting them pile up.

## Let AI help — and check its work

- Record a verdict on every Rivet finding. Reviews improve only when you say where they are right and wrong.
- Read every AI draft before it sends. AI drafts; people decide.
- Ask the Agent specific questions that name records, and verify its answers against the records it cites.

## Manage commitments, not memory

- Turn promises into tasks with due dates on the repair order — "call customer," "check parts ETA" — so anyone covering for you can see them.
- Check Today every morning. It is the fastest way to spot what is slipping.
- Close the CSI loop the same day. A low score answered within hours often becomes a good review.

## For admins

- Keep membership lean: Member by default, Admin only for people who manage the shop, and as few Owners as practical.
- Review members quarterly and remove access when someone leaves — access ends when employment does.
- Treat the authenticator check as protection, not friction. It is what stands between a stolen password and your customer data.
