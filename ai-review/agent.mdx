---
title: "The AI Agent"
description: "Ask questions and get things done in plain language — grounded in your shop's real data."
---

The **Agent** screen is a chat interface over your shop's data. Ask it things, ask it to draft things, and let it execute bounded, approved actions — always with a human in the loop for anything consequential.

## What it's good at

- **Answering questions:** "Which repairs are past their target date?", "When is the Accord's fender arriving?", "Show me this week's deliveries."
- **Summarizing:** a repair order's history, a customer thread, what's blocking a job.
- **Drafting:** customer updates, follow-up messages — drafts you review before anything sends.
- **Finding:** records, files, and facts faster than clicking through screens.
- **Executing bounded actions:** well-scoped operations the system has been explicitly approved to perform, each logged and attributable.

## What it will never do

<Warning>
The agent cannot change your permissions, see other shops' data, invent a repair status, or make coverage, safety, financial, or delivery commitments on its own. Generated text has no authority — a human approves anything that goes out.
</Warning>

## How to get good answers

- **Be specific.** "Repairs past target assigned to me" beats "what's late?"
- **Name the record.** Customer name, vehicle, or repair order number grounds the answer.
- **Check the grounding.** Answers show the records they came from — if it cited the wrong repair order, correct it.
- **Use previews.** Action cards and record previews show exactly what the agent is about to reference or do before it does it.

## Trust but verify

The agent reads the same data you can see — it can't access records you don't have permission to see. Treat it like a very fast new hire: check its work on anything that leaves the building, correct it when it's wrong, and it'll keep getting more useful as your data stays clean.
