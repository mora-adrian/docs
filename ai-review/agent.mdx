---
title: "The AI Agent"
description: "Ask questions and get things done in plain language — grounded in your shop's real data."
---

The **Agent** screen is a chat interface over your shop's data. Ask it questions, ask it to draft text, and let it perform bounded, approved actions — always with a person in the loop for anything consequential.

## What the Agent does well

- **Answers questions** — for example: "Which repairs are past their target date?", "When is the Accord's fender arriving?", "Show me this week's deliveries."
- **Summarizes** — a repair order's history, a customer thread, or what is blocking a job.
- **Drafts** — customer updates and follow-up messages that you review before anything sends.
- **Finds** — records, files, and facts faster than clicking through screens.
- **Executes bounded actions** — specific, approved operations, each logged and attributable.

## What the Agent never does

<Warning>
The Agent cannot change your permissions, see another shop's data, invent a repair status, or make coverage, safety, financial, or delivery commitments on its own. Generated text has no authority — a person approves anything that goes out.
</Warning>

## Get good answers

- **Be specific.** "Repairs past target assigned to me" works better than "what's late?"
- **Name the record.** A customer name, vehicle, or repair order number grounds the answer.
- **Check the grounding.** Answers show the records they came from. If the Agent cited the wrong repair order, correct it.
- **Read the previews.** Action cards and record previews show exactly what the Agent is about to reference or do before it does it.

## Trust, then verify

The Agent reads the same data you can see — it cannot reach records you lack permission to view. Treat it like a fast new hire: check its work on anything that leaves the building, correct it when it is wrong, and keep your records clean so its answers stay sharp.
