**Rivet** reviews estimates for operations that are likely missing or under-represented — scans, masking, remove-and-install work, blends, and other non-included operations that shops routinely leave unbilled. It is a second set of eyes trained on a large corpus of reviewed estimates.

## The Estimates screen

The list shows every estimate imported into Rivet, with a search field and a status filter.

**Status filter options:**

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| All | Every estimate. |
| Action needed | Estimates whose review found something undecided — your work queue. |
| Not audited | Imported but never reviewed. |
| Review queued | Review requested, waiting to run. |
| Review running | Analysis in progress. |
| Audited | Review complete, all findings decided. |
| Review failed | The analysis could not complete — retry it. |

Estimate badges also flag **Needs re-review** when a changed estimate makes the last review stale, and **View audit results** when a finished review has findings to look at.

## Review an estimate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import the estimate">
    Estimates arrive from CCC ONE automatically, or you can import one from the Rivet screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the review">
    Select **Run review**. Analysis runs in the background — typically a few minutes — and the screen updates when it finishes. Keep working; you do not need to wait on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through the findings">
    Each finding is an operation Rivet believes belongs on the estimate: a claim-ready line title, a justification, the supporting reasoning, and the evidence behind it. Where estimate photos exist, Rivet's vision pass uses them — damage visible in a photo can support or kill a finding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record a verdict on every finding">
    Use the thumbs-up for real misses and thumbs-down (with a short reason) for findings that do not apply. Your reason — "already on line 47," "the carrier denied this" — teaches the next review more than a bare rejection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verdicts

| Verdict | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| **Added** | The finding is real — you added (or will add) the operation to the estimate. |
| **Already on estimate** | The operation is already billed — Rivet flagged something covered. |
| **Duplicate** | The same finding appeared more than once. |
| **Not applicable** | The operation does not apply to this vehicle or repair. |

Findings can carry a **Gold evidence** tier — *almost always*, *usually*, *consider* — indicating how often the operation proves out across reviewed estimates. Treat high-tier findings as near-checklist items and *consider* findings as prompts to look.

## Why verdicts matter

Verdicts are the feedback loop. Added and rejected findings feed back into later reviews for your shop — each verdict brings Rivet's recommendations closer to your judgment. That is also why **Run review again** opens a *new* run beside the old one instead of replacing it: the history of your decisions is data.

<Info>
Read the reasoning, not just the recommendation. Rivet proposes — the estimator decides. Reject confidently when the photos or vehicle context disagree.
</Info>

## Get the most from Rivet

- **Review every estimate**, not only large ones — small missed operations add up fastest.
- **Verdict every finding.** Skipped verdicts teach nothing.
- **Stay consistent.** Opposite verdicts on the same operation send noisy feedback; align in a team meeting when estimators disagree.
- **Reuse the claim-ready text.** Findings are drafted in the language supplements expect — adapt it rather than rewriting from scratch.
