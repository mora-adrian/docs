---
title: "Rivet estimate review"
description: "AI review of body-shop estimates — missed operations, evidence, and a feedback loop that learns your verdicts."
---

**Rivet** reviews estimates for operations that are likely missing or under-represented — scans, masking, remove-and-install work, blends, and other non-included operations that shops routinely leave unbilled. It is a second set of eyes trained on a large corpus of reviewed estimates.

## Review an estimate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import the estimate">
    Bring an estimate into Rivet from the Rivet screen or an estimate import. Estimates can also arrive automatically from your estimating system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the review">
    Start a review and keep working. Analysis runs in the background and typically takes a few minutes; the screen updates when it finishes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through the findings">
    Each finding describes an operation Rivet believes belongs on the estimate, with claim-ready wording — a line title, a justification, and the supporting reasoning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record your verdict on every finding">
    Accept the findings that are real; reject the ones that are not. A finding already billed on the estimate shows as **"Already on estimate."**
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why verdicts matter

Your verdicts are the feedback loop. Accepted and rejected findings are read back into later reviews for your shop, so each verdict brings Rivet's recommendations closer to your judgment. This is also why a re-review opens a *new* run beside the old one rather than replacing it: the history of your decisions is data.

<Info>
Read the reasoning, not just the recommendation. Rivet proposes — the estimator decides. Reject confidently when the photos or the vehicle context disagree.
</Info>

## Photo analysis

When estimate photos are available, Rivet uses them as evidence — damage visible in a photo can support or kill a finding. Upload clear, well-lit photos of the damage for the best results.

## Get the most from Rivet

- **Review every estimate**, not only large ones. Small missed operations add up fastest.
- **Always record a verdict.** Skipped verdicts teach Rivet nothing.
- **Stay consistent.** Opposite verdicts on the same operation send noisy feedback. When estimators disagree, align in a team meeting.
- **Reuse the claim-ready text.** Findings are drafted in the language supplements expect — adapt it instead of rewriting from scratch.
