Some paperwork has to be signed before work can start or before a car can be released. The **Signatures** tab lets you send those documents to the customer's phone and see when they have opened and signed them. It also holds the **facts-of-loss** questionnaire, which asks the customer to describe the accident in their own words.

Customers never need an account or an app. They open a link on their phone, read, and sign or answer. See [What customers see](/csi/customer-pages) for their side of it.

<Info>
Anyone with the **Member** role or higher can send these. Sending a signature request asks for a fresh authenticator code, because it puts a legal document in front of a customer.
</Info>

## The Templates

The **Template** list at the top of the tab holds your shop's contracts, plus **Facts of Loss**. The standard set is:

| Template | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Contract of Repair and Repair Authorization** | The customer's written permission to repair the vehicle, and the terms: payment, damage analysis and additional damage, how the insurer's estimate is used, the effect of non-original parts, the shop's workmanship warranty, and the shop's rights if the car is left unpaid. This is the **work authorization**. Once signed, it clears the missing-authorization warning. |
| **Vehicle Release, Waiver, & Hold Harmless** | Used when a car is a **total loss**. The customer releases the vehicle. |
| **Direction of Payment** | The customer tells their insurer to pay the shop directly. |
| **Appraisal Clause** | A notice invoking the customer's right to an independent appraisal, naming the consumer advocacy firm as the customer's appraiser. |
| **Facts of Loss** | Not a contract: a questionnaire. See [below](#facts-of-loss). |

Each template fills in the customer's name, address, vehicle, VIN, claim number, insurance carrier, and the shop's details for you.

## Send a Signature Request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    Pick it from **Template**. If the list says **No templates available**, there are none set up for your shop yet. Contact Lever support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview it">
    Choose **Preview** to open the document filled in for this customer. Check the name, vehicle, and claim number. A **Preview failed** message means the document could not be built; try again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Choose **Send via text**. Lever texts the customer a link and, if you have not verified lately, asks for your authenticator code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the status">
    The request appears in the list below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choosing **Send via text** twice for the same document **does not send a second one**. Lever recognizes the same request and delivers the original again.

### Reading the List

Each row shows the **recipient**, a status badge, a line of dates, and when the request was created.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Sending** | Lever is preparing and delivering it. |
| **Sent** | The customer has the link. The row says **Awaiting signature**. |
| **Viewed** | The customer opened it. The row says **Viewed** and the date. |
| **Signed** <Pill tone="ontrack">done</Pill> | The customer signed. The row says **Signed** and the date, and the signed copy is filed on the **Files** tab with the matching tag (for example **Work Authorization**). |
| **Declined** | The customer refused to sign. |
| **Expired** | The link ran out before they signed. Send a new one. |

<Tip>
**A document stuck on Sent?** Call the customer. Many people ignore a text from an unfamiliar number. Tell them to expect it and look for a text from the shop.
</Tip>

## Facts of Loss

The **Facts of Loss** questionnaire collects the customer's own account of the accident. It helps the shop and the insurer understand how the damage happened, and it supports the repair plan. It is not a signature.

Choose **Facts of Loss** in the template list and the buttons change:

- **Copy link**: copies a link you can paste into a conversation or email.
- **Send link via text**: texts the customer the link. The button then reads **Sent**.

The link is shown below the buttons so you can see it.

### What the Customer Is Asked

The form has four sections, all on one page, and every question must be answered before the customer can submit.

| Section | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| **About you** | How should we contact you with updates (**Text**, **Email**, or **Phone call**)? How did you hear about us? |
| **The accident** | What happened? Was the vehicle **moving** or **parked or standing still**? If moving: how fast, did you feel the impact, did the vehicle slide or leave skid marks? |
| **Who was in the vehicle** | Which seats had someone in them, and which seatbelts were buckled? A seat diagram makes this easy. Only seats with someone in them can be marked buckled. |
| **Since the accident** | Have warning lights or messages appeared? Any noises or problems driving? Any custom or add-on equipment near the damage? Any paint protection film or ceramic coating? A **Tell us more** box opens for each "yes". |

### What Happens When They Submit

The customer sees **Thank you. We got your answers and added them to your repair order.** Lever turns the answers into a PDF, files it on the repair order's **Files** tab tagged **Facts of Loss**, and records it on the Activity tab.

### If the Link Does Not Work

The customer sees **This link is not working. It may have expired or already been used.** and is told to call or text the shop for a new one. Send a new link from the same tab.

<Warning>
Links are for **one customer**. Never forward a facts-of-loss or signature link to anyone else, and never paste one into a public place.
</Warning>
