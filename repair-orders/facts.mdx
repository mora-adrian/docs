The panel on the left of a full repair order lists its **facts**. Read this page once and you will know what every row means, which ones you can change, and which ones Kinetic works out for you.

## Who owns each fact

Each row is marked so you know where to change it.

| You see | It means | How to change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A small **CCC ONE** mark (hover: *From CCC ONE. Change it there and it updates here automatically.*) | CCC ONE supplies it. | In CCC ONE. There is no pencil. |
| A **pencil** | The shop owns it. | Click the pencil, change it in the small window, then choose **Save**. |
| **Calculated** in gray | Kinetic worked it out. | You can override it, and release the override later. |
| **Please enter in CCC ONE** | A CCC ONE field that is still blank. | Fill it in CCC ONE. |
| **Please fill** | A shop-owned field that is still blank. | Click the pencil. |
| **Unassigned** | Nobody is in that role. | Roles come from CCC ONE and your shop's advocate rules. |

## The facts, one by one

| Row | Source | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Repair order #** | CCC ONE | The number everyone says out loud. |
| **Claim #** | CCC ONE | The insurer's claim number. A blank value or the text **NEEDCLAIMINFO** from CCC ONE counts as "missing". |
| **Repairability** | You | **Repairable** or **Total loss**. Blank until somebody decides. See [below](#repairability). |
| **Outcome likelihood** | You or calculated | A 1 to 4 meter. See [below](#outcome-likelihood). |
| **Estimate** | CCC ONE | The estimate total. |
| **Expected insurance payout** | Calculated | A market-value range for the vehicle. See [below](#expected-insurance-payout). |
| **Start** | Kinetic | The date the repair order started. |
| **Target** | You or automatic | When the car is expected out. See [below](#target-date). |
| **Appointment**, **Arrival**, **Completed**, **Delivered** | CCC ONE | Key dates. |
| **Client Advocate** | CCC ONE and shop rules | The person who owns the customer relationship. Hover for *Client advocate · CSR / Service advisor*. |
| **Repair Planner** | CCC ONE | The person who plans the work. Hover for *Repair planner · Estimator / Production manager*. |
| **Technician** | CCC ONE | Taken from CCC ONE's labor assignments. |

### Where a person shows up and why

The **Client Advocate** and **Repair Planner** rows decide whose **Today** list a job appears on. The advocate is also the person who hears about an unhappy survey answer, and the planner is the person who gets the email when a Rivet review finishes. If a seat is empty, nobody's Today picks the job up for that role. See [Today](/work/today).

## First scan

The **first scan** (also called the **pre-repair scan**) is the diagnostic read of the vehicle's computers before any repair begins. Insurers and manufacturers expect it, and a missing one is a risk. Kinetic watches for it on vehicles being repaired.

You will see its icon on board cards, in the drawer header, and in the full-screen header:

| Icon | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Filled green** | The first scan is **done**. Hover to see how it was recorded and the date. |
| **Empty gray** | The first scan is **not done** yet. |
| **Empty and gently pulsing** | The car has reached **Disassembly & Repair Planning** with no scan. The missing scan is now the blocker. |

The drawer's **First scan** row says **Not done**, or **Done** with the source and date.

### How a scan gets recorded

The **first** of these to arrive wins the stamp. Later ones are still logged as "also confirmed by", so the history shows every source.

1. **A file tagged "Pre-repair Scan"** on the [Files](/repair-orders/files) tab.
2. **CCC ONE**, when its data shows the scan happened.
3. **A text from a technician** to the shop's assistant, in a [team chat](/ai/text-kinetic), saying the first scan is done.

<Tip>
The fastest way to turn the icon green is to upload the scan report and tag it **Pre-repair Scan**. It also gives you the document for the supplement.
</Tip>

## Target date

The **target** (also called the **out date**) is when the shop expects to hand the car back. It is one of the most scrutinized numbers on a job, so Kinetic is careful about where it comes from. It picks, in this order:

1. **A date you set by hand.** It wins over everything, until you switch back.
2. **A date CCC ONE supplied.**
3. **Kinetic's own projection.**

### Kinetic's projection

The projection starts from the **arrival date**. With no arrival date there is no projection and the target is blank.

| Situation | Projected target |
| --- | --- |
| Before **Supplement Hold** | Arrival plus the shop's **repairable cycle days** (set in [Estimating and cycle time](/admin/repair-order-settings#estimating-and-cycle-time)). |
| At **Supplement Hold** or later, and the estimate has labor hours | Arrival plus the estimate's **total labor hours ÷ labor hours per calendar day**, rounded up. |
| A **total loss** | Arrival plus the shop's **total-loss cycle days**. |

Kinetic recalculates automatically when a status changes, when the arrival date changes, and when estimate lines change. It never overwrites a date you set or one CCC ONE supplied.

### Changing it

1. Click the pencil beside **Target** (full screen only).
2. Choose a date and **Save**. The window explains: *The automatic target is arrival plus the shop's cycle days. A date set here wins over it until you switch back.*
3. To go back, open the pencil again and choose **Use automatic date**.

You can also drag the end of the **Plan** bar on the repair order's [Calendar tab](/repair-orders/calendar).

### How sure is the target date

Next to a target you may see an outlined badge reading **High confidence**, **Medium confidence**, or **Low confidence**. It compares three dates:

| Date | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Entered** | The target on the repair order. |
| **Hours per day** | Arrival plus the estimate's labor hours ÷ labor hours per day. |
| **Average cycle** | Arrival plus the shop's repairable cycle days. |

The **spread** is the number of days between the earliest and latest of the three. A tight spread means the date agrees with the estimate and with history. With the standard settings, a spread of **4 days or less** reads **High**, **7 days or less** reads **Medium**, and anything wider reads **Low**. Admins set the day counts in [Estimating and cycle time](/admin/repair-order-settings#estimating-and-cycle-time).

Hover the badge to see all three dates, with the one that sits farthest from the other two marked **outlier**, and the spread in days.

The badge appears only when it can be trusted: the vehicle is **past Parts Hold**, the target is a human or CCC ONE judgment (not Kinetic's own projection), and the estimate has labor hours.

<Note>
A **Low confidence** badge is a prompt, not a verdict. It means the date and the estimate disagree. Check whether the labor hours changed, or whether the date was set before a supplement.
</Note>

## Outcome likelihood

**Outcome likelihood** answers: *how close is the cost of this repair to what the car is worth?* A repair that costs a small fraction of the car's value is safe to repair. One that costs most of its value may end as a total loss.

It is a 1 to 4 scale, drawn as four segments.

| Level | Meter | Meaning (with standard boundaries) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | <LikelihoodMeter level={1} /> | Repair costs **under 25%** of the vehicle's value. |
| 2 | <LikelihoodMeter level={2} /> | **25–50%** of value. |
| 3 | <LikelihoodMeter level={3} /> | **50–75%** of value. |
| 4 | <LikelihoodMeter level={4} /> | **75% or more** of value. The most likely to become a total loss. |

Your admin can change the 25/50/75 boundaries in [Estimating and cycle time](/admin/repair-order-settings#estimating-and-cycle-time). Hover a meter to see the exact range for your shop.

### Calculated or set by hand

- Kinetic **calculates** the level once it knows the vehicle's market value. A gray **Calculated** label says so. It recalculates when the estimate changes.
- You can **set it yourself** with the pencil: pick a level, or **Not set**. A level you set by hand is never overwritten.
- Choose **Use calculated value** in the same window to hand control back to Kinetic.

<Info>
Outcome likelihood describes the **money**. It never decides whether a car is repairable. That is **Repairability**, below, and it is only ever set by a person or by CCC ONE.
</Info>

## Repairability

**Repairability** is a simple, honest record of reality: the car is being **Repaired** or has been declared a **Total loss**. It reads blank until somebody rules on it. An unknown never shows as "Repairable".

Click the pencil to open **Change repairability** and flip the **Total loss** toggle. The window warns: *A total loss uses the total-loss estimate and cycle days for its defaults.* The target date and the default estimate follow.

Marking a vehicle as a total loss also puts it in the **Possible Total Loss** lane on the Repairs board, and it is no longer eligible for Rivet or the customer survey. See [Total loss](/playbooks/total-loss).

## Expected insurance payout

The **Expected insurance payout** is a range of what the vehicle is worth on the market, based on recent sales, which is what an insurer is likely to value the car at if it is declared a total loss.

- It shows a range such as **$14,200 – $16,100**. Hover to see trade-in and wholesale ranges, the date, and the mileage used.
- If the value is more than 30 days old it turns gray and says **as of** with the date. It is shown, never hidden.
- **No market value yet** means it has not been looked up. **No market value for this VIN** means the lookup found nothing. **Couldn't get a market value** means the lookup failed.
- In the full screen, the circular-arrow **Refresh value** button asks for a fresh figure. Refreshes are rate-limited.
- **Vehicle value isn't set up for this shop yet** means your shop has not enabled the market-value service.

## Parties

The **Parties** section lists every person and company involved in the job.

| Row | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | The primary person or company. Pencil: **Edit**, which opens their record form. |
| **Vehicle** | Year, make, model, trim. Pencil: **Edit**. |
| **Company** | Shown when the customer is a company. |
| **Adjuster** | Shown when set. Its pencil edits the adjuster's contact record. |
| **Referral** | Where the customer heard about the shop. Comes from the customer's record. |

Under **Other parties** you can add a person or company in each of these roles: **Referrer**, **Driver**, **Attorney**, **Insurer**, **Adjuster**, **Appraiser**, and **Other**.

1. Click the pencil beside the role. The window is titled **Change referrer** (or the role you picked).
2. Pick a person or company from the search list, or choose to clear the role.
3. Save.

**Insurer** and **Adjuster** carry the **CCC ONE** mark: CCC ONE supplies them. The others are yours.

Removing a party is a protected action and may ask for a fresh authenticator code.

## The pinned photo

The photo at the top of the drawer is the repair order's **preview image**. By default Kinetic chooses one automatically. You can pin a specific one in the [Files](/repair-orders/files#preview-photo) tab.

## Do and don't

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Set a target date you believe in. Fill in **Parties** (the adjuster especially) so emails and calls land with the right person. Upload and tag the pre-repair scan as soon as it is done.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't set a target date just to make a number look good; the confidence badge will catch it. Don't set **Repairability** to total loss "to see what happens"; it moves the vehicle to another lane and ends survey eligibility.
  </Card>
</Columns>
