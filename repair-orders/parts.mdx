A repair stops when a part is missing. The **Parts** tab answers the question every customer eventually asks: *"Is my part here yet?"*

It lists every part on the estimate and shows whether it has arrived, so you can see at a glance what is holding a car up.

<Info>
Kinetic **reads** parts information from CCC ONE. It does not order parts or mark them received. Receipt is whatever CCC ONE reports. If a part has arrived but the tab still says **Outstanding**, update the receipt in CCC ONE.
</Info>

## The summary card

At the top of the tab a card summarizes receipt:

- A large percentage, such as **67%**, and **4 of 6 parts received**.
- A green progress bar that fills as parts arrive.
- **Not reported** instead of a percentage when CCC ONE has not sent any receipt information yet.

The same figure shows as **Parts received** in the [drawer](/repair-orders/drawer).

## The parts list

Below the card, a table lists **every estimate line that has a part number**.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Line** | The estimate line number. |
| **Part** | What the part is. |
| **Part #** | The part number. |
| **Qty** | How many are needed. |
| **Unit price** | The price of one. |
| **Total** | Unit price times quantity. |
| **Paint** | **Yes** when the part needs to be painted by the paint department (hover the heading for the explanation); **No** otherwise. |
| **Status** | **Received**, **Outstanding**, or **Not reported**. |

### What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Received** | CCC ONE says the part has arrived. |
| **Outstanding** | CCC ONE says the part has **not** arrived. This is the one to chase. |
| **Not reported** | CCC ONE has not sent any receipt information for this part. Treat it as unknown, not as "late". |

### Filter and sort

A row of buttons under the card, labeled **Filter parts**, narrows the list:

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every part. |
| **Outstanding** | Only parts still to come. |
| **Received** | Only parts that have arrived. |
| **Needs paint** | Only parts that need refinishing. Use it when the paint department asks what is coming. |

Click any column heading to sort. Sorting by **Status** puts the **parts you still need to chase first**, rather than sorting alphabetically.

### The totals bar

At the bottom:

- **Outstanding**: the combined value of the parts still not received. It appears only when something is outstanding.
- **Numbered parts total**: the combined value of every part in the list.

<Note>
The **Numbered parts total** here can be lower than the **Parts** figure on the [Estimate](/repair-orders/estimate) tab. This list includes only lines **with a part number**. The Estimate tab also counts part prices on lines without a number, such as fees that CCC ONE prices like a part. Hover the label for the same explanation.
</Note>

## Empty states

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **No estimate yet** | There is no estimate on the repair order, so there are no parts. |
| **No numbered parts** | There is an estimate, but none of its lines has a part number. |

Both come with the reminder: *Parts come from the CCC ONE estimate: every line with a part number shows here.*

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Before you call a customer about timing">
    Open **Parts** and choose **Outstanding**. If nothing is outstanding, the car is not waiting on parts. If something is, you have the part and line number to quote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="When a car sits in Parts Hold">
    Check **Outstanding**. Compare with your supplier order. Add a [note](/repair-orders/activity) with the ETA, and create a [task](/work/tasks) to check again on that day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="When paint asks what is coming">
    Choose **Needs paint** to see which refinish parts are still outstanding.
  </Step>
</Steps>
