The **Activity** tab is the story of a repair order, newest first. Every text, email, call, note, and status change lands here, with who did it and when. It is the first place to look when you pick up someone else's job, and the best place to leave a trail for the next person.

## The Note Box

At the top of the tab is a box that says **Write a note…**.

1. Type your note. Use <Key>⌘</Key>+<Key>Enter</Key> (<Key>Ctrl</Key>+<Key>Enter</Key> on Windows) to send, or choose **Post note**.
2. Or choose the **microphone** (**Talk to text**) and speak. It changes to **Stop and insert text** while it listens, then shows **Transcribing**, and your words appear at the cursor. A single take runs up to **two minutes**.
3. Choose **Post note**. The note is saved to the history under your name.

A note is visible to everyone in your shop who can see the repair order. It is **never** sent to the customer.

### Your Draft Is Saved for You

Below the box, a small line shows the draft's state:

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **Saving draft…** | Kinetic is saving what you typed. |
| **Draft saved** | It is safe. You can leave, close the tab, or come back later. |
| **Draft could not be saved. Your text is still here.** with **Retry** | A network hiccup. Your text is intact. Choose **Retry**. |
| A yellow box: **This draft changed on another device or tab. Autosave is paused so nothing is overwritten.** | The same note was being edited elsewhere. Choose **Keep my version** or **Use the other version**. |
| A yellow box: **This draft was deleted on another device or tab. Your text is still here.** | Choose **Keep my text** or **Clear it**. |

### What Makes a Good Note

A good note lets a teammate act without calling you. Say **what happened**, **what you decided**, **who is waiting on whom**, and **what the next step is**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="A weak note">
    "Talked to customer."
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="A strong note">
    "Called Ms. Rivera at 2:10. She approved supplement 2 (+$840, rear body panel). Insurer approved it this morning. Parts ETA is Thursday. I told her the target is Monday. Next: confirm paint code with the paint department."
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## The Feed

The feed lists each event with an icon on a vertical rail, a bold title, a time on the right, and any detail below it.

### Filter It

A row of chips narrows the feed to one kind, each showing a count:

| Chip | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Everything. |
| **Notes** | Notes people wrote, notes entered in CCC ONE, and emails filed from the mailbox. |
| **Calls** | Phone calls. |
| **Texts** | Text messages. |
| **Emails** | Email. |
| **Events** | System events: status changes, target date changes, assignments, scans, and other changes. |

Choose **Load older activity** at the bottom to go further back in time.

### What the Entries Say

| Entry | Example title |
| --- | --- |
| A note | **Alex Lee added a note** |
| A note from CCC ONE | **Leslie Chavez entered a note in CCC ONE**, or **Note entered in CCC ONE** when CCC ONE did not say who. |
| A filed email | **Email filed** or **Alex Lee filed an email**, followed by a percentage such as **· 92%** when Kinetic matched it automatically. The percentage is how sure Kinetic was that the email belongs here. |
| An email | **Inbound · Re: Supplement 2 · Mark at Example Insurance** (**Inbound**, **Outbound**, or **Draft** first). |
| A call | **Phone call from Jordan Rivera · 3:42**, with **Recording available** when it was recorded. |
| A text | The text, with who sent it and its delivery state. |
| An event | **Repair order created in Arrival & Triage**, a status change, a target-date change, a scan, and so on. |
| A CCC ONE status Kinetic could not place | **CCC status unmapped: …**. See [Statuses](/pipeline/statuses#how-statuses-change). |

<Note>
Entries are written by Kinetic as things happen. You cannot type an event or edit one after the fact, which is what makes the history trustworthy for disputes with insurers.
</Note>

## When to Write a Note, and When to Do Something Else

| You want to… | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Record a decision or a phone conversation | A **note** on Activity. |
| Tell the customer something | A **text** or **email**. |
| Promise something with a deadline | A [**task**](/work/tasks). |
| Tell a coworker something right now | A [task assigned to them](/work/tasks), or a message in a [team chat](/ai/text-kinetic). |
| Record a document | A file on the [Files](/repair-orders/files) tab. |

## Do and Don't

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Write the note while the call is fresh. Name the person you spoke to and the time. Put numbers and dates in the note. Use dictation from the shop floor.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't put passwords, full card numbers, or anything private that does not belong on a repair order. Don't use a note as a to-do. Don't write opinions about a customer you would not want read back to you.
  </Card>
</Columns>
