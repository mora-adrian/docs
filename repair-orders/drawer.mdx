The **drawer** is Kinetic's quick look. Click a card on a board, a row in a table, a line in Messages, a day on the Calendar, or a notification, and a panel slides in from the right with the essentials of that repair order. It is designed to answer "what state is this car in?" in about five seconds.

## How it behaves

- It is **read-only**. You cannot change anything by accident.
- It is **non-modal**: the screen behind stays live. Click another row and the drawer swaps to that job. The row you are looking at stays highlighted.
- Dismiss it with <Key>Esc</Key> or the **Close drawer** button (the X).
- It has **one action**: **Open full screen**, the button at the very top, which takes you to the [full repair order](/repair-orders/overview).
- It opens the same way from every screen that shows a repair order: the boards, Messages, Calendar, Notifications, CSI, Today, and the records screens.

## What is in it, top to bottom

<Steps>
  <Step title="Header">
    The **repair order number** in monospaced type, the **status pill**, and for vehicles being repaired the **first-scan icon**. Underneath, the name of the board it is on. The X closes the drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open full screen">
    A full-width button. This is the only way forward from the drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Photo and identity">
    If someone has pinned a photo, it sits at the top in a wide frame. Below it:

    - The **customer's name** (with a nickname in quotes, if there is one), their **phone** and **email**.
    - The **vehicle**: year, make, model, and trim, with its **VIN**.
    - The **insurer and claim number** on one line, when there is one.

    No fact disappears because a photo exists. See [Files](/repair-orders/files#preview-photo) for how to pin the photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Status">
    The status name with **days in this status** in brackets, a row of progress segments, and a percentage. A car past its target date turns its current segment red.

    <StatusRail current={5} total={8} />
  </Step>
  <Step title="At a glance">
    The facts that matter most, always visible:

    | Row | What it tells you |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Target out** | The date the car is expected to leave, with a confidence badge when there is one. See [Facts](/repair-orders/facts#target-date). |
    | **Parts received** | `4 of 6 received · 67%`. Shows **No numbered parts** if the estimate has none and **Not reported** if CCC ONE has not sent receipt yet. |
    | **First scan** | Repairs only. **Not done**, or **Done** with how it was recorded and the date. |
    | **Outcome likelihood** | A 1 to 4 meter. Says **Calculated** when Kinetic worked it out. |
    | **Repairability** | **Repairable**, **Total loss**, or blank while nobody has said. |
    | **Expected insurance payout** | A market-value range for the vehicle. |
    | **Client Advocate**, **Repair Planner**, **Technician** | Who is on the job, with **Unassigned** where nobody is. |
    | **Customer survey** | The current step of the satisfaction survey, when one exists. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recent activity">
    The **five newest** things that happened: notes, texts, emails, calls, and status changes, newest first. **See all activity** opens the full Activity tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="More info (collapsed)">
    Choose **More info** to reveal the customer's **Address**, the vehicle's **Odometer** and **Color**, and the rest of the facts that are not already shown above: the repair order number, claim number, estimate, start, appointment, arrival, completed, and delivered dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Parties (collapsed)">
    Choose **Parties** to see everyone else attached to the job: referrer, driver, attorney, insurer, adjuster, appraiser, and so on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Example: reading a drawer in five seconds

1. Look at the **status pill**. Is it blue (yours), amber (waiting), green (on track), or red (late)?
2. Look at **Target out** and its badge. Is the date believable?
3. Look at **Parts received**. Is that why the car is waiting?
4. Look at **First scan**. Is a scan missing?
5. Look at **Recent activity**. What was the last thing that happened, and when?

If you need to *do* anything, choose **Open full screen**.

## Marks you will see

| Mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Calculated** (gray, beside Outcome likelihood) | Kinetic computed the level from the estimate and the vehicle's market value. |
| A confidence badge beside the target date | How much to trust the date. See [Facts](/repair-orders/facts#how-sure-is-the-target-date). |
| **Past target** | Shown on lists; in the drawer the status rail turns red instead. |
| A spinner in a row | That value is still loading. |

## Things the drawer will not do

- It will not edit anything. Pencils and forms are in the full screen.
- It will not send messages. Use the **Texts** and **Emails** tabs.
- It will not navigate you away. It closes cleanly and leaves you where you were.
