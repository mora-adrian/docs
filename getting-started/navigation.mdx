Every screen in Kinetic shares the same shell: a navigation rail on the left, a toolbar with a breadcrumb at the top, and a scrollable content area. On mobile, the rail becomes a drawer behind an app bar.

## The sidebar

The rail is organized into groups. Each item shows a count badge when there is something that needs attention.

| Group | Items |
| --- | --- |
| *(top)* | **Today**, **Notifications**, **Tasks** |
| **Communications** | **Messages**, **Calendar**, **CSI**, and **Email review** (visible only after a mailbox is connected) |
| **Pipeline** | **Opportunities**, **Repairs**, **Close & Deliver**, **Enrichment** — each with a live count |
| **Records** | **People**, **Companies**, **Vehicles** |
| **Operations** | **Agent**, **Rivet** |
| **Recent** | Repair orders you opened recently, for one-click return |

The sidebar collapses to icons. The shop name sits at the top; the account menu at the bottom shows your name, role, help actions (report a problem, suggest a feature), and sign-out.

## The command palette

Press `Cmd + K` (Mac) or `Ctrl + K` (Windows), or select **Search** in the sidebar. The palette searches repair orders, people, companies, and vehicles, and offers navigation to every screen and settings section.

- Results respect your shop and your permissions.
- Use arrow keys to move, `Return` to open, `Esc` to dismiss.
- The palette also handles create actions — new repair order, new task — without leaving the keyboard.

## Addresses and shops

- `center.collisionpartners.app` — the company hub and shop chooser.
- `your-shop.collisionpartners.app` — your shop's workspace. Bookmark this.

A repair order's full-screen page lives at `/repair-orders/<id>`; every other screen lives at a clean path like `/messages` or `/repair`. The drawer peeks at a record without navigating; the full screen is the permanent address you can share with a teammate in the same shop.

## Toolbar and breadcrumb

Every screen starts with a breadcrumb in the toolbar. Record screens show the repair order number and customer. The row of tabs or actions under the header is specific to the screen — the sidebar and palette are always the way out.
