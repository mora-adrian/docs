---
title: "Roles & permissions"
description: "Who can do what in Kinetic — owners, admins, members, and the extra verification required for sensitive actions."
---

Access in Kinetic is based on **membership**: an explicit, audited link between your account and a specific shop (or the parent company). Membership decides what you can see and do. There are no share links, no temporary access, and no way to view another shop's data — a record in a different shop is indistinguishable from one that does not exist.

## Membership roles

| Role | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can do, plus ownership-sensitive actions such as transferring or deleting shop configuration. Every shop must keep at least one owner — Kinetic blocks removing or demoting the last one. |
| **Admin** | Day-to-day administration: manage members, shop settings, messaging, integrations, and workflow statuses, and archive or delete records. Cannot transfer ownership or act in other shops. |
| **Member** | Normal operational work: create and update repair orders, customers, vehicles, messages, tasks, notes, files, and statuses. Cannot manage members, change integrations or security settings, or archive and delete. |
| **Company owner** | For multi-shop organizations. Audited administration across the company's shops, entered deliberately from the company hub. |

<Info>
Your job title in the employee directory — technician, estimator, and so on — is informational. Permissions come from your membership role, not your job title.
</Info>

## Verification for sensitive actions

Some actions are marked **sensitive**: managing members and roles, changing credentials or integrations, and destructive operations. When you start one, Kinetic asks for a fresh code from your authenticator app, even though you are already signed in. This verification lasts 240 minutes, so you will not be asked repeatedly during an admin session.

## Choosing the right role

| Person | Recommended role |
| --- | --- |
| Front office, estimators, CSRs (customer service representatives), technicians, parts staff | Member |
| Shop manager, office manager | Admin |
| Shop owner, general manager | Owner — keep it to one or two people |

<Tip>
Use the least privilege that lets someone do their job. It is easier to promote a person later than to undo a deleted record. Read access is not restricted by role — a Member can still view dashboards and reports.
</Tip>

## Changing or removing access

Admins change roles and remove members in **Settings → Members**. Removing a member ends that person's access to your shop. It does not affect their memberships in other shops, and it does not delete their work — the notes, messages, and history they created stay attributed to them.
