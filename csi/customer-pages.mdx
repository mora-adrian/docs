Customers never sign in to Kinetic. Everything they do happens on a **private link** you send by text. This page shows what is on the other end of those links, so you can walk a customer through them over the phone.

<Note>
Every customer link is **personal and unguessable**. It works for that one customer and that one purpose. It carries no login and no other customers' data.
</Note>

## At a Glance

| Link | Who sends it | How it is sent | How long it works |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Satisfaction survey** | Kinetic, automatically | Text, about two business days after the pulse check | 30 days |
| **Facts of loss** | You | **Send link via text**, or **Copy link** | Until used or expired |
| **Signature request** | You | **Send via text** | Until signed, declined, or expired |

## The Satisfaction Survey

This is the full survey described in [CSI](/csi/overview). It opens as a single, mobile-friendly page with the shop's name on it.

### What the Customer Sees

<Steps>
  <Step title="A loading moment">
    The page says **Loading your survey…** for a second.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A short greeting">
    It names the customer by first name, the shop, and the vehicle, so they know which repair it is about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Five questions rated 1 to 5">
    Each is a row of numbered buttons. The left end reads **Very dissatisfied** and the right end reads **Very satisfied**.

    | Question |
    | --- |
    | Overall, how satisfied are you with your repair experience? |
    | How satisfied are you with the quality of the repair? |
    | How well did we keep you informed? |
    | How satisfied are you with how long the repair took? |
    | How was your vehicle's condition and cleanliness when you picked it up? |
  </Step>
  <Step title="One recommendation question rated 0 to 10">
    **How likely are you to recommend us to a friend or family member?** The ends read **Not at all likely** and **Extremely likely**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="An optional comment">
    **Anything else you'd like us to know?** Up to 2,000 characters. This is the only question the customer can skip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    **Submit my answers** stays unavailable until all six rated questions have an answer. The page says **Please answer every question, then submit.** if the customer tries too early.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What Happens Next

The customer sees **Thank you** and **We got your answers and shared them with the team.** Their answers appear on the repair order's **Customer survey** checklist and on the [CSI screen](/csi/overview#responded).

### Messages a Customer May See

| Message | What it means | What to tell them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **This survey link is no longer active** with *It may have expired or already been used.* | The 30-day window passed, or the link was already used. | Thank them. If they still want to give feedback, take it by phone and add a note on the repair order. |
| **We already received your answers — nothing more to do.** | They submitted twice. | Nothing. Their first submission was kept. |
| **Couldn't load your survey** | The page could not reach the server. | Ask them to reload, or try again in a minute. |
| **Couldn't reach the server. Your answers are safe; try again.** | A temporary connection problem during submission. | Their answers are still on the screen. Ask them to tap **Submit my answers** again. |
| **We could not save your answers.** or **Submission failed.** | The submission was rejected. | Ask them to try again; if it keeps failing, take their feedback by phone. |
| **This link is no longer valid.** | The link was mistyped or cut off in the text. | Ask them to tap the link in the original text rather than copying it. |

<Tip>
A survey link cannot be re-sent from the CSI screen once it expires. If a customer asks for a new one, record their feedback by phone and add it as a **note** on the repair order so it is not lost.
</Tip>

## The Facts-of-loss Form

You send this from the repair order's **Signatures** tab. The customer gets a text with a link and fills in the form on their phone.

### Layout

One page with four sections, in this order:

1. **About you**: preferred contact method and how they heard about the shop.
2. **The accident**: what happened, and whether the vehicle was moving.
3. **Who was in the vehicle**: a seat diagram, with seatbelts.
4. **Since the accident**: warning lights, noises, add-on equipment, and protective coatings.

Every question is required. Questions that depend on an earlier answer appear only when they apply: the speed, impact, and skid questions show only if the vehicle was **moving**, and **Tell us more** boxes open when an answer is **Yes**.

The full list of questions is in [Signatures and facts of loss](/repair-orders/signatures#what-the-customer-is-asked).

### After They Submit

- The customer sees **Thank you. We got your answers and added them to your repair order.**
- Kinetic builds a **PDF** of the answers and files it on the repair order's **Files** tab tagged **Facts of Loss**.
- An entry appears on the **Activity** tab.

### If the Link Fails

The customer sees **This link is not working. It may have expired or already been used.** and is told to call or text the shop. Send a fresh link from the **Signatures** tab.

## Signature Requests

When you choose **Send via text** on a contract, the customer receives a text from the shop's number with a link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="They open the link">
    The document opens on their phone with their name, vehicle, and claim details already filled in. Your Signatures list changes to **Viewed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They read and sign">
    They review the document and sign with a finger on the screen. No account or app is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They finish">
    Your list changes to **Signed**, and the signed copy is filed on the repair order's **Files** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If they refuse, the request shows **Declined**. If they wait too long, it shows **Expired**, and you send a new request.

### Helping a Customer Who Is Stuck

- **They did not get the text.** Check that the mobile number on the customer record is correct and the customer is not marked **Do not text**. Ask them to look for a message from an unfamiliar number.
- **The link does nothing on a computer.** Links are designed for phones, but any modern browser works. Ask them to open it on the phone that received it.
- **They want to read it first.** Use **Preview** on your side and read the document to them, or tell them they can take their time. Nothing is binding until they sign.
- **They cannot find the text later.** Send a new request. Do not send the same link by email unless the customer asked for that.

## Privacy Rules for All Customer Links

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Send links only to the customer's own phone number. Tell the customer to expect a text from the shop. Re-send when a link has expired.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't paste links into group chats or public places. Don't forward a link to another person. Don't ask a customer to read their answers back to you in place of using the form.
  </Card>
</Columns>
