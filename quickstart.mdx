## 1. Get invited

Access is by invitation only. A shop owner or admin adds you in **Settings → Members**. You'll receive an email to set your password.

<Note>
If your invite doesn't arrive, check spam, then ask your admin to resend it. Invites are how your account is linked to the right shop — never share sign-up links.
</Note>

## 2. Sign in

Go to your shop's address — it looks like `your-shop.collisionpartners.app` — or start at `center.collisionpartners.app` and pick your shop.

1. Enter your work email and password.
2. On first sign-in you'll be asked to enroll an **authenticator app** (1Password, Authy, Google Authenticator, etc.). Scan the QR code, then enter the code it shows.
3. Save your recovery codes somewhere safe — they're your way back in if you lose your phone.

<Warning>
CPBMS requires an authenticator app code on every account. Sessions expire after 4 hours of inactivity and always end 12 hours after sign-in, so expect to sign in again each morning.
</Warning>

## 3. Find your way around

- **Navigation rail (desktop):** collapsible sidebar with all sections. The shop name and your name/role show at the bottom.
- **Mobile:** app bar with a navigation drawer — everything except heavy admin work is supported on the floor.
- **Command palette:** press `Cmd/Ctrl + K` to search records, jump to any screen, or create things without touching the mouse.

## 4. Your first day checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Today">
    Start on the **Today** screen. It shows repair orders assigned to you, tasks due, and items that need attention.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk the boards">
    Open **Repair** and scan every vehicle in the shop. Click a card for the quick-peek drawer; open the full record for detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Messages">
    The **Messages** inbox holds inbound texts and emails, including items that need triage. Reply from here — everything is saved to the customer and repair order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update one repair order">
    Change a status, add a note, and upload a photo on any active repair order. If you can do those three things, you can do 90% of daily work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up notifications">
    In **Settings → Preferences**, choose what you want to be notified about and how.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If you get stuck

- **"You do not have permission"** — the action needs a higher role or a recent authenticator check. See [Roles & permissions](/getting-started/roles).
- **Asked for an authenticator app code mid-task** — sensitive actions (changing members, integrations, deleting) require a fresh code. That's normal.
- **Nothing loads** — check your internet, then ask your admin; a membership problem looks different from an outage.
