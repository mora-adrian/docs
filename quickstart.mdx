This page takes you from invitation to productive first day. It takes about 15 minutes.

## Before you begin

You need two things:

- **An invitation.** A shop owner or admin adds you in **Settings → Members**. Kinetic emails you a link to set your password. Access is by invitation only.
- **An authenticator app** on your phone, such as 1Password, Authy, or Google Authenticator. Kinetic requires one for every account.

<Note>
If your invitation does not arrive, check your spam folder, then ask your admin to resend it. Never share invitation links — they connect your account to the correct shop.
</Note>

## Sign in for the first time

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your shop's address">
    It looks like `your-shop.collisionpartners.app`. If you do not know it, go to `center.collisionpartners.app` and pick your shop after sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your work email and password" />
  <Step title="Enroll your authenticator app">
    Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, then type the 6-digit code it shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery codes">
    Store them somewhere safe and offline. They are your only way back in if you lose your phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Sessions expire after 4 hours of inactivity and always end 12 hours after sign-in. Expect to sign in about once per workday.
</Warning>

## Find your way around

- **Desktop:** a collapsible navigation rail lists every section. Your shop, name, and role appear at the bottom.
- **Mobile:** an app bar opens a navigation drawer. Day-to-day work — lookups, messages, photos, status updates — works on the floor.
- **Command palette:** press `Cmd + K` (Mac) or `Ctrl + K` (Windows) to search records, jump to any screen, or create items without the mouse.

## Your first-day checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Today">
    The **Today** screen shows the repair orders assigned to you, tasks due, and items that need attention.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk the Repair board">
    Scan every vehicle in the shop. Click a card for the quick-peek drawer; open the full record for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Messages">
    The **Messages** inbox holds inbound texts and emails, including items that need triage. Reply from here — every message is saved to the customer and the repair order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update one repair order">
    Change a status, add a note, and upload a photo on an active repair order. These three actions cover most daily work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your notifications">
    In **Settings → Preferences**, choose what you want to be notified about and how.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common problems

| Problem | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "You do not have permission" | The action needs a higher role or a fresh authenticator check. | See [Roles & permissions](/getting-started/roles), or ask your admin. |
| You are asked for an authenticator app code mid-task | Sensitive actions (members, integrations, deletes) need a fresh code. | Enter the code — this is normal, not an error. |
| Nothing loads | Your connection, your membership, or a service issue. | Check your internet first, then ask your admin. A membership problem looks different from an outage. |
