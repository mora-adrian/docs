A lot of claim work happens in email that does not start in Lever: an adjuster writes to your inbox about a vehicle, a parts vendor sends a confirmation, an insurer attaches an approval. **Email review** brings those emails to the right repair order so they are not trapped in one person's mailbox.

## How It Works

1. You **connect your work mailbox** once (Google or Microsoft). Lever reads it, **read-only**. It never sends from it, never deletes, and never changes anything in it.
2. Lever looks for claim-related email and tries to match each one to a repair order using the claim number, the repair order number, the vehicle, and the people involved.
3. When it is **confident**, it files the email onto that repair order. It shows on the Activity tab as **Email filed**, with a percentage showing how sure Lever was.
4. When it is **not sure**, it puts the email in your **Email review** queue and asks you.

<Info>
It is **your own** mailbox only. Each person who wants this connects their own. Nobody else's mail is read.
</Info>

## Connect Your Mailbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Preferences">
    Go to **Settings → Preferences** and find **Connected mailbox**: *Incoming email matched to repair orders — your mailbox only*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your provider">
    Choose **Connect Google** or **Connect Microsoft**. You are taken to the provider to sign in and allow read-only access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come back">
    A green check says **Mailbox connected.** If it says **Mailbox connection did not finish — try again**, repeat the steps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once you are connected, **Email review** appears in the sidebar under **Communications**, with a red number when emails are waiting. Until you connect, the item does not appear at all.

If your mailbox is disconnected, **Settings → Preferences** says **Your mailbox is disconnected.** and offers the connect buttons again. To disconnect on purpose, choose **Disconnect** and confirm: *Lever stops watching this mailbox and emails are no longer filed. Emails already filed to repair orders stay in place.* Disconnecting is a protected action and asks for a fresh authenticator code.

## The Queue

Open **Email review**. Each waiting email is a row:

- The **subject** (or *(no subject)*).
- The **sender** (or **Unknown sender**), when it arrived, and a **confidence** percentage if Lever made a guess.
- A three-line **excerpt**.
- The **reasons** it was not filed automatically.
- A badge: **Suggested** with a link to a repair order, or **No suggestion**.

### Your Three Choices

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **File to 24-1087** (appears when there is a suggestion) | Files the email onto the suggested repair order. |
| **Choose…** | Opens a picker. Search for the right repair order, select it, and choose **File email**. |
| **Dismiss** | Removes it from your queue without filing. Use it for anything that is not claim work: newsletters, spam, personal mail. |

When you file an email, it joins that repair order's history, along with its attachments, and a note on Activity says it was filed by you.

<Note>
Emails are filed to **repair orders only**. If an email is about a customer you have no repair order for, dismiss it and handle it elsewhere.
</Note>

### An Empty Queue

**Nothing to review**: *Emails that can't be matched with confidence wait here. You're all caught up.* That is the goal state.

If you have no mailbox connected you see **No mailbox connected**: *Connect a mailbox in Settings and incoming claim emails show up here*, with **Open mailbox settings**.

## Make It a Habit

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Clear the queue every day. File a suggestion if it is right; fix it with **Choose…** if it is wrong. Dismiss junk so it does not pile up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't ignore the red number. An unfiled email from an adjuster is an unanswered adjuster. Don't dismiss something you are unsure about; file it to the closest repair order and add a note.
  </Card>
</Columns>
