Most customers prefer to be texted. In Lever, every text to and from a customer is part of the repair order, so the next person to pick up the job sees the whole conversation. You text from the **Texts** tab of a repair order, or from [Messages](/communicate/messages) once a conversation belongs to a job. Both show the same composer.

<Info>
Texts go out on the **shop's own texting line**, as **iMessage** where the customer's phone supports it and as regular **text (SMS)** otherwise. Customers see one consistent shop number, never your personal one. Admins can see the number in **Settings → Messaging**.
</Info>

<Warning>
**A customer must text your shop first, or already have an open thread, before you can reply.** If you try to start a conversation and Lever says **Message could not be sent. The contact may need to text your shop number first.**, that is why. Ask the customer to text the shop, or call or email them.
</Warning>

## Reading the Thread

- Messages are drawn like iMessage: **yours on the right in blue**, **the customer's on the left in gray**. AI-assistant output appears as plain text.
- Date markers (**Today**, **Yesterday**, a weekday, then a date) divide the days.
- Choose **Load older** at the top to go further back.
- A **typing** bubble appears while the customer is typing.
- Pictures, videos, audio, and files show inline. Click one to open it. Audio plays in place.
- **No messages yet** appears on a new thread, with **Send the first text to start this conversation.**

### Delivery Status

Right-click a message and choose **Info** to see **Message info**: when it was **Received**, who **Sent by**, the **Service** (iMessage or SMS), and its **Status**:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Sending** | On its way out. A new message reads **Awaiting confirmation** until the carrier answers. |
| **Sent** | Left Lever. |
| **Delivered** | Reached the customer's phone. |
| **Read** | The customer opened it (iMessage only). |
| **Received** | An incoming message. |
| **Failed** or **Bounced** | It did not arrive. Try again, or call. |

### Reactions

Hover over a message (or tap, on a phone) for the reaction button. It opens a row of **tapbacks**: **Love**, **Like**, **Dislike**, **Laugh**, **Emphasize**, and **Question**. Reactions work on iMessage conversations. Yours appear in blue on the bubble's bottom edge.

Right-click a message to **Copy** its text.

## The Composer

The composer is one bordered card. The text box sits on top, and a row of buttons runs along the bottom.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Add attachment** | Attach images, video, audio, PDFs, or contact cards from your device. |
| **Attach from repair order files** | Pick files already on the job. |
| **Emoji keyboard** | A searchable emoji picker. |
| **Improve with AI** | Rewrites your draft for clarity and tone. See [below](#improve-with-ai). |
| **Talk to text** | Dictate instead of typing. See [below](#talk-to-text-and-voice-memos). |
| **Record voice memo** | Send a recorded message as audio. |
| **Send** (round arrow) | Sends. <Key>⌘</Key>+<Key>Enter</Key> (<Key>Ctrl</Key>+<Key>Enter</Key> on Windows) does the same. |

You can attach **up to 10** items to one message. Sending several attachments over SMS is less reliable than over iMessage, and a line under the box warns you: **Attachment delivery over SMS/MMS isn't guaranteed.** or **Some carriers may not deliver multiple attachments over SMS/MMS.**

You cannot mix a voice memo with other attachments. Lever tells you: **Remove the voice memo before attaching files.**

### Templates

Type `@` in the box and a menu of your shop's **message templates** opens, each with its name and a two-line preview. Use <Key>↑</Key> and <Key>↓</Key> to move, <Key>Enter</Key> or <Key>Tab</Key> to choose, <Key>Esc</Key> to close.

When you pick one, **variables fill in with this customer's details**:

| Variable | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `$CUSTOMER_NAME` | The customer's name. |
| `$VEHICLE_DESCRIPTION` | Year, make, and model. |
| `$RO_NUMBER` | The repair order number. |
| `$TARGET_DATE` | The target date. |
| `$APPOINTMENT_DATE` | The appointment date. |
| `$ADVOCATE_NAME` | The client advocate's name. |
| `$BRAND_NAME` | Your shop's name. |
| `$TIME_OF_DAY_GREETING` | "Good morning", "Good afternoon", or "Good evening". |

Read the message before you send it. If a variable shows as `$SOMETHING` with nothing filled in, that fact is missing on the repair order. Fix it, or write the sentence yourself.

Admins create and edit templates under **Settings → Messaging → Message templates**. See [Messaging settings](/admin/messaging-settings).

### Improve with AI

**Improve with AI** (the magic-wand button) tidies your draft: clearer, better grammar, more professional. It is deliberately limited:

- It keeps **your meaning and your level of detail**.
- It **will not add** dates, times, prices, ETAs, promises, coverage statements, links, emojis, greetings, or sign-offs you did not write.
- It **keeps every `$VARIABLE`** exactly as it was.
- It aims to stay near your original length.

If it fails, you see **Could not improve this message. Try again.** and your draft is untouched. It only works on text messages.

<Note>
You are still the author. Read the result before you send it. Improve with AI can make wording better; it cannot make a wrong statement right.
</Note>

### Talk to Text and Voice Memos

- **Talk to text** (the person-with-sound-waves button) lets you speak. The button turns into **Stop and insert text** while it listens, and **Transcribing** while it works. The words appear in the box at your cursor. One take is capped at **two minutes**.
- **Record voice memo** (the waveform button) swaps the composer for a live waveform. **Stop voice memo** finishes it, and it is sent as an audio message.

Your browser asks for microphone permission the first time. If you refuse, you will see **Microphone access was denied. Allow it in the browser to record a voice memo.**

### Drafts Are Saved

What you type is saved automatically (**Draft saved** appears below the box), and it stays on the repair order's thread. If someone else edits the same draft, Lever shows both versions and lets you choose **Keep my version** or **Use the other version**.

### Saving a Customer's Photos

Right-click an incoming picture or video and choose **Save to repair order files**, or use the link under the attachment. It files onto the repair order's **Files** tab, and you see **Saved to repair order files**.

## Chat Health and Line Reputation

Texting a person who does not want to hear from you is a bad idea for them and for your texting line. The header of every thread carries up to three live readings, so you know before you press **Send**.

### Conversation Sentiment

An AI reading of how the customer feels: **Concerned**, **Negative**, **Neutral**, **Positive**, or **Delighted**. It shows a **score from 0 to 100**, a **trend** (improving, steady, declining), a one-line summary, and the **signals** behind it, such as *Customer waiting 3 h for a reply* or *4 texts in a row without a reply*.

It appears after the next analysis run. Until then you see **No sentiment reading yet — it appears after the next analysis run.**

### Chat Health

How this conversation is doing, according to the texting network:

| Reading | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Healthy** | The customer is engaging normally. | Carry on. |
| **At risk** | The customer may be cooling off. | Slow down. Send messages that invite a reply. |
| **Critical** | The conversation is in trouble. | Pause routine texts. Have a **person** reach out directly. |
| **Opted out** | The customer texted **STOP**. | **Sending is blocked** until they text back. |
| **Unknown** | No reading yet. | Nothing special. |

### Line Reputation

How the shop's texting line stands with the phone carriers:

| Reading | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Healthy** | In good standing. | Carry on. |
| **At risk** | Carriers may start filtering the line. | Send less. Wait for replies. |
| **Critical** | The line is probably being filtered. | Pause non-essential texting. |
| **Unknown** | No reading yet. | Nothing special. |

A small badge near the top shows the line's connection and reputation together.

### Warnings in the Composer

When readings get worse, the composer warns you with specifics, for example *You've sent 4 texts without a reply* or *Line reputation is slipping. Send less and wait for replies.* An **opted-out** customer is a hard stop: sending is blocked, not just discouraged.

## When You Cannot Send

| You see | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **{Name} is flagged do not text** (with the reason) | Someone marked this person **Do not text** on their record. Texts are blocked. Email and calls still work. See [People](/records/people#contact-preferences). |
| **The customer opted out of texts (STOP). Sending is blocked until they text back.** | They replied STOP. Respect it. |
| **Messaging is no longer configured for this shop** | The shop's text line is down. Tell your admin. |
| The **Send** button is disabled | The box is empty, an attachment is still uploading, or the customer has no mobile number. |

## Good Habits

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Text from here, not your phone. Use a template for routine updates and edit the details. Keep texts short and specific: what happened, what happens next, and when. Check the health strip before a long message.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't promise a date you have not confirmed. Don't text a customer who said stop. Don't send five texts in a row to a customer who has not replied. Don't discuss money or coverage in a text if a call would be clearer.
  </Card>
</Columns>
