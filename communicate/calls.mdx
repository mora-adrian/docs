Phone calls are still how many insurers and customers prefer to talk. Kinetic connects to the shop's phone system so that calls leave a record on the repair order, and so that the person answering the phone knows who is calling before they say hello.

<Note>
**Kinetic does not place calls.** There is no dial button. Your phone system handles every call. Kinetic does two things around it: it shows your staff **who is calling** the moment the phone rings, and it keeps a **full record of the calls its AI receptionist handles**. For a call a person takes, write a [note](/repair-orders/activity) on the repair order so the conversation is on file.
</Note>

## The Calls Tab

Open a repair order and choose **Calls**. It lists the calls recorded for that job, most often those the AI receptionist handled (see [below](#the-ai-receptionist)). Each call is a row:

- An arrow icon for **Incoming** or **Outgoing**, and a phone or video icon for **Phone call** or **Video call**.
- Who the call was with, how long it lasted, and when.
- A **Play recording** button when it was recorded. It changes to **Pause recording** while playing.
- **Show details**, which opens (and **Hide details** closes) three parts:

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Disposition** | How the call ended: answered, missed, voicemail, and so on. |
| **Summary** | A short, AI-written account of what the call was about. |
| **Transcript** | The words spoken, written out. |

An empty tab says **No calls logged**: *Calls placed or received on this repair order will appear here.*

Calls also appear in the [Activity feed](/repair-orders/activity) as **Phone call from Jordan Rivera · 3:42**, with **Recording available** when there is a recording.

<Tip>
Use the summary and transcript to prepare for a return call, and to settle "I never said that" disagreements. Don't paste a transcript into a message to a customer without reading it first.
</Tip>

## Screen Pops

When the shop phone rings, a small card appears in the **top right of your screen**, wherever you are in Kinetic, if the call is for you.

- The title reads **Incoming call · Jordan Rivera**, with the caller's name, or **Incoming call · (704) 555-0100** when the number is not recognized.
- If the number matches a person or company, you see a button for each of their **open repair orders**. Click one to open that job's drawer.
- If several records share the number, each is listed with its name.
- If the number is unknown, a **Create contact** button opens a small form prefilled with the number, so you can start the record while you talk.
- When someone answers the call, an **Answered** badge appears. The card then clears itself after a short while.
- The **X** (**Dismiss call**) closes it sooner.

A pop appears for **the person whose phone extension rang**. Your shop's admin links each member to their extension. If you never see pops, ask your admin to check that you have an extension in **Settings → Shop**. See [Shop settings](/admin/shop-settings#phone-system).

## The AI Receptionist

Your shop can have an **AI receptionist** answer inbound calls. When it is on, it:

1. **Works out who is calling.** A number on file is recognized. For a number Kinetic does not know, it asks for details and treats the person as unverified.
2. **Finds the repair order.** It matches what the caller says (repair order number, VIN, claim number, last name, vehicle) to a file. For an unknown caller, **at least two independent details must agree** with one file before it will say anything about that file. If the details point to several files, it asks for one more detail and never lists names or vehicles aloud.
3. **Routes the call during business hours** to the right person: the file's client advocate for questions about a job, accounting for payments and paperwork, parts for parts questions, a manager for escalations, or everyone at once when there is no clearer answer.
4. **Helps after hours**, answering questions about a car that is in the shop, and **records a callback** when nobody can help.
5. **Leaves a trail.** The call appears on the repair order's **Calls** tab with a summary and transcript, and you are notified.

When it takes a call you will see a notification marked **Call answered by the AI**. When it cannot help, a **callback** is recorded as a notification for the client advocate and as a task with a due date at the next time the shop is open.

### What It Will Never Do

- It will not give out details about a file to someone who has not matched it.
- It will not make up a phone number, extension, or person. Every destination comes from your employee directory.
- It will not make promises about coverage, liability, or delivery dates.
- It never dials a number it was not given.

### Business Hours and Holidays

When the receptionist routes calls it uses your shop's **business hours**, set in **Settings → Shop**. It treats the days you mark as closed (and, if you turn it on, the major holidays) as after hours, and tells the caller why.

<Info>
If you would rather not have the AI answer, ask your admin or the Kinetic team to turn it off. Without it, calls ring through to staff as usual and still show screen pops and logs.
</Info>

## Calls to a Person Who Is Out

If you set days off in **Settings → Preferences → Schedule**, and the AI is allowed to cover for you, a customer who texts you on those days gets one friendly reply saying you are out and when you will be back. See [Your preferences](/admin/personal-preferences#your-schedule).
