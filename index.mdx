CPBMS is the operating system for your collision center. It brings repair orders, customers, vehicles, claims, communications, scheduling, signatures, customer surveys, and AI-powered estimate review into one place — so your team stops switching between spreadsheets, sticky notes, and a dozen apps.

## Key concepts

| Concept | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Shop** | Your collision center. All of your data — customers, repair orders, messages — belongs to exactly one shop and is never visible to another. |
| **Repair order** | A single repair job on one vehicle. It keeps the same ID from first opportunity through delivery and beyond. |
| **Status** | The workflow column a repair order sits in (for example: Opportunity → Estimate → Authorized → In repair → QC → Ready → Delivered). Your admin configures the exact statuses for your shop. |
| **Lifecycle board** | The four big buckets of work: **Opportunity**, **Repair**, **Close**, and **Enrichment**. Every repair order lives on one of them. |
| **Phase** | Where the vehicle is right now. Status drives the boards; lifecycle is derived from it. |
| **Membership** | Your access. You sign in once; what you can see and do depends on your membership and role in each shop. |

## How access works

You sign in with your work email and an authenticator app code. If you belong to one shop, you land directly in its workspace. If you belong to several, you pick a shop from the company hub. There is no "location switcher" inside the app — switching shops is a deliberate step, which keeps every shop's data isolated.

## Where things live

- **Today** — your personal command center: what's due, what's blocked, what needs you.
- **Opportunity / Repair / Close / Enrichment** — the lifecycle boards.
- **Repair orders** — search, filter, and open any job.
- **People / Companies / Vehicles** — the records behind every job.
- **Messages** — the shared inbox for text and email, plus call history.
- **Calendar** — appointments, drops, pickups, and route planning.
- **CSI** — customer satisfaction surveys and responses.
- **Rivet** — AI estimate review.
- **Agent** — ask questions and get help drafting in plain language.
- **Settings** — shop configuration, members, messaging, and integrations.
