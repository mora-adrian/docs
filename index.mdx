Kinetic is where your shop runs. Every repair, every customer, every text and email, every appointment and every decision about a vehicle lives in one place, so nobody has to dig through sticky notes, spreadsheets, or someone's personal phone to find out what is going on.

This guide is the full manual. It is written so that someone who has never worked in a body shop, and never used software like this, can follow it from the first sign-in. It also goes deep, because most of what makes Kinetic useful is in the small features: the dot on a card that says a scan is missing, the badge that says a target date is shaky, the switch that follows up with an adjuster for you.

<Note>
**How current is this guide?** It was rewritten in October 2026 and checked against the app itself, screen by screen. Where Kinetic behaves in a way you might not expect, this guide says so plainly.
</Note>

## Three ideas that explain most of Kinetic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Repair orders come from CCC ONE">
    Your estimating system, CCC ONE, creates and updates repair orders in Kinetic. You do not type a new repair order in. What you do is work it: move it along, talk to the customer, track the money, and keep it honest. [How that works](/start/how-kinetic-thinks).
  </Step>
  <Step title="A status tells everyone what is happening">
    Every repair order has one status, such as <Pill tone="waiting">Parts Hold</Pill> or <Pill tone="ontrack">Paint</Pill>. The color dot says whether the ball is in your court, someone else's, or the car is late. [All statuses explained](/pipeline/statuses).
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI drafts, people decide">
    Kinetic can summarize, suggest, and draft. It never decides coverage, never promises a delivery date, and never sends a message to a customer unless a person approves it. [What AI will and won't do](/ai/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Conventions in this guide

- **Bold** is a label you can see on screen: click **Settings**.
- <Key>⌘</Key> <Key>K</Key> means press those keys together. On Windows, use <Key>Ctrl</Key> instead of <Key>⌘</Key>.
- A <Pill tone="needs">colored pill</Pill> in the text is a status, drawn the way the app draws it.
- Boxes labeled **Do**, **Don't**, and **Good to know** are the rules of the road. Skim them even if you skip the rest.
- Examples use made-up names and numbers. They are illustrations, never real customers.
- "Shop" means your collision center. Kinetic calls it a shop everywhere.

## Need a person?

Open the shop menu at the top of the left sidebar and choose **Support** to email the Kinetic team. Use **Report a problem** or **Suggest a feature** from the same menu to send details straight from the screen you are on.
