Kinetic is Collision Partners' collision repair management operating system. It puts repair orders, customers, vehicles, claims, communications, scheduling, signatures, customer surveys, and AI-powered estimate review in one place, so your team works from one system instead of spreadsheets, sticky notes, and separate apps.

This guide explains how to use Kinetic. It is written for everyone in the shop: front-office staff, estimators, technicians, parts staff, managers, and owners.

## Key concepts

These terms appear throughout Kinetic and this guide.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Shop** | Your collision center. All of your data — customers, repair orders, messages — belongs to exactly one shop and is never visible to another shop. |
| **Repair order** | One repair job on one vehicle. A repair order keeps the same ID from first contact through delivery and beyond. |
| **Status** | The workflow step a repair order is in — for example: Opportunity → Estimate → Authorized → In repair → QC → Ready → Delivered. Your administrator configures the exact statuses for your shop. |
| **Lifecycle board** | One of the four boards that group repair orders by where they are in their journey: **Opportunity**, **Repair**, **Close**, and **Enrichment**. |
| **Membership** | Your access. You sign in once. What you can see and do depends on your membership and role in each shop. |

## How access works

You sign in with your work email, a password, and a code from an authenticator app. If you belong to one shop, you go straight to its workspace. If you belong to more than one, you pick a shop from the company hub. There is no shop switcher inside the workspace — switching shops is a deliberate step, which keeps each shop's data isolated.

## Where things are

| Screen | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | Your daily command center: what is due, what is blocked, what needs you. |
| **Opportunity / Repair / Close / Enrichment** | The four lifecycle boards. |
| **Repair orders** | Search, filter, and open any job. |
| **People / Companies / Vehicles** | The records behind every job. |
| **Messages** | The shared inbox for texts and email, plus call history. |
| **Calendar** | Appointments, drop-offs, pickups, and route planning. |
| **CSI** | Customer satisfaction surveys and responses. |
| **Rivet** | AI estimate review. |
| **Agent** | Ask questions and get help drafting in plain language. |
| **Settings** | Shop configuration, members, messaging, and integrations. |
