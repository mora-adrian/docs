The **Calendar** is the shop's logistics board. It answers: *what cars are coming in, going out, or travelling to a vendor, on which day, and who is moving them?* Open it from **Communications → Calendar**.

It is separate from a repair order's own [Calendar tab](/repair-orders/calendar), which plans one job. This one is for the whole shop.

## Layout

At the top, the toolbar has:

- **Week | Month**, a switch for the **Calendar view**. Week shows seven days; Month shows the whole month and also adds milestone chips for repair-order arrival and delivery dates.
- **Previous week/month** and **Next week/month** arrows, and a **Today** button.
- **New appointment**, which opens the scheduling form.

Each day is a cell with small **chips**, one per appointment, showing the time and a short title. If more appointments exist than fit, a chip reads **+3 more**; click the day to see them all.

Click a day and the **day panel** opens beside (or below) the calendar. On a wide screen a **map** of the day's routes sits next to it.

## The Day Panel

The panel is headed by the date, and lists:

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Arrivals** | One chip for each repair order whose arrival date falls on this day, with its repair order number and a label such as **Vehicle in**. |
| **Deliveries** | One chip for each repair order delivered this day, labeled **Vehicle out**. |
| **The appointments** | Every appointment on this day, in time order, each a row you can edit, complete, cancel, or drag. |
| **Tow plan** | Truck routes for the day. See [Tow planning](/schedule/tow-planning). |

With nothing scheduled it says **Nothing scheduled**: *No tows or appointments on this day.*

### Duplicates

If the same repair order, type, transport, and address has been booked more than once on a day, a button appears: **Remove 2 duplicates**. Click it and Kinetic keeps one and removes the extras. If there is nothing to remove it says **No duplicates found.**

## Appointment Types

When you choose **New appointment**, the **Type** list is grouped by who moves the car.

| Group | Type | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Concierge** (the shop's truck moves it) | **Concierge in** | A truck collects the car from the customer and brings it to the shop. |
| | **Concierge out** | A truck delivers the finished car to the customer. |
| | **Rental in** | A truck collects a rental car from the customer. |
| | **Rental out** | A truck delivers a rental car to the customer. |
| **Sublets** (outside vendors) | **Sublet out** | A truck takes the car to a vendor (glass, alignment, and so on). |
| | **Sublet return** | A truck brings it back. |
| **In-Person** (the customer moves it) | **Experience in** | The customer drives the car in. |
| | **Experience out** | The customer picks the car up themselves. |

Older appointments may also show **Suggested** (an estimate visit), **Mechanical out** and **Mechanical return**, or **Coatings out** and **Coatings return**. They appear under **Current type** when you edit one.

## Schedule an Appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose New appointment">
    The window is titled **New appointment** and says **Schedule tows, estimates, and vendor trips.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repair order">
    Search **Search repair order number or customer…** and pick the job. Kinetic fills in the customer's address for you. If you forget, it says **Pick a repair order.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type">
    Choose from the grouped list above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="When">
    Pick the **date** and a **time** in half-hour steps. If you opened the form from a day, that day is selected; **Selected date** shows it with a prompt to choose a different one if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transport">
    **In person** or **Tow truck**. Types that need a truck set it to **Tow truck** for you and lock it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place">
    One field, whose label changes with the type: **Pickup address**, **Drop-off address**, **Address**, or **Vendor or address**. Start typing a business name or street address and choose from the suggestions. A hint such as **≈ 1:20 · 12 mi from the depot** tells you the drive from the shop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gate code and garage code">
    Shown only for truck moves. The driver sees them on the calendar feed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notes">
    Anything the driver or the next person should know.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    Choose **Schedule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Scheduling Rules

- **Weekdays only.** **Appointments can't be scheduled on weekends.**
- **Between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM.** The time picker only offers those hours.
- **No missing times.** On the day clocks spring forward, 2:30 AM does not exist. Kinetic says **That time doesn't exist on the target day. The clock skips it for daylight saving.**

## Change an Appointment

- **Edit.** Click the appointment's row, or its **Edit appointment** button, and change anything.
- **Mark complete.** On a scheduled row. The row then shows a **Completed** badge.
- **Cancel appointment.** On a scheduled row. The row then shows **Cancelled**.
- **Move by dragging (mouse).** Drag a chip from one day to another to change the date; the time of day is kept. In the day panel, drag one appointment onto another to swap their times. A grip on the row (**Drag to rearrange this appointment**) shows it can be dragged. On a touch screen, use **Edit appointment** instead.

A row with no vehicle recorded reads **Vehicle not identified**. Click the repair order's number to open its [drawer](/repair-orders/drawer).

## The Calendar Feed for Drivers

Your drivers do not need Kinetic. In the day panel choose **Copy calendar link**. Kinetic creates a shared **Tow calendar** link. Anyone with it can subscribe from their phone's calendar app and see every tow as an event that updates by itself.

**On an iPhone:** Settings → Apps → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Add Other Account → Add Subscribed Calendar, then paste the link.

Each tow appears as an event with a title like **P/U in Denver, NC 28037 | 2025 Porsche 911 Blue** (**P/U** is a pickup) or **D/O in …** (**D/O** is a delivery or drop-off), and in its details the address, **gate and garage codes**, the customer's phone number, and the planned arrival time once the day is routed.

<Warning>
**Treat the link like a key.** Anyone who has it can see addresses and gate codes. Share it only with your drivers, and if it ever gets out, contact Support so it can be replaced.
</Warning>

## Do and Don't

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Book the appointment the moment the customer agrees. Put gate codes in the form, not in a text. Click **Reoptimize** after you add or move a tow. Record what actually happened by choosing **Mark complete**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Don't schedule on a weekend or after 7 PM. Don't create a second appointment to "move" one; edit the first. Don't leave a no-show scheduled; cancel it.
  </Card>
</Columns>
